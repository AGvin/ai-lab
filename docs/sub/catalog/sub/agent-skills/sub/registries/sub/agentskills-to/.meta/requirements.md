# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present AgentSkills.to as an independent Agent Skills marketplace/directory oriented toward discovering skills usable across multiple coding/agent clients.
- Keep current client-support, categories, installation instructions, ranking, publishing/submission, and marketplace behavior tied to current first-party evidence.
- Distinguish marketplace discovery from the canonical identity and authority of each indexed skill or source repository.
- Do not present listing or popularity as proof of safety, quality, or compatibility.

## Original marketplace freshness and rights boundary — 2026-11-09

- [First-party home](https://www.agentskills.to/) advertises `4K+` skills and four primary supported platforms (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Cursor and Gemini CLI), while its displayed category counters sum to many more entries. Category aggregation, overlapping labels, stale cache snapshots and duplicate installs could explain differences, but are not independently reconciled; **do not promote one mutable figure to a canonical catalog total**.
- [First-party About](https://www.agentskills.to/about) describes a community-contributed marketplace of prompts, scripts and workflows; its "production-ready" language is an operator claim, not verified third-party code or platform compatibility.
- [First-party Privacy Policy](https://www.agentskills.to/privacy), last updated 2026-06-19, says a submitter's public profile and submitted skills may be visible to all users, the platform uses Google Analytics and submitted account/GitHub identifiers may be collected. Do not submit private data, passwords, internal workflows, credentials or proprietary material as a public skill without explicit approval.
- The site's [Terms link](https://www.agentskills.to/terms) exists in first-party navigation but was not machine-retrievable during this review. **Rights/redistribution clearance is unresolved**: the privacy policy is not a license grant, marketplace publication is not ownership proof, and individual skill source licenses and any upload terms require separate verification. A recorded rights boundary here is not a positive legal sign-off.
- First-party pages were read only; no account, installer, script, skill submission, terms acceptance or plugin execution was performed. Coverage of the mutable first-party marketplace surface is bounded, not a full live skill inventory or tested security posture.
