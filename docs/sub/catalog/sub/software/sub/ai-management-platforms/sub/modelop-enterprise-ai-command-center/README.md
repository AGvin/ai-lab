# ModelOp Enterprise AI Command Center

ModelOp Enterprise AI Command Center is enterprise AI management and governance software installed in the customer's environment. It supports on-premises, private-cloud and hybrid deployments; ModelOp describes it as a product that is not public SaaS.

## Scope and boundaries

The platform combines an AI inventory and system of record, lifecycle workflows, governance and policy controls, and operational intelligence. These capabilities belong to one organization-wide management product covering internally developed and third-party AI assets.

Deployment choices, integrations, supported asset types and regulatory mappings depend on current product capabilities. Check the applicable first-party documentation and deployment terms when evaluating the platform.
