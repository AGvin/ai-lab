# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present AI Management Platforms as the software category for installable or self-managed control-plane products whose primary identity is organization-wide AI access, governance, policy, cost, identity, provider/model administration, and related operational management across multiple AI capabilities.
- Do not classify a product here merely because it has an admin UI or a gateway; the management/control-plane identity must be broader than a single gateway, observability, workflow, or model-serving role.
- List every materialized direct child exactly once and keep product-specific facts with child profiles.

## Validation

- Navigation matches the materialized direct children.
- Gateway-only and observability-only products remain with their primary owners.
