# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Arize Phoenix as a self-hostable AI observability and evaluation platform released by Arize AI under the Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2).
- Preserve its primary placement under `evaluation-and-observability/observability-platforms`; evaluation is a major integrated capability rather than a reason to duplicate the product under evaluation frameworks.
- Preserve Arize AI, Inc. as the canonical producer through the `produced-by` relation.

- Keep integrations, package decomposition, deployment modes, model/provider support, and other mutable details source-backed when expanded.
- Include current official Phoenix documentation, repository, and license references.

## Validation

- The page reflects both observability and evaluation capabilities while retaining one canonical product profile.
- Licensing is stated precisely as ELv2 rather than relying on an unqualified OSI-open-source classification.
- Local/self-managed Phoenix is not conflated with hosted operation or Arize AX.
- The producer link resolves to the canonical Arize AI, Inc. profile.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
- The page contains no temporary-placeholder wording.
