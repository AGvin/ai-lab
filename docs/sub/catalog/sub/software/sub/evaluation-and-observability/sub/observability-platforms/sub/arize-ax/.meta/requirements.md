# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Arize AX as Arize's AI engineering platform for production observability, evaluation, experimentation, and continual agent/application improvement.
- Distinguish Arize AX from Arize Phoenix: Phoenix is a separate open-source/local-first product, while AX is the managed enterprise platform with SaaS and current Enterprise self-hosted deployment options.
- Cover traces, online/offline evaluations, experiments, annotations, agent trajectory analysis, prompts, and current improvement workflows only when source-backed.
- Treat SaaS and self-hosted Enterprise as delivery modes of the same AX product identity.
- Keep plan tiers, deployment modes, feature availability, and current product positioning freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- AX and Phoenix are not collapsed into one canonical product.
- Self-hosted availability is not generalized to plans that do not provide it.
