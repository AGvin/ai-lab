# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify the Fiddler AI Control Plane as Fiddler's current platform identity for evaluation, monitoring/observability, runtime policy enforcement, guardrails, and governance across AI agents and models.
- Preserve product lineage without creating a duplicate legacy node: current 2026 sources position Fiddler as an AI Control Plane, while earlier official material used AI Observability and Security Platform / model-observability wording for the same evolving platform.
- Keep the software ownership classification source-backed: Fiddler supports SaaS, customer VPC/private-cloud, and on-premises or air-gapped deployment options, so hosted access is not the sole product identity.
- Distinguish the control-plane product from individual Fiddler Centor Models, guardrail models, evaluators, telemetry integrations, SDKs, and the acquired Lumeus technology unless any later passes the independent-identity intake gates.
- Keep exact latency, evaluator accuracy, cost/TCO, metric counts, integrations, deployment packaging, compliance certifications, and analyst/market claims freshness-sensitive and explicitly sourced when expanded.
- Preserve the current product boundary across traditional/predictive ML, generative AI, agents, and coding-agent governance without treating every supported workload as a separate product.
- Render producer relation and official resources only during the final render phase.

## Validation

- Current Control Plane positioning and earlier Fiddler observability/security naming are not represented as two separate products.
- Fiddler is not duplicated as both a service and software solely because SaaS and self-managed deployment modes coexist.
- Mutable performance, security, deployment, and market claims retain a current-source boundary.
