# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify OMP, also known as Oh My Pi, as a terminal-first AI coding agent derived from Pi.
- Preserve its coding-oriented expansion around integrated editing/developer tools, language tooling, browser capabilities, subagents, sessions, and extensions at a high level.
- Represent the Pi lineage through the canonical `derived-from` relation and a reader-facing related link.
- Keep provider/tool counts, benchmark claims, implementation-size claims, and other mutable metrics out of the concise profile.
- Include the official OMP site and repository.

## Validation

- OMP is represented as a materially distinct Pi derivative, not as a Pi version or alias.
- The Pi relation resolves to the canonical sibling software node.
