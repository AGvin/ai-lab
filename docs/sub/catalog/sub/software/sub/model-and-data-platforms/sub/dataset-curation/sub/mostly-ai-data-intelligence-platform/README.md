# MOSTLY AI Data Intelligence Platform

The MOSTLY AI Data Intelligence Platform combines production-data analysis with synthetic, mock, and simulated data generation. Its official materials describe enterprise deployment on customer compute with Kubernetes or OpenShift.

The Synthetic Data SDK is separately installable Apache-2.0 software. Its April 2026 transition removed platform-only surfaces, so historical client-mode integration does not establish current platform API parity. The SDK's license does not cover every Data Intelligence Platform component.

## Provenance

- Produced by: [MOSTLY AI Solutions MP GmbH](../../../../../../../producers/sub/m/sub/mostly-ai-solutions-mp-gmbh/) — historical company producer. The 2025 product accounts predate the brand acquisition; the public company handbook page dated July 11, 2025 records the legal name.

[Syntho](../../../../../../../producers/sub/s/sub/syntho/) announced acquisition of the MOSTLY AI brand on June 9, 2026 and the branding “MOSTLY AI, powered by Syntho.” That brand announcement does not establish software authorship or ownership.
