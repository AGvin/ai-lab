# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present AMD ROCm.AI as AMD's generally available AI-native development platform/experience for building, running, diagnosing, deploying, and optimizing AI workloads on AMD hardware.
- Preserve its current product boundary as the integrated experience around AMD Skills, ROCm CLI, Hyperloom, ROCm Core SDK access, and agent/coding-assistant workflows; do not collapse the broader ROCm runtime/software stack into ROCm.AI.
- Keep AMD Skills as a contained skill surface unless separately materialized as a durable first-party skill collection; keep Hyperloom and ROCm CLI as components unless upstream lifecycle establishes independently durable peer products.
- Treat supported assistants, hardware, commands, optimization workflows, ROCm-version coupling, package availability, component boundaries, and performance claims as freshness-sensitive.
- Attribute AMD performance-improvement claims to AMD and preserve tested hardware/software/workload conditions.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- General availability remains traceable to the ROCm 10 launch.
- ROCm.AI remains distinct from ROCm itself and from concrete model-serving runtimes.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.
