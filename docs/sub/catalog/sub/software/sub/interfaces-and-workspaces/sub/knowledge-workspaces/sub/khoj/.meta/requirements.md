# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Khoj as an open-source, self-hostable personal AI and second-brain knowledge workspace centered on a durable personal corpus of notes, documents, and other user knowledge.
- Preserve its primary placement under `interfaces-and-workspaces/knowledge-workspaces`: personal knowledge ingestion, semantic search, source-grounded chat, and working with the user's persistent knowledge corpus are central to the current product identity.
- Treat conversational access, agents/automation, public-internet research, and multi-client access as substantial capabilities without letting them override the primary knowledge-workspace boundary.
- State that the hosted Khoj Cloud service was shut down on 2026-04-15; preserve the continuing open-source, self-hostable software boundary and do not present Khoj Cloud as currently available.
- Preserve Khoj Inc. as the organization behind the product without collapsing organization, software, and the historical hosted-service identity.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep supported document formats, model providers, client integrations, automation/agent features, and other mutable software details source-backed when expanded.
- Include current official Khoj documentation, repository, legal, and Cloud-sunset references.
