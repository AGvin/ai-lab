# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Big-AGI as a local-first, multi-model AI workspace whose open-source foundation supports advanced conversations, personas, multi-model workflows, files, media, and other expert-oriented AI interaction features.
- Preserve its primary placement under `interfaces-and-workspaces/integrated-workspaces`; multi-model reasoning and agentic capabilities remain workspace features rather than a reason to classify Big-AGI as an agent framework.
- Distinguish self-hosted Big-AGI Open from hosted Free/Pro surfaces operated through `big-agi.com` while retaining their shared product family.
- Preserve Enrico Ros as origin producer and Token Fabrics as current hosted-product operator; do not collapse production, operation, or ownership semantics.
- Do not add an unsupported `LLC` suffix to Token Fabrics or infer ownership solely from branding/copyright presentation.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep model/provider support, subscription features, release-specific tools, sync/storage behavior, and other mutable product details source-backed when expanded.
- Include current official Big-AGI site and repository references.

## Validation

- The page preserves one canonical product family while distinguishing open/self-hosted and hosted subscription boundaries.
- Hosted features are not implied to exist in the open-source self-hosted tier unless current sources support them.
- Enrico Ros origin provenance and Token Fabrics operation remain distinct, with no unsupported ownership claim.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
- The page contains no temporary-placeholder wording.
