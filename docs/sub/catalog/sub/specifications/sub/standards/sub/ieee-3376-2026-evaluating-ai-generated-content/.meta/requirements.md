# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 3376-2026 as the active IEEE recommended practice for evaluating artificial-intelligence-generated content.
- Preserve its cross-cutting evaluation boundary around generative-AI capabilities, evaluation content and metrics without converting the standard into a specific benchmark dataset, content detector, model or application.
- Keep concrete AIGC benchmarks, provenance/watermarking standards, moderation/safety systems and generated-content products with their own canonical owners.
- Treat final publication metadata, detailed metric definitions, amendments/corrigenda, successor standards and lifecycle status as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Active-standard status and Board approval remain traceable to IEEE SA.
- The entity remains an evaluation standard/recommended-practice artifact rather than a benchmark or software product.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-08

- **IEEE 3376-2026** is marked **Active Standard** on its official IEEE SA page with Standards Board approval **2026-09-25**. IEEE titles the approved artifact an **Approved Draft Recommended Practice** for evaluating AI-generated content; preserve that wording rather than inferring a separate publication date.
- The publisher's public description covers generative-AI capabilities, evaluation content and metrics. It is not a particular benchmark corpus, watermark, detector, model, measured safety score or compliance certification.
- Verified original publisher source: https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/3376/11303/
- The source gate establishes only the existing IEEE record, lifecycle and public overview; full licensed text, experimental AIGC evaluation, independent safety/legal/security analysis, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean and final gates remain unverified.
