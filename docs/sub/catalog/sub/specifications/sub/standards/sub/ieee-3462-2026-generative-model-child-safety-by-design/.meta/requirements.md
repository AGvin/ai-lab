# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 3462-2026 as the active IEEE recommended practice applying Safety by Design across the lifecycle of generative AI models to prioritize child safety.
- Preserve its cross-modal lifecycle scope across development, deployment and maintenance and across text, image, video and audio model releases.
- Keep concrete safety models, moderation APIs, child-safety services, risk frameworks and legal/compliance obligations with their own canonical owners.
- Do not present the standard as sufficient proof of legal compliance, production safety or suitability for minors; implementation and applicable-law assessment remain context-specific.
- Treat detailed safeguards, amendments/corrigenda, successor standards and lifecycle status as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Active-standard status and Board approval remain traceable to IEEE SA.
- The entity remains a safety recommended-practice standard rather than a product or policy claim.
