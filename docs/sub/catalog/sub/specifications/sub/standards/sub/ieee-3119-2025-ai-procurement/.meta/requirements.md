# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 3119-2025 as the published IEEE standard for responsible procurement of AI and automated decision systems.
- Preserve its procurement-process scope, including problem definition, solicitation preparation, vendor evaluation, solution evaluation, contract negotiation, and contract monitoring.
- Keep procurement guidance distinct from technical model evaluation, organization-wide AI governance, legal compliance, and vendor-specific sourcing processes.

## Validation

- The entity remains a formal procurement standard rather than a training product or procurement service.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-08

- **IEEE 3119-2025** is an **active IEEE Standard**, published **2025-05-23** following Standards Board approval on **2025-03-27**. The original standard record defines AI/automated-decision-system procurement risk management, from problem definition and solicitation through vendor/solution evaluation, negotiation and contract monitoring.
- Its procurement lifecycle and risk-control guidance is not a technology qualification test, vendor certification, legal procurement obligation, organizational AI governance standard or guarantee of an individual contract's compliance.
- Verified original standard publisher record: https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/3119/10729/ (direct record, in addition to the existing IEEE publisher explanatory/approval links).
- Acceptance covers the existing standard identity and publicly available status, date and summary only; paid full normative text, vendor/procurement execution, independent legal/security/conformance, root-wide validation, clean and final gates were not reviewed or credited.
