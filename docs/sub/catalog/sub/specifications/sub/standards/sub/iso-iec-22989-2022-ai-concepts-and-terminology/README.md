# ISO/IEC 22989:2022

ISO/IEC 22989:2022 is the published International Standard establishing artificial-intelligence terminology and describing AI concepts.

## Standard boundary

The formal terminology artifact is distinct from AI Lab's own explanatory concept taxonomy and glossary. Local concepts may reference the standard without becoming duplicate owners of the standard itself.

Corrections, revisions, national adoptions, and downstream mappings are freshness-sensitive. This page identifies the standard without reproducing its normative terminology set.
