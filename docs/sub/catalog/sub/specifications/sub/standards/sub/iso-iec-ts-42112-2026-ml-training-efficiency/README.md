# ISO/IEC TS 42112:2026

ISO/IEC TS 42112:2026 is the published Technical Specification providing guidance on machine-learning model training-efficiency optimization.

## Specification boundary

The artifact covers factors and optimization approaches for training efficiency. As a Technical Specification, it is not a mandatory certification or hardware-acceleration standard, and its efficiency guidance remains distinct from particular training frameworks, accelerators, and vendor products.
