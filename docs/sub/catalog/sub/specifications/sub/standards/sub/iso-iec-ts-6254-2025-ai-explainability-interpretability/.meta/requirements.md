# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TS 6254:2025 as the published Technical Specification describing explainability and interpretability objectives, approaches, and method applicability across the AI-system life cycle.
- Preserve its guidance/Technical Specification identity rather than presenting it as a certification requirement, a specific XAI library, or a legal transparency mandate.
- Keep it distinct from ISO/IEC 12792 transparency taxonomy and IEEE 2894 architectural guidance while noting their adjacent decision-support roles.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published TS lifecycle and exact edition/year.
