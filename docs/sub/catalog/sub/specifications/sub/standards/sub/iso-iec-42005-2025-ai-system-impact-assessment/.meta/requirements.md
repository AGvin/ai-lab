# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 42005:2025 as the published international standard for AI-system impact-assessment guidance and preserve the exact edition/year identity.
- Keep the impact-assessment artifact distinct from general AI risk management, AI management-system requirements, regulatory impact-assessment mandates, and organization-specific templates.
- Describe lifecycle impact-assessment purpose only at the scope supported by current ISO metadata; do not invent mandatory assessment steps from secondary summaries.
- Treat revisions, mappings, implementation guidance, and regulatory references as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- The entity is classified by its upstream International Standard identity rather than by the word `guidance` in its scope.
- AI Lab content does not substitute for the normative standard text.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 42005:2025 is the published **Edition 1 (May 2025) International Standard** offering guidance for impact assessments of AI systems, especially potential impacts on people, groups and society across the system life cycle. The public ISO overview emphasizes documenting effects and supporting transparency and accountability.
- AI-system impact assessment is distinct from ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS requirements, ISO/IEC 23894 general AI risk guidance and any jurisdiction's legally mandated impact study. Public summaries do not specify universally mandatory process steps, certification or regulator approval.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://www.iso.org/standard/42005
- Official identity, edition and abstract-only source qualification; no full licensed normative text or organization-specific assessment reviewed, and no full legal/privacy/security certification, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
