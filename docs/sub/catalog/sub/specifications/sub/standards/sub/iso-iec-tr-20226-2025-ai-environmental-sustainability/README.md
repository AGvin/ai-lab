# ISO/IEC TR 20226:2025

ISO/IEC TR 20226:2025 is the published Technical Report on environmental-sustainability aspects of AI systems across their life cycle.

## Report boundary

The report addresses resource utilization, carbon impact, pollution, waste, transportation, location, and potential metrics for AI systems. Its scope is environmental sustainability **of AI systems**, not the use of AI to improve sustainability outcomes in other domains.

As a Technical Report, it is distinct from mandatory management-system, risk, or product-certification requirements.
