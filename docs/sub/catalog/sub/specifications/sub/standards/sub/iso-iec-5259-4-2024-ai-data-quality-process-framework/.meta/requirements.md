# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Part 4 as the published ISO/IEC 5259 data-quality process framework for analytics and ML.
- Keep operational process guidance distinct from Part 3 management requirements and Part 5 governance.

## Validation

- The entity remains Part 4 of the formal published series.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 5259-4:2024 is the published **Edition 1 (July 2024)** International Standard for the **data quality process framework** covering analytics and machine learning. ISO's public overview describes operational data quality practices, including data labeling/evaluation and applications across supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised and reinforcement learning.
- This operational process framework differs from Part 3's organizational data quality management requirements and Part 5's higher-level governance role; publication is not evidence that any dataset was labeled correctly, that a particular data pipeline meets the clauses, or that an ML system performs safely.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/81093.html
- Public ISO identity/edition/abstract only accepted; no paid full normative text, in-house process implementation, dataset quality audit, legally binding conformance, whole-repository schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
