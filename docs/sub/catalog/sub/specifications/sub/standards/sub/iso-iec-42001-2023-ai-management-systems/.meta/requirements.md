# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 42001:2023 as the published international AI management-system standard and preserve the exact edition/year identity.
- Summarize its role as a requirements-oriented management-system standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving organizational AI management.
- Keep certification services, implementation products, consulting guidance, national adoptions, audit evidence, and organization-specific controls outside the standard identity.
- Treat amendments, corrigenda, revision status, certification ecosystem claims, and conformance interpretations as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- The entity remains a released standard rather than a generic AI-governance framework.
- Requirements/conformance claims are not generalized beyond the published edition or paraphrased as substitute normative text.
