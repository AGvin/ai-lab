# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 42001:2023 as the published international AI management-system standard and preserve the exact edition/year identity.
- Summarize its role as a requirements-oriented management-system standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving organizational AI management.
- Keep certification services, implementation products, consulting guidance, national adoptions, audit evidence, and organization-specific controls outside the standard identity.
- Treat amendments, corrigenda, revision status, certification ecosystem claims, and conformance interpretations as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- The entity remains a released standard rather than a generic AI-governance framework.
- Requirements/conformance claims are not generalized beyond the published edition or paraphrased as substitute normative text.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the published **Edition 1 (December 2023) International Standard** for an organizational AI management system (AIMS), specifying requirements for its establishment, maintenance and continual improvement. ISO's public description addresses both providers and users of AI systems and distinguishes management governance from implementation of any particular model.
- The standard's status is not a claim that any organization holds accredited certification, or that adoption guarantees AI safety or statutory compliance. Certification processes, national adoptions, amendments and full conformance controls have separate evidence and rights boundaries.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://www.iso.org/standard/42001
- Existing released-standard identity and public ISO abstract scope accepted **metadata-only**. Full copyrighted normative text, certification criteria and actual organizational conformance were not independently accessed, reproduced or audited. No schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
