# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 24029-1:2021 as the published overview of methods for assessing neural-network robustness.
- Preserve the Part 1 overview boundary and keep methodology-specific formal/statistical assessment parts as separate standard artifacts.
- Do not conflate neural-network robustness assessment with general AI trustworthiness, model safety policy, adversarial-security tooling, or concrete robustness benchmarks.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains Part 1 of the ISO/IEC 24029 robustness series.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC **TR** 24029-1:2021 is the published **Edition 1 (March 2021) Technical Report** surveying approaches to assessing neural-network robustness. It is the overview identity, distinct from ISO/IEC 24029-2:2023's separately released methodology for applying formal methods.
- A list or overview of robustness methods does not prove any model is robust, nor does it establish that every verifier, neural architecture or statistical validation method is universally applicable. Later series parts need their own status and source checks rather than implied publication.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/77609.html
- Publisher-issued edition, abstract and status accepted as a source gate only. No robustness experiment, copyrighted normative text reproduction, full compliance/security audit, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
