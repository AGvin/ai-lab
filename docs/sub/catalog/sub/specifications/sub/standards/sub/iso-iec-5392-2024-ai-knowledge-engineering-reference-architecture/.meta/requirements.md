# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 5392:2024 as the published international standard defining a reference architecture, roles, activities, layers, components, relationships, and vocabulary for knowledge engineering in AI.
- Keep the standard distinct from concrete knowledge-graph products, RAG architectures, ontologies, vector databases, and AI Lab explanatory concepts.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a formal knowledge-engineering architecture standard rather than a software implementation.
