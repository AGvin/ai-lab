# ISO/IEC 23894:2023

ISO/IEC 23894:2023 is the published International Standard providing AI-specific risk-management guidance.

## Standard boundary

Its organizational risk-management role is distinct from ISO/IEC 42001 management-system requirements, AI impact-assessment standards, regulatory law, and non-standard guidance frameworks. The guidance-oriented standard is not a certification requirement or mandatory control catalog.

Amendments, revisions, mappings, national adoptions, and implementation interpretations remain freshness-sensitive.
