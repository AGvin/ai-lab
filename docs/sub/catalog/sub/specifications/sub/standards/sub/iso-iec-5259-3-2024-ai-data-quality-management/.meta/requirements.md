## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 5259-3:2024 is the published **Edition 1 (July 2024)** International Standard for **data quality management requirements and guidelines** in analytics and ML. Its ISO public overview discusses managing data quality across the data life cycle and a flexible data quality management approach, not an automatically certified organization.
- Keep Part 3 management requirements and guidance distinct from Part 2 measures, Part 4 operational processes and Part 5 governing-body oversight. Published requirements do not mean an organization has implemented them or obtained an independent audit or legal certification.
