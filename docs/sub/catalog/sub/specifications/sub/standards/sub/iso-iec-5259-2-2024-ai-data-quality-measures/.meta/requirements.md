# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Part 2 as the published ISO/IEC 5259 data-quality model and measures standard for analytics and ML.
- Keep formal measures distinct from implementation-specific monitoring metrics and dataset scorecards.

## Validation

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 5259-2:2024 is the published **Edition 1 (November 2024)** International Standard for **data quality measures** in analytics and machine learning. Its public ISO overview describes a data quality model and measurable characteristics, distinct from Part 1 terminology, Part 3 management requirements, Part 4 processes and Part 5 organizational governance.
- The published model is not equivalent to a measured dataset, an independent benchmark, or proof that a trained model is accurate, unbiased or fit for purpose. Exact indicators, formal definitions, compliance mappings, and assessment claims require separately licensed normative sources and actual evaluation evidence.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/81860.html
- Source acceptance is restricted to publisher identity, published edition/status and public abstract. The copyrighted standard's complete normative clauses, dataset measurement, organization-level conformance, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass and final gates were not assessed.
