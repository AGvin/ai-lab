# ISO/IEC TS 6254:2025

ISO/IEC TS 6254:2025 is the published Technical Specification describing objectives and approaches for explainability and interpretability of ML models and AI systems.

## Specification boundary

The guidance spans method applicability across the AI-system life cycle. It is not a certification requirement, specific XAI library, or legal transparency mandate. It remains distinct from ISO/IEC 12792's transparency taxonomy and IEEE 2894's architectural guidance.
