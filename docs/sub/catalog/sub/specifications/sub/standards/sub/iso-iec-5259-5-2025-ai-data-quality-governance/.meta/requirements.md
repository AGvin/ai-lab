# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Part 5 as the published ISO/IEC 5259 governance framework for organizational oversight of data quality in analytics and ML.
- Keep governance-level responsibilities distinct from Part 3 management and Part 4 operational process guidance.

## Validation

- The entity remains Part 5 of the formal published series.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 5259-5:2025 is the published **Edition 1 (February 2025)** International Standard for the **governance framework** of analytics/ML data quality. The public ISO summary addresses governing bodies and executives directing policy, accountability and oversight, distinct from Part 3 management and Part 4 operational processes.
- ISO's official item title and General Information confirm **2025**, although the bundle-promotion copy on the same page mistakenly calls the item `5259-5:2024`. Use the item record's 2025 identifier and publication date as authoritative; do not create a duplicate 2024 identity or infer certification of any organization.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/84150.html
- Source acceptance is limited to the publisher's published edition and abstract. No full copyrighted normative text, organization-specific governance review, compliance assessment, full schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
