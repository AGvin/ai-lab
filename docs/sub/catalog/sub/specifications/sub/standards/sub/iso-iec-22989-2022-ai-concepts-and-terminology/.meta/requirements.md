# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 22989:2022 as the published international standard establishing AI terminology and describing AI concepts.
- Keep the normative terminology artifact distinct from AI Lab's own concepts taxonomy and glossary; local explanatory concepts may reference the standard without becoming duplicate standard owners.
- Preserve edition/revision status and avoid presenting selected terms as an exhaustive reproduction of the standard.
- Treat corrections, revisions, national adoptions, and downstream mappings as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- The standard remains the canonical catalog identity for the formal artifact, while reusable AI subject explanations remain under `concepts/`.
- AI Lab does not reproduce protected normative text beyond what is necessary for identification/scope.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 22989:2022 is the published **Edition 1 (July 2022) International Standard** establishing terminology and describing concepts for AI across stakeholders and organizations. It is the formal document identity, not a copy of the AI Lab domain glossary or a complete, freely redistributable vocabulary.
- ISO identifies a corrected French-language version dated December 2025 and amendments under development. Neither changes the current English 2022 edition's identity nor supplies authority to assume a final revised terminology publication. Exact definitions and downstream mappings depend on the licensed text.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://www.iso.org/standard/74296.html
- Scoped original-source acceptance only for official edition, abstract and status; no extraction or reproduction of copyrighted normative glossary, full correction analysis, organizational application audit, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
