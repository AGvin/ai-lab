# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 22989:2022 as the published international standard establishing AI terminology and describing AI concepts.
- Keep the normative terminology artifact distinct from AI Lab's own concepts taxonomy and glossary; local explanatory concepts may reference the standard without becoming duplicate standard owners.
- Preserve edition/revision status and avoid presenting selected terms as an exhaustive reproduction of the standard.
- Treat corrections, revisions, national adoptions, and downstream mappings as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- The standard remains the canonical catalog identity for the formal artifact, while reusable AI subject explanations remain under `concepts/`.
- AI Lab does not reproduce protected normative text beyond what is necessary for identification/scope.
