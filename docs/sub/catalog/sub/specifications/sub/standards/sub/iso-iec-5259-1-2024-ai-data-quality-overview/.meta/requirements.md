# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Part 1 as the published overview, terminology, and examples foundation for the ISO/IEC 5259 AI/ML data-quality series.
- Keep series terminology distinct from local glossary/concept ownership and preserve exact part/year identity.

## Validation

- The entity remains Part 1 of the formal published series.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 5259-1:2024 is the **published Edition 1 (July 2024)** part of the ISO/IEC 5259 series for analytics/ML data quality. Part 1 supplies the **overview, terminology and examples**, rather than Part 2 measures, Part 3 management requirements, Part 4 process workflows or Part 5 governance.
- Official ISO series packaging lists five distinct released parts, and none of their existence guarantees that a dataset or deployed model has been assessed or certified. Detailed normative definitions and any organization-specific quality criteria belong to licensed/source-controlled work.
- Verified original publisher source 1: https://www.iso.org/standard/81088.html
- Verified original publisher source 2: https://www.iso.org/publication/PUB200525.html
- Source qualification covers public release metadata and high-level scope, not the full copyrighted text, audit, dataset measurement, actual QA implementation, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass, or final gate.
