# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TS 42112:2026 as the published Technical Specification providing guidance on factors and optimization approaches for ML model training efficiency.
- Preserve its Technical Specification identity and do not overstate it as a mandatory certification or hardware-acceleration standard.
- Keep efficiency concepts separate from particular training frameworks, accelerators, or vendor products.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published TS lifecycle and exact edition/year.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC **TS** 42112:2026 is the **published first-edition Technical Specification (June 2026)** on factors that affect ML-model training efficiency and possible optimization approaches, intended to inform comparison of training strategies.
- The public ISO description explicitly excludes specifying device-implemented training acceleration mechanisms of ISO/IEC TR 17903. It is distinct from a measured training-system benchmark, processor requirement, universal accelerator compatibility claim or compulsory certification.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/88246.html
- Acceptance is limited to existing identity, published Technical Specification edition/status and public description; no licensed full text, implementation measurements, vendor hardware validation, full legal/security/freshness audit, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean or final delivery credit.
