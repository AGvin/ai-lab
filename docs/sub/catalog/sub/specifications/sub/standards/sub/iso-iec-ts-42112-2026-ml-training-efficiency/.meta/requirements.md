# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TS 42112:2026 as the published Technical Specification providing guidance on factors and optimization approaches for ML model training efficiency.
- Preserve its Technical Specification identity and do not overstate it as a mandatory certification or hardware-acceleration standard.
- Keep efficiency concepts separate from particular training frameworks, accelerators, or vendor products.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published TS lifecycle and exact edition/year.
