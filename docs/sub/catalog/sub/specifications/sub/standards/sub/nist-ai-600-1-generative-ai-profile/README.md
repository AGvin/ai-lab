# NIST AI 600-1 — Generative AI Profile

**NIST AI 600-1**, published **July 26, 2024**, is a separate, citable [Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence) for the [NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0](../nist-ai-rmf-1-0/). The publisher is [NIST](../../../../../producers/sub/n/sub/nist/), a U.S. federal standards institute.

## Profile identity and application

This voluntary, cross-sectoral profile focuses risk-management practices on generative AI systems: possible harms and failure modes, organizational governance, deployment, evaluation and actions to manage risk according to an organization's context. It **does not** replace the broader 2023 NIST AI RMF, create a new RMF version, constitute a binding certification standard or guarantee that an LLM, provider or agent is secure.

The [official NIST citation](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) identifies the original **2024** publication. Its original publication describes a then-applicable U.S. executive-order context; citing that original history does not establish that the same order remains in force in **2026**. NIST's current AI RMF site separately notes that the **base RMF 1.0 is under revision**. Any future revised profile, mandates or crosswalks must be checked independently rather than silently projected onto this version.

## Operational and provenance boundaries

Use the profile as risk-analysis guidance, not as a substitute for the deployed system's actual threat model, security/privacy controls, model provenance, license review, testing, evaluation or current legal requirements. NIST did not produce or certify the AI models or commercial products a reader might evaluate with its guidance.
