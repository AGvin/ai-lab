# ISO/IEC 42006:2025

ISO/IEC 42006:2025 is the published standard defining additional requirements for bodies that audit and certify AI management systems against ISO/IEC 42001.

## Standard boundary

The standard governs conformity-assessment bodies rather than organization-level AIMS requirements or a specific certification service. It remains distinct from ISO/IEC 42001 itself and from product or service certification schemes.
