# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 7015-2026 as the active IEEE standard establishing an operational framework for data and AI literacy, skills, and readiness.
- Preserve its focus on competencies, proficiency levels, common definitions, educational/training inclusion, policy interventions, measurable indicators, and readiness rather than treating it as a technical AI-system performance benchmark.
- Keep literacy/readiness standards distinct from model evaluation, AI governance, risk management, professional certification, and AI product training material.
- Treat future revisions, amendments, corrigenda, competency-level details, and status changes as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Active-standard status and 2026-06-04 Standards Board approval remain traceable to IEEE SA.
- The entity remains a standards artifact rather than an AI literacy learning curriculum.
