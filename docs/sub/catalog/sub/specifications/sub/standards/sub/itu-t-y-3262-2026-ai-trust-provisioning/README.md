# ITU-T Y.3262 — Trust Provisioning for Networks and Services Using AI

**ITU-T Y.3262 (08/2026)** is an ITU-T Recommendation on the framework and lifecycle management of trust provisioning for networks and services using artificial-intelligence technologies. It was approved on **August 13, 2026**. The official edition record marks it **in force (prepublished)**; the publicly viewable summary and final accessible publication rights are distinct matters. [ITU-T prepublication record](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-Y.3262-202608-P/en).

## Architecture and lifecycle boundary

Its public scope concerns trust-provisioning architecture and lifecycle-management requirements in networks and services that use AI. It is not a concrete IAM product, AI security service, trust-score calculator, deployed agent system or certificate of overall AI safety, privacy, security or regulatory compliance.

## Availability and evidence

The ITU-T page lists restricted documents for TIES users while providing an official public record and summary. Preserve that access boundary: in-force approval and restricted prepublication do not imply that unrestricted full normative text is publicly available. More detailed controls and conformance require authorized access to the exact document and later publication/corrigendum checks.
