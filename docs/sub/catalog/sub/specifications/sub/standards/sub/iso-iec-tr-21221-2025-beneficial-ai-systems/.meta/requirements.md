# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 21221:2025 as the published Technical Report describing how stakeholders may perceive functional, economic, environmental, social, societal, cultural, intellectual, and personal benefits delivered by AI systems.
- Preserve its Technical Report/guidance identity and do not present it as a certification requirement, legal mandate, or quantitative benefit benchmark.
- Keep illustrative use cases and benefit concepts distinct from AI risk, impact-assessment, governance, and management-system standards.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC **TR** 21221:2025 is the published **Edition 1 (September 2025) Technical Report** describing stakeholder-perceived benefits from AI systems, with public ISO scope covering functional, economic, environmental, social, societal, cultural, intellectual and personal outcomes plus illustrative use cases.
- It is a qualitative overview of possible benefits, not a guaranteed net-benefit benchmark, independent impact assessment, numerical score, legal/ethical certification, or replacement for risk-management and AI governance standards. Specific benefits require observed evidence in the intended context.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/86690.html
- Existing published TR identity/status and public abstract only accepted as source evidence. No full copyrighted normative report, measured stakeholder impacts, independent audit, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
