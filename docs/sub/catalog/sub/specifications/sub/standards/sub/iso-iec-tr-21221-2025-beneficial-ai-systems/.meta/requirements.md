# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 21221:2025 as the published Technical Report describing how stakeholders may perceive functional, economic, environmental, social, societal, cultural, intellectual, and personal benefits delivered by AI systems.
- Preserve its Technical Report/guidance identity and do not present it as a certification requirement, legal mandate, or quantitative benefit benchmark.
- Keep illustrative use cases and benefit concepts distinct from AI risk, impact-assessment, governance, and management-system standards.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.
