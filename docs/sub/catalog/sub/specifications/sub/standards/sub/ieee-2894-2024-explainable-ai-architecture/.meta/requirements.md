# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 2894-2024 as the active IEEE guide for an architectural framework and application guidance for explainable AI.
- Preserve its focus on XAI concepts, method classes, application scenarios, architecture, and evaluation rather than presenting it as a specific explainability library or legal transparency mandate.
- Keep the standard distinct from ISO/IEC 12792 transparency taxonomy and local AI Lab explainability concepts.

## Validation

- The entity is represented as the published 2024 guide rather than a draft project.
