# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 23894:2023 as the published international standard for AI-specific risk-management guidance and preserve the exact edition/year identity.
- Keep its organizational risk-management role distinct from ISO/IEC 42001 management-system requirements, AI impact-assessment standards, regulatory law, and non-standard guidance frameworks.
- Do not present a guidance-oriented standard as if it defines certification requirements or a mandatory control catalog.
- Treat amendments, revisions, mappings, national adoptions, and implementation interpretations as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Upstream identity remains `International Standard` even though the standard itself provides guidance.
- AI Lab summaries do not reproduce or substitute for normative/copyrighted standard text.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 23894:2023 is the published **Edition 1 (February 2023) International Standard** supplying risk-management *guidance* to organizations developing, deploying, producing or using AI systems. Its public scope includes integrating AI-specific risk management into organizational work.
- This is a guidance document, not the ISO/IEC 42001 management-system requirements contract, an AI-system impact-assessment procedure, a certification program or mandatory regulation. Application to a specific enterprise and any standard revisions require separate verification.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html
- Existing standard identity, edition and public ISO abstract only qualified as **metadata/source evidence**. Full copyrighted normative text, technical requirements and conformance results were not examined or reproduced. No whole-catalog schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
