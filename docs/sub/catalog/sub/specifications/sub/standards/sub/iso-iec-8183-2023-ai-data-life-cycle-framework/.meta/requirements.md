# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 8183:2023 as the published international framework defining stages and associated data-processing actions throughout the AI-system life cycle, from acquisition/creation through development, deployment, maintenance, and decommissioning.
- Keep the formal data-life-cycle framework distinct from concrete data platforms, dataset-curation products, governance tooling, and the ISO/IEC 5259 data-quality series.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a cross-cutting formal AI data-life-cycle standard rather than an implementation architecture or product.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 8183:2023 is the published **Edition 1 (July 2023)** International Standard framing data-processing stages/actions across the AI-system life cycle, including acquisition, creation, development, deployment, maintenance and decommissioning. The public ISO abstract expressly states it does not define particular services, platforms or tools.
- This cross-cutting lifecycle framework is distinct from the ISO/IEC 5259 data-quality series, operational ETL/ELT pipelines and any concrete dataset license or governance program. A standard's publication does not prove implementation, traceability or compliance in a given organization.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/83002.html
- Bounded publisher edition, status and abstract source gate only; no complete copyrighted normative text, data-processing-system audit, legal compliance check, full repository schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
