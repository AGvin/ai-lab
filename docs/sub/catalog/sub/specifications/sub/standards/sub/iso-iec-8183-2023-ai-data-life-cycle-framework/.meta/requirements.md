# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 8183:2023 as the published international framework defining stages and associated data-processing actions throughout the AI-system life cycle, from acquisition/creation through development, deployment, maintenance, and decommissioning.
- Keep the formal data-life-cycle framework distinct from concrete data platforms, dataset-curation products, governance tooling, and the ISO/IEC 5259 data-quality series.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a cross-cutting formal AI data-life-cycle standard rather than an implementation architecture or product.
