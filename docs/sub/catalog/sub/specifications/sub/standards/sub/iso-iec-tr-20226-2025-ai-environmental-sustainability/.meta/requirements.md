# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 20226:2025 as the published Technical Report covering environmental-sustainability aspects of AI systems across their life cycle, including resource utilization, carbon impact, pollution, waste, transportation, location, and potential metrics.
- Preserve its Technical Report identity and its explicit scope: it analyzes environmental sustainability aspects of AI systems rather than how AI can improve sustainability outcomes in other domains.
- Keep environmental metrics and lifecycle concerns distinct from mandatory management-system, risk, and product-certification requirements.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC **TR** 20226:2025 is the published **Edition 1 (July 2025) Technical Report** describing environmental-sustainability aspects and possible metrics of AI systems across their lifecycle, including workloads, resources/assets, emissions, waste, transportation and location.
- The published ISO abstract explicitly excludes potential opportunities for AI applications to improve environmental, social or economic sustainability outcomes in other domains. This Technical Report is not a mandatory environmental certification, full product carbon accounting methodology or assertion that a particular AI workload has been measured.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/86177.html
- Only official Technical Report identity, publication date/status and public abstract are source-qualified. Full copyrighted normative material, operational carbon measurements, lifecycle inventory, environmental/legal assurance, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass and final gates were not reviewed.
