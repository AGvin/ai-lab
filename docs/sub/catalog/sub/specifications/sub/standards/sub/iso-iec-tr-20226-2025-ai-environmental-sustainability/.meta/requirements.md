# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 20226:2025 as the published Technical Report covering environmental-sustainability aspects of AI systems across their life cycle, including resource utilization, carbon impact, pollution, waste, transportation, location, and potential metrics.
- Preserve its Technical Report identity and its explicit scope: it analyzes environmental sustainability aspects of AI systems rather than how AI can improve sustainability outcomes in other domains.
- Keep environmental metrics and lifecycle concerns distinct from mandatory management-system, risk, and product-certification requirements.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.
