# ISO/IEC TR 21221:2025

ISO/IEC TR 21221:2025 is the published Technical Report on beneficial AI systems.

## Report boundary

The report describes how stakeholders may perceive functional, economic, environmental, social, societal, cultural, intellectual, and personal benefits delivered by AI systems. It is guidance rather than a certification requirement, legal mandate, or quantitative benefit benchmark.

Its benefit concepts and illustrative use cases remain distinct from AI risk, impact-assessment, governance, and management-system standards.
