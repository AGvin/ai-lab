# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 3398-2026 as the active IEEE recommended practice for integrating GPT-based capabilities across the software engineering life cycle.
- Preserve its cross-cutting framework boundary around intelligence levels, competency requirements and evaluation methods across requirements, design, coding, testing, delivery and operations.
- Keep concrete coding agents, IDEs, software-development benchmarks, SDLC platforms and organization-specific adoption frameworks with their own canonical owners.
- Do not treat the standard as an endorsement of a particular model/provider or as proof that a given coding agent satisfies a defined intelligence level without controlled assessment.
- Treat detailed level definitions, assessment methods, amendments/corrigenda and lifecycle status as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Active-standard status and Board approval remain traceable to IEEE SA.
- The entity remains a software-engineering recommended-practice artifact rather than coding-agent software.
