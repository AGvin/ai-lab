# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 3379-2025 as the active IEEE standard specifying technical architecture, flow, functions, and input/output parameters for deep-learning compiler interfaces.
- Preserve its compiler-interface/interoperability scope rather than treating it as a particular compiler implementation, model-optimization product, accelerator ISA, or inference runtime.
- Keep the standard distinct from IEEE 3549 inference-optimization systems and from concrete compilers such as TVM, IREE, Olive, Optimum, or NNCF.
- Preserve active-standard lifecycle and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity is represented as the published active standard rather than its project/draft state.
