# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 2863-2026 as the active IEEE recommended practice for organizational governance of AI, covering governance principles, processes, implementation, performance auditing, training, compliance, and case-study application.
- Keep its organizational-governance guidance distinct from ISO/IEC 38507, ISO/IEC 42001, legal requirements, and organization-specific governance frameworks.
- Preserve active-standard lifecycle and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity is represented as a published recommended practice, not the superseded draft project state.
