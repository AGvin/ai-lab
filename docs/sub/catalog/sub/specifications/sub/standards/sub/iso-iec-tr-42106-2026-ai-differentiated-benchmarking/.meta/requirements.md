# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 42106:2026 as the published Technical Report surveying conceptual frameworks for differentiated/graded benchmarking of AI-system quality characteristics based on system complexity and context of use.
- Preserve its Technical Report identity and do not present it as a mandatory benchmark suite, certification rule, or replacement for concrete evaluation datasets.
- Keep benchmarking methodology distinct from ISO/IEC 25059 quality-model definitions and from individual model/agent benchmark results.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.
