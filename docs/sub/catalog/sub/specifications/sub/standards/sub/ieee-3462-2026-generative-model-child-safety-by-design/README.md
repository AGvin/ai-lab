# IEEE 3462-2026 — Safety by Design in Generative Models to Prioritize Child Safety

**IEEE 3462-2026** is an IEEE recommended practice on applying Safety by Design to the development, deployment and maintenance of generative AI models with particular attention to child safety. IEEE SA records it as **Active Standard** under the title **Approved Draft Recommended Practice**. Its public description addresses child sexual abuse and exploitation risks across text, image, video and audio systems. [IEEE publisher record](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/3462/11584/).

## Safety scope and limits

This practice provides a standards-level lifecycle framework rather than a child-safety service, content classifier, moderation API, legal regulation or product certification. Adopting a practice alone is not evidence that deployed safeguards are effective, legally sufficient or suitable for every population and usage context.

Detailed safeguards, controls, evaluation obligations and later corrections remain specific to the licensed normative text and active revision. No production safety assessment or compliance assurance is implied by this profile.
