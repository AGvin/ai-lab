# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify the *published edition* **NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1)**, issued January 26, 2023 by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology. Keep the fixed 1.0 publication separate from NIST's announced 2026 revision work; never claim a draft/revision concept is a currently final successor.
- Clearly classify this as a **voluntary, non-sector-specific risk management framework**, not an ISO/IEC International Standard, federal regulation, certification requirement, certification service, software platform, audit badge or guarantee of legal compliance. The standards catalog includes independently published guides and recommended practices without upgrading them to law.
- Describe its four current published Core functions **Govern, Map, Measure, Manage**, emphasizing continuous lifecycle risk management and adaptable organizational outcomes rather than a strictly linear checklist or 100% safety guarantee.
- Distinguish the separately published 2024 **NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile** (companion application) and NIST's changeable AI RMF **Playbook** from this primary 2023 framework; do not collapse them into multiple numbered editions of the same artifact.
- Link the NIST source publication and official current framework hub; explicitly note the 2026 ongoing revision so date/lifecycle advice remains time scoped. Avoid importing verbatim copyrighted long passages and avoid implying that NIST endorses any commercial AI product.
- Add one canonical source organization **NIST**, a federal non-regulatory agency within the U.S. Department of Commerce, with exactly reciprocal `produced-by` / `produces` relation. The agency is neither a commercial model vendor nor an owner of third-party AI systems described in the framework.

## Validation

- Separate NIST AI 600-1 profile and changing Playbook, no new legal obligation or certification invented.
- One exact NIST producer inverse; scoped readback is not whole-catalog schema/cache/CI, final render or legal compliance assurance.
