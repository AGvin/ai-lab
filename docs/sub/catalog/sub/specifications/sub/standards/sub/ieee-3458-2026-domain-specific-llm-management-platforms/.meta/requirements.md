# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 3458-2026 as the active IEEE standard specifying an architecture and requirements for domain-specific large-language-model management platforms.
- Preserve its scope across deployment, security, management, integration, service guidance, and evaluation indexes without treating the standard as a concrete commercial AI-management platform, model, runtime, or benchmark.
- Keep concrete AI-management/governance platforms, model-serving stacks, domain-specific models, and implementation products with their own canonical owners.
- Treat amendments, corrigenda, successor standards, detailed normative requirements, and lifecycle status as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Active-standard status and publication date remain traceable to IEEE SA.
- The entity remains a standards artifact rather than a software/service product.

- The publisher's public description covers platform architecture, deployment/security/management/integration requirements, service guidance and evaluation indexes, not any one deployed platform, foundation model, provider service, certified governance product or measured benchmark.

- Scope limited to official record/status, dates and public synopsis. No paid full IEEE text, domain-platform integration, independent legal/security/conformance assessment, full schema/cache/CI/render, clean or final delivery credit.
