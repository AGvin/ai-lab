# IEEE 7015-2026 — Data and AI Literacy, Skills, and Readiness

**IEEE 7015-2026** is an IEEE standard defining an operational framework for data and artificial intelligence literacy, skills and readiness. IEEE SA marks it **Active Standard**, with Standards Board approval on **June 4, 2026**, and the record title **Approved Draft Standard**. Do not infer a separate final-publication date from approval alone. [IEEE publisher record](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/7015/10688/).

## Scope and implementation boundary

Its public overview covers common terminology, competency and proficiency levels, educational or training inclusion, policy considerations, measurable indicators and readiness assessment. The standard is not itself an instructional curriculum, AI model benchmark, professional certificate or binding AI governance law.

Specific level descriptions, indicators, assessment methods and conformance language need the applicable edition. No institution's readiness, training outcomes or compliance was tested for this catalog entry.
