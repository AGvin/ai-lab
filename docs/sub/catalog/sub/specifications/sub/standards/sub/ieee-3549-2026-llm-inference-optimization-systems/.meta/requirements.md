# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 3549-2026 as the active IEEE standard specifying architecture, functional requirements, performance metrics, and evaluation methods for LLM inference-optimization systems.
- Preserve its cross-cutting inference-system scope rather than treating it as a concrete inference engine, model compiler, accelerator product, benchmark leaderboard, or deployment service.
- Keep the standard distinct from IEEE 2937 AI-server benchmarking, IEEE 3379 compiler-interface standardization, and vendor-specific runtime/optimization frameworks.
- Preserve active-standard lifecycle and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity is represented as a published/active technical standard rather than its earlier PAR/draft state.
