# ITU-T Y.4615 — On-device Framework and Requirements for Robot Services

**ITU-T Y.4615 (06/2026)** is an **in-force ITU-T Recommendation** covering an Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT) on-device framework and requirements for robot services. It was approved on **June 29, 2026**; the official final-edition page lists an English PDF posted on **September 1, 2026**. [ITU-T final-edition record](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-Y.4615-202606-I).

## Standards boundary

This Recommendation concerns requirements and framework-level integration of AI/IoT functions in on-device robot services, including local-processing and hardware-environment considerations. It is not itself a physical robot, certified robotics safety system, model, installable robot framework or deployed edge runtime.

Products such as Strands Robots, LeRobot and NVIDIA Isaac runtimes require separate compatibility and operating evidence. An ITU-T standards entry does not certify their conformity or reliable control of real-world hardware.

## Currentness and interpretation

Detailed technical requirements, applicability and successors are edition-specific. The public record verifies its identity and lifecycle; no independent robot benchmark, physical test, safety assurance or full-text conformance assessment was performed.
