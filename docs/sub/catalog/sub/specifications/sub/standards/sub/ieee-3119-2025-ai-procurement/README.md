# IEEE 3119-2025

IEEE 3119-2025 is the published IEEE Standard for the Procurement of Artificial Intelligence and Automated Decision Systems.

## Standard boundary

Its procurement-process scope covers problem definition, solicitation preparation, vendor evaluation, solution evaluation, contract negotiation, and contract monitoring. It remains distinct from technical model evaluation, organization-wide AI governance, legal compliance, vendor-specific sourcing processes, and procurement services.
