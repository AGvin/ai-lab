# ISO/TS 24971-2:2026 — ML-enabled Medical Device Risk Management Guidance

**ISO/TS 24971-2:2026**, Edition 1, is a **published Technical Specification** from June 2026 covering AI/ML-specific risk considerations in applying ISO 14971 medical-device risk-management processes. It supplements ISO 14971 and ISO/TR 24971; it does not replace their underlying risk-management framework. [ISO publisher record](https://www.iso.org/standard/24971-2).

## Important scope exclusion

The current ISO publisher abstract explicitly excludes **machine-learning-enabled medical devices using large language models or generative AI**. This Technical Specification must therefore not be cited as a complete risk-management standard for LLM-based or generative medical devices. Its scope differs from general AI risk guidance in ISO/IEC 23894 and management-system requirements in ISO/IEC 42001.

## Evidence and compliance boundary

This catalog entry records the edition, publisher scope and public overview. Detailed requirements depend on the licensed Technical Specification and applicable medical-device regulatory framework. No clinical performance, device conformity, regulatory approval or independent legal/safety assessment is established by this page.
