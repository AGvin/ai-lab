# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 12792:2025 as the published international standard providing a transparency taxonomy for AI systems.
- Preserve the formal taxonomy/terminology boundary and do not conflate it with legal transparency obligations, explainability techniques, model cards, or organization-specific disclosure policy.
- Keep edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a formal published standard artifact relevant to AI transparency/governance decision support.
