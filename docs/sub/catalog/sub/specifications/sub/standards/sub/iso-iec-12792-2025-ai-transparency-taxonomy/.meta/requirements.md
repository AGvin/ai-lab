# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 12792:2025 as the published international standard providing a transparency taxonomy for AI systems.
- Preserve the formal taxonomy/terminology boundary and do not conflate it with legal transparency obligations, explainability techniques, model cards, or organization-specific disclosure policy.
- Keep edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a formal published standard artifact relevant to AI transparency/governance decision support.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 12792:2025 is the published **Edition 1 (November 2025)** International Standard presenting a taxonomy of AI-system transparency information elements, including stakeholder objectives and relevance of disclosure. It supplies vocabulary and classification, not a model-card template, algorithmic-explainability benchmark or jurisdictional disclosure obligation.
- Applicability to organizations and AI applications does not prove that a specific AI service is transparent or compliant. Definitions, mandatory field interpretations and cross-standard mappings require the complete authorized normative source and organization-specific evidence.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/84111.html
- Only ISO public identity, edition, publication status and abstract accepted as a bounded original-source gate. No full copyrighted clauses, model inspection, legal audit, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
