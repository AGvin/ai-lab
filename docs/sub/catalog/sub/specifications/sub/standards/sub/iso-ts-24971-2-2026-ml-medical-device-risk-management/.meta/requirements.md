# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/TS 24971-2:2026 as the published Technical Specification giving guidance on AI/ML-specific risks and application of ISO 14971 risk management to machine-learning-enabled medical devices.
- Preserve its intended conjunction with ISO 14971 and ISO/TR 24971 rather than presenting it as a standalone replacement risk-management system.
- Preserve the explicit scope exclusion: the current edition does not apply to ML-enabled medical devices employing large language models or generative AI.
- Keep medical-device risk guidance distinct from general AI risk-management standards such as ISO/IEC 23894 and from AI management-system standards such as ISO/IEC 42001.
- Treat later editions, amendments, scope changes, and related medical-device AI standards as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- The entity is represented as a published Technical Specification, Edition 1 (2026), not as a generic medical-AI concept.
- The LLM/generative-AI exclusion is not omitted or generalized away.
