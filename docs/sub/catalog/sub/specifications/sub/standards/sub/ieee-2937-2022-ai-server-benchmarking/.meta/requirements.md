# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 2937-2022 as the active IEEE standard defining formal methods, metrics, measures, and tool requirements for benchmarking AI server, cluster, and HPC systems.
- Preserve its system-performance benchmarking scope rather than treating it as a specific accelerator benchmark suite, model benchmark, or procurement requirement.
- Keep concrete MLPerf results, vendor performance claims, hardware products, and AI-agent benchmark standards/projects separate.

## Validation

- The entity remains a cross-cutting AI-server benchmarking standard.
