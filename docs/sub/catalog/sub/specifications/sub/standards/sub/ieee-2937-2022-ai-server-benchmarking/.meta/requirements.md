# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IEEE 2937-2022 as the active IEEE standard defining formal methods, metrics, measures, and tool requirements for benchmarking AI server, cluster, and HPC systems.
- Preserve its system-performance benchmarking scope rather than treating it as a specific accelerator benchmark suite, model benchmark, or procurement requirement.
- Keep concrete MLPerf results, vendor performance claims, hardware products, and AI-agent benchmark standards/projects separate.

## Validation

- The entity remains a cross-cutting AI-server benchmarking standard.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-08

- **IEEE 2937-2022** is an **active IEEE Standard**, published **2022-09-14** after Standards Board approval on **2022-06-16**. The public publisher record addresses benchmarking AI servers, clusters and HPC systems, with formal testing approaches, metrics and requirements for benchmarking tools.
- The specification concerns system and infrastructure performance benchmarking, not an executed accelerator comparison, MLPerf submission, specific trained-model leaderboard, certification of a vendor product or an AI-agent task benchmark.
- Verified original publisher source: https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/2937/10376/
- Bounded source acceptance covers existing standard identity, published status, date and official abstract. Licensed normative details, measured hardware results, independent conformance/security/legal audit, repository-wide validation, clean passes and final gates are not credited.
