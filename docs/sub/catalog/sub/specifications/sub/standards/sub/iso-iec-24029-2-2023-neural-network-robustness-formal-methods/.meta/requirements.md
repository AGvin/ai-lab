# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 24029-2:2023 as the published Part 2 methodology for using formal methods to assess robustness properties of neural networks.
- Keep this methodology-specific standard distinct from Part 1's overview and from the still-unpublished Part 3 statistical-methodology work.
- Do not turn formal-method examples into claims about a particular verifier, model architecture, or benchmark.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains Part 2 of the ISO/IEC 24029 robustness series and does not imply Part 3 is released.
