# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 24029-2:2023 as the published Part 2 methodology for using formal methods to assess robustness properties of neural networks.
- Keep this methodology-specific standard distinct from Part 1's overview and from the still-unpublished Part 3 statistical-methodology work.
- Do not turn formal-method examples into claims about a particular verifier, model architecture, or benchmark.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains Part 2 of the ISO/IEC 24029 robustness series and does not imply Part 3 is released.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 24029-2:2023 is the published **Edition 1 (August 2023)** International Standard providing methodology for use of formal methods to assess neural-network robustness properties. It addresses selecting, applying and managing formal assessment methods; its identity is distinct from Part 1's overview and unverified future work.
- Publication does not certify a neural network, verifier, architecture, performance level or robustness against every real-world input. Exact proof obligations, models and conformance claims cannot be reconstructed from public catalogue text.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/79804.html
- Scoped official edition/status/abstract accepted only; no licensed full normative text, formal proof performed, validation of a concrete model, independent security/legal certification, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
