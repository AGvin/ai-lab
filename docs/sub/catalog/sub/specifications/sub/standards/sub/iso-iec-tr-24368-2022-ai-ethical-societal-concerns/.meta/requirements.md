# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 24368:2022 as the published Technical Report providing a high-level overview of ethical and societal concerns, concern sources, principles, processes, and relevant standards for AI.
- Preserve its non-prescriptive overview identity: it does not mandate one ethical value system, certify products, or replace organization-specific governance and impact-assessment practices.
- Keep ethics/societal concerns distinct from dedicated bias, transparency, risk, governance, and beneficial-AI artifacts while preserving their adjacency.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC **TR** 24368:2022 is the published **Edition 1 (August 2022) Technical Report** describing ethical and societal concerns around AI, their sources and relevant principles/processes/related standards. The publisher's summary addresses a cross-disciplinary stakeholder audience without establishing a single mandatory ethical value system.
- This overview is not a jurisdictional ethics law, an accredited product certification or a substitute for context-specific stakeholder engagement, bias assessment, transparency commitments and impact/risk management. Exact normative clauses and scope cannot be reconstructed from the freely accessible summary.
