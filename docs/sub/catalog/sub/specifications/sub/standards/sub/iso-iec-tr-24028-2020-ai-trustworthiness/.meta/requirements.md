# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 24028:2020 as the published Technical Report surveying AI trustworthiness topics, threats, pitfalls, mitigations, and assessment approaches across attributes such as reliability, resilience, safety, security, privacy, transparency, explainability, and controllability.
- Preserve its overview/guidance identity rather than presenting it as a quantitative trust score, certification scheme, or product-security standard.
- Keep trustworthiness distinct from dedicated risk, bias, robustness, security, transparency, and explainability standards while preserving cross-links in decision support.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC **TR** 24028:2020 is the published **Edition 1 (May 2020) Technical Report** giving an overview of AI trustworthiness, including transparency, explainability, controllability, pitfalls, threats and mitigations, plus reliability, availability, robustness, security, safety and privacy dimensions.
- ISO's public abstract expressly excludes specifying numerical levels of trustworthiness. It is not an AI trust score, certified assurance level or demonstration that a particular model/system meets each property; implementation evidence and other detailed standards remain distinct.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/77608.html
- Existing TR edition/status/public ISO abstract only source-qualified; no full protected report text, independent model-risk assessment, formal trust rating, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
