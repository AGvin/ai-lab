# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 24028:2020 as the published Technical Report surveying AI trustworthiness topics, threats, pitfalls, mitigations, and assessment approaches across attributes such as reliability, resilience, safety, security, privacy, transparency, explainability, and controllability.
- Preserve its overview/guidance identity rather than presenting it as a quantitative trust score, certification scheme, or product-security standard.
- Keep trustworthiness distinct from dedicated risk, bias, robustness, security, transparency, and explainability standards while preserving cross-links in decision support.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.
