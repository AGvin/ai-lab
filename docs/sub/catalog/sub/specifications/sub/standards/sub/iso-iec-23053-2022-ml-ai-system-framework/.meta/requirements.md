# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 23053:2022 as the published international standard defining a framework for describing AI systems that use machine learning.
- Keep the formal standard artifact distinct from reusable ML-system concepts and from under-development amendments such as the Generative AI amendment until those amendments are published.
- Preserve the exact edition/year and treat amendments, corrigenda, revision state, and conformance interpretations as freshness-sensitive.
- Do not promote under-development amendments into the current released standard identity.

## Validation

- Placement follows the artifact's upstream International Standard identity even though `framework` appears in its title.
- AI Lab summaries do not substitute for the normative standard text.
