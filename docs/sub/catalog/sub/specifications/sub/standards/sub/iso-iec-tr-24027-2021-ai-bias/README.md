# ISO/IEC TR 24027:2021

ISO/IEC TR 24027:2021 is the published Technical Report addressing bias in AI systems and AI-aided decision making across the AI-system life cycle.

## Report boundary

Its scope covers bias analysis and assessment methods. It is not a legal fairness mandate, specific debiasing library, or benchmark dataset. Bias concerns remain adjacent to but distinct from broader trustworthiness, ethics, risk-management, and transparency standards.
