# IEEE 2863-2026

IEEE 2863-2026 is the active IEEE Recommended Practice for Organizational Governance of Artificial Intelligence.

## Standard boundary

It addresses organizational AI-governance principles, processes, implementation, performance auditing, training, compliance, and case-study application. Its scope is distinct from ISO/IEC 38507, ISO/IEC 42001, legal requirements, and organization-specific governance frameworks.

The catalog tracks the published recommended practice and its active lifecycle rather than the superseded project or draft state.
