# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present ITU-T F.748.74 (06/26) as the in-force Recommendation defining requirements and a framework for evaluating multimodal foundation models.
- Preserve its formal standards identity separately from concrete multimodal benchmarks, model leaderboards, evaluator products, datasets, and individual model cards.
- Treat detailed evaluation dimensions, metrics, conformance interpretation, amendments, corrigenda, and successor Recommendations as freshness-sensitive to the current official publication.
- Do not convert the Recommendation into an AI Lab model ranking or imply that compliance/evaluation under the framework establishes universal quality, safety, or suitability for every workload.

## Validation

- The entity remains a formal Recommendation rather than a generic multimodal-evaluation concept.
- Any concrete benchmark or metric examples remain owned by their applicable catalog/concept nodes.
- Lifecycle/status claims stay tied to the official ITU-T record.
