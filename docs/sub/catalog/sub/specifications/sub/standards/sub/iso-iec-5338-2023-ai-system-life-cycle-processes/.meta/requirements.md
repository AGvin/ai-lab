# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 5338:2023 as the published international standard defining AI-system life-cycle processes and associated concepts.
- Keep the formal process standard distinct from AI Lab explanatory lifecycle concepts, project-management practices, and software-development frameworks.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative process text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a formal standard artifact linked to lifecycle/process decision support rather than replacing it.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 5338:2023 is the published **Edition 1 (December 2023) International Standard** for processes and related concepts describing the life cycle of AI systems, including machine-learning and heuristic-based components. The ISO abstract identifies alignment with established system/software life-cycle standards and AI-specific process additions.
- It is a process specification, not a ready-made MLOps tool, standalone project methodology, certification badge or assurance that a particular deployed AI system was correctly managed. Versioned process clauses and implementation evidence require access to the authoritative normative text and organizational records.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://www.iso.org/standard/81118.html
- Scope and edition accepted from publisher metadata only; no reproduction of full copyrighted process text, independent implementation assessment, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
