# IEEE 3376-2026 — Evaluating Artificial Intelligence Generated Content

**IEEE 3376-2026** is an IEEE recommended practice addressing evaluation of AI-generated content, including generative capabilities, evaluation content and metrics. The official IEEE Standards Association record marks it **Active Standard** and gives **September 25, 2026** as the Standards Board approval date. Its record describes an **Approved Draft Recommended Practice**; this approval does not establish an independently verified final-publication date. [IEEE publisher record](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/3376/11303/).

## Scope and interpretation

The practice concerns how generated content can be assessed, rather than prescribing a single benchmark dataset, model, detector, watermark or automated safety system. Exact metric definitions and conformance details require the applicable licensed standards text and edition. Approval status is not proof that a model or vendor product passes any particular evaluation.

## Publication and use boundary

Preserve the IEEE edition and its active lifecycle separately from future amendments or corrigenda. No benchmark was run, and the full licensed normative text, claimed safety performance and external compliance have not been independently verified here.
