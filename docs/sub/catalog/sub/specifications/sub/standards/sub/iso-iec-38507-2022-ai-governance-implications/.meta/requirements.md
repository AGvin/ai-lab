# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 38507:2022 as the published international guidance standard on organizational governance implications of AI use.
- Keep governing-body guidance distinct from ISO/IEC 42001 management-system requirements, ISO/IEC 23894 risk guidance, regulatory law, and organization-specific governance frameworks.
- Preserve edition/revision status and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a formal published standard artifact.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 38507:2022 is the **published Edition 1 (April 2022)** International Standard offering guidance on organizational-governing-body implications of AI use. The governance audience and outcomes differ from management-system requirements in ISO/IEC 42001 and from AI risk-management methods in ISO/IEC 23894.
- This guidance does not create a mandatory regulation or certify governing bodies or vendors. Specific governance controls, compliance mappings, national adoptions and legal applicability require separate current evidence and access to applicable normative texts.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/56641.html
- Source acceptance is scoped to the public ISO edition, title, status, and scope only; no reproduction of the paid standard, actual organizational assessment, legal assurance, full schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass, or final gate.
