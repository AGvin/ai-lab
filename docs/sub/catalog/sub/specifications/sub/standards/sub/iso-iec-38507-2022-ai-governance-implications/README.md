# ISO/IEC 38507:2022

ISO/IEC 38507:2022 is the published International Standard providing guidance on organizational governance implications of AI use.

## Standard boundary

Its governing-body guidance is distinct from ISO/IEC 42001 management-system requirements, ISO/IEC 23894 risk guidance, regulatory law, and organization-specific governance frameworks.

The page preserves the released edition and revision boundary without reproducing normative text.
