# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 24027:2021 as the published Technical Report addressing bias in AI systems and AI-aided decision making across the AI-system life cycle.
- Preserve its bias-analysis and assessment-method scope rather than presenting it as a legal fairness mandate, a specific debiasing library, or a benchmark dataset.
- Keep bias concerns distinct from broader trustworthiness, ethics, risk-management, and transparency standards while noting their adjacent decision-support roles.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC **TR** 24027:2021 is the published **Edition 1 (November 2021) Technical Report** on bias in AI systems and AI-aided decision-making. The public ISO abstract covers bias-related vulnerabilities, assessment and measurement approaches across data collection, learning, system design, tests and use.
- The report does not impose a jurisdiction-wide fairness threshold, certify any model as unbiased or establish a specific dataset, metric or debiasing library as universally sufficient. Broader legal obligations and protected-group outcome assessment must be evaluated for the concrete deployment.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/77607.html
- Only publisher identity, released TR edition, status and abstract source-qualified; no licensed full normative text, bias evaluation, legal/certification audit, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
