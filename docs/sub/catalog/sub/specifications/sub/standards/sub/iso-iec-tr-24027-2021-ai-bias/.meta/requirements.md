# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC TR 24027:2021 as the published Technical Report addressing bias in AI systems and AI-aided decision making across the AI-system life cycle.
- Preserve its bias-analysis and assessment-method scope rather than presenting it as a legal fairness mandate, a specific debiasing library, or a benchmark dataset.
- Keep bias concerns distinct from broader trustworthiness, ethics, risk-management, and transparency standards while noting their adjacent decision-support roles.
- Preserve edition/revision state and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The catalog preserves the upstream published Technical Report lifecycle and exact edition/year.
