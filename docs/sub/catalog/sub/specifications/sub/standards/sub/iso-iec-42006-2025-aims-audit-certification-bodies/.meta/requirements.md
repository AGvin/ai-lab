# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 42006:2025 as the published standard defining additional requirements for bodies that audit and certify AI management systems against ISO/IEC 42001.
- Keep certification-body requirements distinct from the organization-level AIMS requirements in ISO/IEC 42001 and from product/service certification schemes.
- Preserve edition/revision status and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a conformity-assessment standard rather than a certification service.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 42006:2025 is the published **Edition 1 (July 2025)** International Standard defining additional requirements for bodies that audit and certify AI management systems (AIMS). ISO describes its relationship to ISO/IEC 42001 and ISO/IEC 17021-1; the standard addresses **audit and certification bodies**, not the AI-system vendor or general end-user organization directly.
- A released standard for auditors does not establish that any certification body is accredited, that a customer is certified, or that any model satisfies security, safety or regulatory rules. Specific accreditation and legal outcomes require separate evidence.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/42006
- Only the ISO published identity, edition and abstract source-qualified. No full paid normative clauses, real certification-body audit, model safety testing, root-wide schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
