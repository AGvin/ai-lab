# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 42006:2025 as the published standard defining additional requirements for bodies that audit and certify AI management systems against ISO/IEC 42001.
- Keep certification-body requirements distinct from the organization-level AIMS requirements in ISO/IEC 42001 and from product/service certification schemes.
- Preserve edition/revision status and avoid reproducing normative text.

## Validation

- The entity remains a conformity-assessment standard rather than a certification service.
