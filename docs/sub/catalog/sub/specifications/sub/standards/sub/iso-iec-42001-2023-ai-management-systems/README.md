# ISO/IEC 42001:2023

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the published International Standard for artificial-intelligence management systems.

## Standard boundary

It is a requirements-oriented management-system standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving organizational AI management. Certification services, consulting, implementation products, national adoptions, audit evidence, and organization-specific controls remain outside the standard identity.

Amendments, corrigenda, revision status, certification-ecosystem claims, and conformance interpretations are freshness-sensitive.
