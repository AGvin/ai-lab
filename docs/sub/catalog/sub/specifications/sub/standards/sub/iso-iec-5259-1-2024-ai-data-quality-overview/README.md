# ISO/IEC 5259-1:2024

ISO/IEC 5259-1:2024 is the published Part 1 foundation for the ISO/IEC 5259 data-quality series for analytics and machine learning.

## Standard boundary

Part 1 provides the series overview, terminology, and examples. Its formal terminology remains distinct from AI Lab's local glossary and concept ownership, and it should not be collapsed with the more specific measures, management, process, or governance parts of the series.
