# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify **NIST AI 600-1 — Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative AI Profile**, published July 26, 2024. Treat it as a separate, released, citable NIST publication with its own durable report/DOI identity, not as AI RMF 2.0, a preview of 2026 revision or a renamed implementation of the base 2023 framework.
- The profile is a **voluntary companion** to NIST AI RMF 1.0; it addresses cross-sector risks specific to generative AI and suggested risk-management actions. It is not a binding technical regulation, mandatory model evaluation suite, single-model safety certificate, ISO standard or assurance that deployments are safe.
- Distinguish its original 2024 publication context, including the cited then-current presidential executive order, from the law and government policies actually applicable in November 2026; do not imply that an earlier executive order remains legally active solely because the 2024 publication mentions it.
- Link to the distinct [AI RMF 1.0 2023 canonical entity] and NIST's current framework hub; the separately maintained NIST Playbook and prospective revisions are not silently counted as new versions of the 2024 publication.
- Maintain one original NIST governmental publisher and exact inverse `produced-by` / `produces` pair. Do not infer endorsement of a vendor, rights to third-party models, certification or full current risk assessment from NIST publication.
- Cite first-party NIST dated publication page and the stable DOI. Future amendments or revisions are freshness-sensitive, not assumed.

## Validation

- Exactly one NIST AI 600-1 2024 publication, distinct from 2023 AI 100-1 and later draft revisions.
- Voluntary guidance versus legal certification is explicit; original date and policy context retained.
- Reciprocal NIST organization relation and local reader links verified in the scoped commit; no whole-repository schema/CI assertion.
