# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 25059:2023 as the currently published Edition 1 SQuaRE quality-model standard for AI systems.
- Explicitly preserve its current lifecycle state: published but expected to be replaced by the under-development Edition 2; do not promote the FDIS to released status prematurely.
- Keep formal quality characteristics distinct from local evaluation guidance and benchmark results.

## Validation

- The released 2023 edition remains canonical until its replacement is actually published.
