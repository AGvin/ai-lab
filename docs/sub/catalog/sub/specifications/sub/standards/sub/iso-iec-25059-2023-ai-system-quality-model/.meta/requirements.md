# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ISO/IEC 25059:2023 as the currently published Edition 1 SQuaRE quality-model standard for AI systems.
- Explicitly preserve its current lifecycle state: published but expected to be replaced by the under-development Edition 2; do not promote the FDIS to released status prematurely.
- Keep formal quality characteristics distinct from local evaluation guidance and benchmark results.

## Validation

- The released 2023 edition remains canonical until its replacement is actually published.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- ISO/IEC 25059:2023 is the **published Edition 1 (June 2023)** International Standard for the AI-system quality model in the SQuaRE series. Its public scope is a quality-characteristics model used in quality requirements and evaluation, distinct from model benchmarking results or a specific runtime conformance report.
- ISO's official catalogue marks the edition as **published, under revision** and links the replacement `ISO/IEC FDIS 25059` as under development. An FDIS is not the published new edition; preserve the 2023 identity pending a formal revision release.
- Verified original publisher source: https://www.iso.org/standard/80655.html
- Scope limited to ISO's public title, edition, status, and abstract. Full copyrighted clauses, conformance, AI-quality testing, and independent safety/security certification were not accessed or claimed. No whole-repository schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass, or final gate.
