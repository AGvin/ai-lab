# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ChatGPT as OpenAI's hosted conversational AI and assistant-workspace service spanning chat, research, files, multimodal work, projects, memory/personalization, connected apps/plugins, reusable skills, and tool-enabled workflows where currently available.
- Keep the product boundary distinct from underlying OpenAI model identities and from OpenAI Codex as the repository-oriented coding-agent product.
- Preserve current workspace/project boundary at a stable level: personal and managed-workspace controls, enabled tools, apps/plugins, retention, data residency, memory, and administrative policy can materially change available behavior.
- Preserve connected-app/plugin trust boundaries: external data/action access depends on granted scopes, app/plugin permissions, workspace controls, user approval policy, and third-party service permissions; installing a skill does not itself grant access to an external system.
- Preserve data-control distinctions: consumer and managed/business offerings can have different training, retention, administration, and contractual defaults; keep exact plan-specific claims source-backed and time-scoped.
- Preserve the legacy repository-owner first-hand evaluation as explicitly dated subjective evidence, not as an OpenAI claim, benchmark, or universal product fact. The observation dated 2026-07-25 reports repeated instruction-following/hallucination overhead and unreliable observable Skill triggering in ordinary conversations for the owner's tested configurations; retain its scope/model/date qualifiers and require future reassessment.
- Keep exact plans, model availability, usage limits, tool/plugin/skill catalogs, workspace controls, regional availability, and UI behavior source-backed and time-scoped when expanded.
- Link the canonical OpenAI producer and OpenAI Codex Hybrid Agent profiles where relevant.
- Include current official ChatGPT product/help, Terms, data-controls, Projects, and Apps documentation.

## Validation

- ChatGPT is represented as a hosted service/workspace, not installable software merely because native client apps exist.
- ChatGPT is not collapsed into a GPT model family or OpenAI Codex.
- Connected app/plugin access is not described as automatically granted by a skill or prompt.
- The first-hand owner observation is clearly labeled subjective and dated, with no generalization to all users/models/deployments.
- The producer relation resolves to OpenAI.
