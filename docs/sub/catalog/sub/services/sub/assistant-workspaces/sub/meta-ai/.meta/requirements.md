# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Meta AI as Meta's hosted general-purpose AI assistant product spanning the standalone Meta AI app/web experience and Meta-operated assistant surfaces across supported Meta applications/devices.
- Preserve Meta AI's independent assistant identity from the separately canonical Muse personal-agent service and from underlying Muse/Llama trained-model identities.
- Keep personalization/memory, voice/live perception, image generation/editing, web/search/community-context grounding, subagent/parallel reasoning, shopping/discovery and cross-Meta-app surfaces as product capabilities rather than peer products unless a later durable lifecycle establishes a separate owner.
- Keep specialized Meta AI support-assistant workflows, Facebook/Instagram feature embeddings, AI-glasses hardware, underlying model APIs and Meta social products with their own canonical owners.
- Treat model routing, countries/languages, app/device integrations, memory/personalization, voice/live features, shopping, social grounding, data controls/training, teen/supervision controls and rollout state as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- Meta AI remains a hosted assistant service rather than the Muse or Llama model family.
- Meta AI is not collapsed into the distinct Muse background personal-agent service.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to Meta.
