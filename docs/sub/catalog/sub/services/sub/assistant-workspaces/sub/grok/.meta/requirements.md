# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Grok as xAI's hosted AI assistant service available through xAI-operated web and mobile surfaces.
- Keep the hosted assistant boundary distinct from underlying Grok model identities and from Grok integrations delivered through separately operated third-party surfaces.
- Keep exact plans, models, feature limits, integrations, account controls, regional availability, and data-handling behavior source-backed and time-scoped when expanded.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current official Grok product, overview, and FAQ resources.

## Validation

- Grok is represented as a hosted assistant service rather than as a model family.
- xAI-operated Grok surfaces are not conflated with separately operated third-party services.
- Mutable product claims are not presented as timeless facts.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
