# ChatGPT

ChatGPT is OpenAI's hosted conversational AI and assistant-workspace service for chat, research, files, multimodal work, projects, memory/personalization, connected applications, reusable skills, and tool-enabled workflows where those features are available.

## Product and workspace boundary

ChatGPT is the hosted product/workspace identity. Underlying GPT/model families remain separate model-reference entities, and [OpenAI Codex](../../../../../software/sub/agents/sub/hybrid/sub/openai-codex/) owns the repository-oriented coding-agent product boundary.

Capabilities vary by plan, managed-workspace policy, region, model/mode, and enabled features. Projects inherit relevant workspace controls; apps/plugins can connect external data and actions, with effective access determined by connection scopes, app/plugin permissions, workspace administration, and user approval policy. A skill or prompt does not by itself grant access to an external system.

Data-use, retention, residency, training, memory, and administrator controls differ between consumer and managed/business contexts and can change. Verify the active account/workspace and current policy before using sensitive data.

## Dated first-hand evaluation

**Repository-owner observation — 2026-07-25; subjective evidence, not an OpenAI claim or benchmark.**

In the repository owner's tested ChatGPT configurations, repeated practical use sometimes required substantial correction of instruction-following failures or hallucinations even when requirements and repository conventions were supplied in detail. The owner also observed that Skills exposed to ordinary ChatGPT conversations sometimes did not provide reliable observable evidence that they had triggered or affected the workflow.

This observation is deliberately scoped to the owner's tested sessions, models, account surfaces, and date. It must not be generalized to every ChatGPT user, model, deployment, Skill, or later product version and should be reassessed after material platform changes.
