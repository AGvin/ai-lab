# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Gemini Apps as Google's hosted conversational AI and assistant-workspace service, using `Gemini` as the canonical product name for this service node.
- Keep the hosted product boundary distinct from underlying Gemini model identities and from Gemini CLI as separate installable agent software.
- Keep exact plans, models, features, Connected Apps, account or workspace controls, regional availability, activity retention, and data-handling behavior source-backed and time-scoped when expanded.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current official Gemini Apps product, help, and privacy resources.

## Validation

- Gemini is represented as a hosted service/workspace rather than as a model family or Gemini CLI.
- Mutable product and account-policy claims are not presented as timeless facts.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-07

- Gemini Apps is Google's hosted conversational assistant surface, distinct from the Gemini trained model family, Gemini API/developer platform and separately installable Gemini CLI. Connected Apps and task-related features operate within per-account permissions and regional/plan availability rather than automatically inheriting CLI or developer access.
- Google's Gemini Apps Privacy Hub (last updated 2026-10-19) describes collection, Keep Activity, retention, Connected Apps and feature-specific processing. These disclosures do not establish an account's actual settings or that all content types and managed-workspace agreements follow one policy.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://gemini.google.com/
- Verified first-party source 2: https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961
- Source qualification is limited to existing hosted-product identity and documented access/data boundaries; no authenticated app session, connector/API execution, full privacy/security/legal audit, global schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final gate.
