# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Grok Bot as SpaceXAI's released hosted AI-teammate/digital-worker service for persistent multi-step work across web applications, connected tools, scheduled routines, team collaboration, and vendor-managed cloud-computer execution.
- Preserve the hosted execution boundary: Bots operate on a persistent cloud computer, can sign in to external tools, run in parallel and continue work while the user's local device is offline.
- Keep Grok's general hosted assistant, Grok Build coding-agent product, underlying Grok model identities, Cursor product surfaces, and third-party apps/connectors separate from the Grok Bot service identity.
- Treat Team Bots as a current collaboration capability/evolution of Grok Bot rather than a separate peer product unless later first-party lifecycle establishes an independently durable product boundary.
- Treat the Grok Bot Marketplace as a discovery/install surface within the Grok Bot product boundary; do not materialize every marketplace Bot or template as a peer catalog identity by default.
- Keep plans, pricing, eligible subscriptions, supported desktop/mobile platforms, marketplace inventory, app/site compatibility, computer isolation, memory/learning behavior, scheduling, approval controls, DLP/network controls, data handling, and token limits freshness-sensitive.
- Treat external side effects such as email, purchases, CRM updates, deployments, or other connected-tool actions as permissioned operations requiring the target system's scopes and review/approval controls.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

- Preserve the FAQ boundary reviewed on 2026-10-21: all Bots of one user share one persistent cloud computer, including files, browser and signed-in accounts; isolation is per user, not per Bot. Do not represent parallel Bots as separate credential compartments.

## Validation

- Grok Bot remains a hosted digital-worker service rather than a trained Grok model or local coding-agent client.
- Team Bots and marketplace entries are not duplicated as standalone products without stronger lifecycle evidence.
- Producer provenance resolves to the current SpaceXAI organization identity.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-08

- **Grok Bot** is the separately hosted agent teammate and digital-worker service documented at the current publisher-owned `x.ai/bot` product page and `docs.x.ai/grok-bot` guides, not the Grok model, consumer chatbot, API, Cursor software product, or individual marketplace bot.
- The service runs workflows on persistent vendor-managed cloud computers, supports multi-bot routines, connected applications and team sharing, with scope-dependent approvals. The current official FAQ expressly states that **a user's Bots share the same cloud computer, browser logins, files and command-line credentials**: different Bots are not separate isolation or credential domains. Team-shared conversations have their own contextual and access boundaries, which must be rechecked before deploying sensitive tasks.
- Team Bots and template/marketplace discovery are features of the service; supported plans, app platforms, quotas, sign-in, shared-bot security policies, memory/retention and connector approvals are mutable and not established by the launch announcement alone. No credentials, external tools, agent/browser commands or subscriptions were used.
- First-party direct product sources: https://x.ai/bot ; https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq ; https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy ; https://x.ai/changelog/bot .
- This source gate qualifies exactly one existing Grok Bot service identity and publicly documented service/computer/security scope. It does not imply executable agent success, independent privacy/security or legal certification, product feature completeness, current worldwide availability, global schema/cache/CI/render, clean or final acceptance.
