- Identify Claude as Anthropic's hosted conversational AI and assistant-workspace service.
- Keep the hosted product boundary distinct from underlying Claude model identities and from Claude Code as a separate coding-agent product.
- Keep exact plans, model availability, feature limits, integrations, account or workspace controls, regional availability, and data-handling behavior source-backed and time-scoped when expanded.

- Mutable product claims are not presented as timeless facts.
