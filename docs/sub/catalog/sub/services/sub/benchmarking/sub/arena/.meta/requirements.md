# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Arena as a hosted public AI benchmarking and leaderboard resource for comparing models across supported arenas/modalities.
- Preserve the legacy operational classification that it is a hosted service and the decision-use warning to verify methodology, model coverage, update cadence, and leaderboard limitations before relying on rankings.
- Link the official Arena surface and leaderboard as canonical research/identification references.
- Do not turn leaderboard rank values into intrinsic model facts or persistent recommendations.

## Validation

- Facts remain source-backed and service-scoped.
- Mutable benchmark results are not represented as timeless canonical entity facts.
- Recommendations remain with decision support.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-06

- Arena measures public AI model preference in user-voted head-to-head comparisons, with separate task/modality leaderboard views. Arena's official ranking guidance distinguishes raw rank from rank spread and reports statistical confidence intervals; raw position alone does not establish a significant quality difference. Arena policies, eligible models, judge population and score aggregation evolve.
- Treat live rankings, vote counts, session counts, and modality-specific performance as dated observational evidence, not intrinsic model facts or a universal benchmark. Verify the exact comparison population and rank uncertainty before a procurement or deployment recommendation.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://help.arena.ai/articles/7011479247-how-to-see-ai-rankings-in-arena-leaderboards
- Verified first-party source 2: https://arena.ai/blog/ranking-method
- Verified first-party source 3: https://arena.ai/leaderboard
- Source qualification accepts the present service identity and documented boundaries only; not a complete freshness or legal/security audit, independent benchmark replication, runtime execution, full schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final-delivery gate.
