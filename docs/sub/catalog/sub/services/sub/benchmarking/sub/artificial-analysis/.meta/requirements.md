# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Artificial Analysis as a hosted independent AI benchmarking resource covering models, inference providers/endpoints, systems, and adjacent AI-stack performance dimensions.
- Preserve the legacy decision-use warning to verify methodology, provider/model coverage, benchmark coverage, pricing assumptions, and leaderboard limitations before using results for decisions.
- Keep provider/model measurements as service output/evidence rather than copying them into canonical provider or model identity facts.
- Link the official site and methodology as research/identification references.

## Validation

- Facts remain source-backed and service-scoped.
- Mutable benchmark results are not represented as timeless canonical entity facts.
- Recommendations remain with decision support.
