# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify BenchLM as a hosted AI benchmark/benchmark-evidence resource used to compare and evaluate AI models.
- Preserve the legacy decision-use warning to verify methodology, model coverage, evidence provenance, update cadence, and benchmark limitations before relying on results.
- Keep aggregated benchmark evidence distinct from canonical model facts and from AI Lab recommendations.
- Link the official BenchLM site as the primary research/identification reference.

## Validation

- Facts remain source-backed and service-scoped.
- Mutable benchmark results are not represented as timeless canonical entity facts.
- Recommendations remain with decision support.
