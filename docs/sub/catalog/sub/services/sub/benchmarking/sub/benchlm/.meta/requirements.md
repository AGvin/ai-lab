# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify BenchLM as a hosted AI benchmark/benchmark-evidence resource used to compare and evaluate AI models.
- Preserve the legacy decision-use warning to verify methodology, model coverage, evidence provenance, update cadence, and benchmark limitations before relying on results.
- Keep aggregated benchmark evidence distinct from canonical model facts and from AI Lab recommendations.
- Link the official BenchLM site as the primary research/identification reference.

## Validation

- Facts remain source-backed and service-scoped.
- Mutable benchmark results are not represented as timeless canonical entity facts.
- Recommendations remain with decision support.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-06

- BenchLM operates an AI benchmark evidence directory and a composite model-ranking surface. Its published overall ranking identifies an estimated/common BenchAlign score derived from uneven public evidence; the provider labels rows with supported or estimated confidence and displays conditional uncertainty intervals. A latest rank is a dated inference, not an independently replicated result for every row.
- Do not present aggregate score as universal model quality. Examine underlying benchmark source, whether the row is measured or estimated, interval width, prompt/harness coverage, model/version identity and missing runs before using its ranking in decisions.
- Verified first-party source 1: https://benchlm.ai/best/overall
- Verified first-party source 2: https://benchlm.ai/benchmarks
- Source qualification accepts the present service identity and documented boundaries only; not a complete freshness or legal/security audit, independent benchmark replication, runtime execution, full schema/cache/CI/render, clean pass or final-delivery gate.
