# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present IBM watsonx.governance as a producer-operated AI governance/management control plane for lifecycle governance, risk, compliance, evaluation evidence, and organizational oversight of AI assets.
- Keep security, monitoring, and evaluation capabilities secondary when organization-wide governance/accountability remains the durable product center.
- Distinguish the managed service identity from independently self-managed governance software when deployment creates a separate complete product identity.
- Treat supported asset types, integrations, deployment/region options, pricing, and regulatory mappings as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- The entity remains AI-management/governance-first rather than security- or observability-first.
- Producer provenance resolves to IBM.
