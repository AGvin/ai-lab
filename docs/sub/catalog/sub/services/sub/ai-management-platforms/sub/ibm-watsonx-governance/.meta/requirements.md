# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present IBM watsonx.governance as a producer-operated AI governance/management control plane for lifecycle governance, risk, compliance, evaluation evidence, and organizational oversight of AI assets.
- Keep security, monitoring, and evaluation capabilities secondary when organization-wide governance/accountability remains the durable product center.
- Distinguish the managed service identity from independently self-managed governance software when deployment creates a separate complete product identity.
- Treat supported asset types, integrations, deployment/region options, pricing, and regulatory mappings as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- The entity remains AI-management/governance-first rather than security- or observability-first.
- Producer provenance resolves to IBM.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-06

- IBM watsonx.governance is an enterprise AI governance/control plane centered on asset discovery, model/agent inventory, risk assessment, approval, regulatory content, continuous monitoring and auditable accountability; this differs from IBM watsonx Orchestrate's operational agent builder and control plane despite interoperable registries and observability integrations.
- IBM's October 14, 2026 first-party release adds discovery of agents, their tools, foundation models, MCP servers and collaborators across IBM Orchestrate, AWS and Microsoft environments, plus telemetry integrations such as Arize and Langfuse. Discovering a third-party agent or importing evaluation evidence is not independently verifying its security, model ownership, or compliance.
- Governance graphs, framework/standard mappings, ROI metrics and vendor impact claims remain provider source material; exact policy-pack versions, customer inventory, monitoring coverage, deployment/cloud option, regulated data handling and pricing need per-tenant proof.
