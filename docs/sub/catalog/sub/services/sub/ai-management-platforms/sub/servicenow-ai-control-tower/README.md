# ServiceNow AI Control Tower

ServiceNow AI Control Tower is an enterprise AI control plane for AI discovery and inventory, lifecycle governance, compliance, observability, security controls, and value management across agents, models, and related AI assets.

## Service boundary

Organization-wide inventory, governance, and control are the durable product center. Security and observability capabilities remain supporting functions rather than the primary classification.

Supported asset types, integrations, controls, release-specific modules, licensing, and availability are freshness-sensitive.
