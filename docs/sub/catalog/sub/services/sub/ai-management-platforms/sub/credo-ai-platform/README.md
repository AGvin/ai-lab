# Credo AI Platform

Credo AI Platform is a managed AI-governance and control-plane product for discovery, registry, lifecycle risk, policy and compliance, monitoring, and governance across AI agents, models, applications, and vendors.

## Service boundary

Enterprise governance and accountability are the product center. Security, guardrail, and observability integrations remain supporting capabilities rather than separate primary identities on this page.

Supported frameworks, policy packs, integrations, regulatory mappings, module names, and availability are freshness-sensitive.
