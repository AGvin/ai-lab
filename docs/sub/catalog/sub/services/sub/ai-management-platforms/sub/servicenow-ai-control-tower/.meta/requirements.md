# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present ServiceNow AI Control Tower as an enterprise AI control plane for AI discovery/inventory, lifecycle governance, compliance, observability, security controls, and value management across agents, models, and related AI assets.
- Keep security and observability capabilities secondary while organization-wide inventory/governance/control remains the durable product center.
- Treat supported asset types, integrations, controls, release-specific modules, licensing, and availability as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- The entity remains AI-management/governance-first rather than security- or observability-first.
- Producer provenance resolves to ServiceNow.
