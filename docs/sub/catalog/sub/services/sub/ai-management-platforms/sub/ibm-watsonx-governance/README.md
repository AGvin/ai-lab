# IBM watsonx.governance

IBM watsonx.governance is IBM's producer-operated AI governance and management control plane for lifecycle governance, risk, compliance, evaluation evidence, and organizational oversight of AI assets.

## Service boundary

Organization-wide governance and accountability are the durable product center; security, monitoring, and evaluation capabilities are supporting functions. Independently self-managed governance software remains a separate identity when its deployment forms a complete product of its own.

Supported asset types, integrations, deployment and region options, pricing, and regulatory mappings are freshness-sensitive.
