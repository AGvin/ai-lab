# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Holistic AI Governance Platform as Holistic AI's managed enterprise AI-governance platform for discovery/inventory, risk and bias assessment, policy/compliance enforcement, monitoring, and governance across models, agents, applications, APIs, pipelines, and vendors.
- Preserve governance/control-plane identity as the product center. Security testing, bias testing, discovery, inventory, regulatory mapping, audit evidence, and operative/guardian agent features remain platform capabilities rather than separate peer products unless upstream establishes independently durable product identities.
- Treat integration counts, regulatory mappings, module names, automated-agent capabilities, customer deployment claims, and availability as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- The entity remains AI-management/governance-first rather than generic observability, AI security, or consulting.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to Holistic AI.
