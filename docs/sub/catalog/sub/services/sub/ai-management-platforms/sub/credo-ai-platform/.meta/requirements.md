# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Credo AI Platform as a managed AI-governance/control-plane product for discovery, registry, lifecycle risk, policy/compliance, monitoring, and governance across AI agents, models, applications, and vendors.
- Keep security, guardrail, and observability integrations secondary while enterprise governance/accountability remains the product center.
- Treat supported frameworks, policy packs, integration counts, module names, regulatory mappings, and availability as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- The entity remains AI-management/governance-first.
- Producer provenance resolves to Credo AI.
