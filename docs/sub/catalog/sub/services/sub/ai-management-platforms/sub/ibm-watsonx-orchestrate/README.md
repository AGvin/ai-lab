# IBM watsonx Orchestrate

IBM watsonx Orchestrate is IBM's producer-operated agentic platform combining agent operations, orchestration, governance, optimization, catalog and discovery, and builder surfaces around a centralized enterprise control plane.

## Service boundary

The product is centered on organization-wide control of agents across internal and external sources while retaining first-class agent-building and multi-agent orchestration capabilities. IBM watsonx.governance remains a separate canonical service focused on governance and compliance lifecycle management.

Cloud and region availability, feature-level GA or preview state, integrations, supported external frameworks and protocols, catalog contents, pricing, and plan eligibility are freshness-sensitive.
