# Keygraph

Keygraph is Keygraph's commercial application-security platform for continuous software-security analysis, exploit-validated testing, findings management, remediation, and verification. It is represented as a **hosted development-security service**, distinct from the open-source Shannon agent.

## Relationship to Shannon

[Shannon](../../../../../../software/sub/agents/sub/local/sub/shannon/) is the self-run open-source white-box pentesting agent. Keygraph builds a broader platform around related agentic testing with additional analysis, continuous operation, findings lifecycle, integrations, remediation/verification, and commercial deployment/coverage options.

Do not treat Shannon as a free edition of the entire Keygraph platform, and do not infer current plan packaging or black-box coverage from legacy pricing text. Verify current editions before purchasing or standardizing a deployment.

## Safe-use boundary

Keygraph can perform active security testing and work with source code, applications, credentials, findings, integrations, and remediation changes. Use it only on systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test; prefer isolated staging/test targets, dedicated credentials, least privilege, recoverable data, and human review before production changes.
