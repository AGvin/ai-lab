# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Pinecone as a producer-operated vector/search data-infrastructure service for production AI applications.
- Keep embeddings, reranking, hybrid retrieval, filtering, and related inference helpers as Pinecone product capabilities rather than separate canonical entities unless an independently durable product identity emerges.
- Keep general database comparisons and selection guidance outside this factual entity profile.
- Treat pricing, regions, limits, supported hosted models, and performance claims as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- Pinecone remains classified under managed vector/search infrastructure rather than model APIs, web-data, or memory/context.
- Producer provenance resolves to Pinecone.
