# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Labelbox as a producer-operated AI data-development and curation platform covering expert labeling and related data-production workflows.
- Keep RLHF, preference data, evaluation-data workflows, review, and quality controls as parts of the same data-production lifecycle when they do not form independent product identities.
- Distinguish persisted working datasets/annotations from live application retrieval/context state.
- Treat current product packaging, expert-network offerings, pricing, supported modalities, and workflow limits as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- Labelbox remains classified under data generation/curation rather than evaluation/observability or data infrastructure.
- Producer provenance resolves to Labelbox.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-06

- Labelbox's official materials present a data-centric AI platform and expert services for multimodal annotations, LLM prompt-response pair production, human preference ratings, supervised fine-tuning data, RLHF and evaluation. Expert network/Alignerr Connect, model-comparison and quality review are connected data-production workflows, not automatically new canonical software or a standalone model research benchmark.
- The provider describes customizable ontologies, multiple annotation and consensus/review stages, label error inspection, model-assisted annotation and data quality measurement. Different annotation modalities and specialist staffing are not interchangeable quality guarantees and must be tested against a project's actual rubrics.
- User permissions, labeling workforce access, private/regulated dataset handling, pricing, ownership/rights for training labels and model output, and compliance depend on current contracts and configurations. Publisher annotation counts and customer gains are not independently validated.

- Verified first-party source 3: https://labelbox.com/guides/how-to-evaluate-and-optimize-your-data-labeling-projects-results/
- Acceptance credits historical or current service identity and publisher-scoped data creation/handling boundaries only. It does not establish current Gretel hosting, redistributable data/model rights, new identity/metadata, independent privacy/compliance/security checks, full freshness, whole-repo schema/cache/CI/render, reader repair, clean pass, final delivery or merge.
