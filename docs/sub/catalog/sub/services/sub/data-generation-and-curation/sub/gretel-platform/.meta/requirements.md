# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Gretel Platform as the historical synthetic-data service developed by Gretel, preserving NVIDIA's 2025 acquisition provenance without rewriting original product authorship.
- Separate acquired business lineage from current hosted-service availability and from legal ownership of every associated software, model, or dataset artifact.
- Record the 2026-10-20 source boundary: the two former Gretel service pages return 404 and the Gretel homepage redirects to NVIDIA's synthetic-data use-case page. Neither observation alone proves a formal product shutdown or continued availability of the former service.
- Do not present Gretel Cloud, Gretel Hybrid, historical modules, cloud support, connectors, or API behavior as current capabilities without current first-party service evidence. Retain the access/operation question: identify the current contracting operator, control plane, customer-run data plane, metadata flows, and support terms for any available deployment.
- Do not infer that a customer-hosted data plane makes a service wholly standalone, or that a website redirect makes NVIDIA NeMo products identical to the former Gretel service.
- Keep historical Data Designer, Safe Synthetics, Navigator, Evaluate, Workflows, SDK/CLI, connectors, and model names within their evidenced product and release scopes; acquisition and shared terminology do not establish current migration or compatibility.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.
- Render the `official-resources` block using canonical sources supporting the stated historical provenance and current access boundary; unavailable legacy pages are not presented as current service documentation.

## Validation

- Gretel retains its synthetic-data service identity and historical authorship; no new entity or taxonomy is inferred.
- Current hosting, deployment, availability, and shutdown claims are limited to what the retrieved first-party evidence establishes.
- Current NVIDIA synthetic-data offerings are not silently substituted for the former Gretel platform.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to NVIDIA.
