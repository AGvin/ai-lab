# Salesforce Agentforce

Salesforce Agentforce is Salesforce's producer-operated AI-agent platform for building, testing, deploying, managing, and orchestrating agents against Salesforce data, metadata, automation, APIs, channels, and business logic.

## Service boundary

Role- and industry-specific agents remain packaged agents within the platform unless an independently durable product boundary is selected. Salesforce Data 360 or Data Cloud, MuleSoft, Slack, Salesforce applications, AgentExchange assets, individual agents, and underlying model-provider integrations remain separate identities or integrations.

Agent catalogs, entitlements, licensing, branding and version labels, builder and runtime features, observability, supported protocols and channels, and component preview or GA status are freshness-sensitive.
