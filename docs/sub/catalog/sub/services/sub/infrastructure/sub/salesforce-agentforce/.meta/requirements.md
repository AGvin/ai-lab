# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Salesforce Agentforce as Salesforce's producer-operated AI-agent platform for building, testing, deploying, managing, and orchestrating agents against Salesforce data, metadata, automation, APIs, channels, and business logic.
- Preserve the platform identity rather than materializing every role/industry agent (Sales, Service, Commerce, Operations, Setup, and similar packaged agents) as a new peer service unless an independently durable product boundary is later selected.
- Keep Salesforce Data 360/Data Cloud, MuleSoft, Slack, Salesforce applications, AgentExchange assets, individual agents, and underlying model/provider integrations separate from the platform identity.
- Treat exact agent catalog, included entitlements, licensing, Agentforce branding/version labels, builder/runtime features, observability, supported protocols/channels, and GA/preview state of components as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- Agentforce remains a hosted agent-platform identity rather than a single assistant, model, or application-specific agent.
- Component-level GA claims are not generalized to all Agentforce capabilities.
- Producer provenance resolves to Salesforce.
