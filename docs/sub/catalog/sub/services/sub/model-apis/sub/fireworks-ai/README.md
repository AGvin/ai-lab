# Fireworks AI

Fireworks AI is Fireworks.ai, Inc.'s hosted inference and model-serving service, with serverless inference and dedicated deployment surfaces.

## Service boundary

Serverless and dedicated deployments remain distinct service modes with different availability, isolation, model-customization, performance, and service-level characteristics. Third-party model identity, licensing, lifecycle, and intrinsic capabilities remain owned by their model authors rather than Fireworks AI merely because the service hosts them.

Current first-party policy describes model-input and training-data use as opt-in and documents zero-data-retention behavior for open models with exceptions. Exact model inventory, pricing, deployment hardware, service levels, retention exceptions, throughput, and other hosted state are freshness-sensitive.
