# Valyu

Valyu is a hosted retrieval and cited research service spanning open-web information and specialized or permissioned third-party data sources.

## Retrieval and content boundary

Search retrieves source results, Contents extracts source material, Answer produces synthesized responses and DeepResearch produces longer research outputs. These workflows belong to one platform rather than separate endpoint-level products.

Source coverage and licensed access, rights to full text and derived outputs, citation use, retention and processing terms depend on the exact source and customer agreement. Sensitive inputs need an appropriate processing arrangement. Valyu is the service operator, not the author of underlying sources; vendor benchmark rankings require the stated task set, version and rubric before comparison.
