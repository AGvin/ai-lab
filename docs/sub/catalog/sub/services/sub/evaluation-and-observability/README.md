# Evaluation and Observability Services

Producer-operated platforms whose primary identity is tracing, inspecting, evaluating, measuring, or improving AI applications and agents without a complete independently self-managed product identity.

Installable or fully self-hostable observability and evaluation products remain under the software catalog. Hybrid products are classified by durable control-plane ownership rather than by the mere presence of a customer-hosted collector or data plane.
