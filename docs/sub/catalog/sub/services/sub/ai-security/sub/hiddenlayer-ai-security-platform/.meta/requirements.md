# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present HiddenLayer AI Security Platform as a purpose-built AI-security service spanning AI discovery, model/supply-chain security, runtime protection, and attack simulation.
- Keep governance/posture capabilities secondary while threat protection and AI-security operations remain the durable product center.
- Treat platform modules, deployment options, detections, integrations, availability, and commercial claims as freshness-sensitive.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- The entity remains security-first rather than governance-, identity-, or evaluation-first.
- Producer provenance resolves to HiddenLayer.
