# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present `ai-security/` as the canonical service owner for producer-operated products whose primary identity is protecting AI models, applications, agents, data flows, or AI supply chains against adversarial, abuse, integrity, or runtime security threats.
- Allow model/supply-chain scanning, runtime/prompt/agent protection, prompt-injection/jailbreak defense, agent/MCP security, posture discovery, attack simulation/red teaming, and threat detection/response when they are part of a security-first product identity.
- Exclude evaluation/red-team products whose primary role is testing, generic gateways with optional guardrails, governance/compliance-first platforms, identity/auth-first products, and generic cybersecurity suites without a durable AI-specific product identity.
- Treat acquisition/rebranding as current product-identity/provenance maintenance; do not preserve retired commercial service identities solely for history.

## Validation

- Children are AI-security-first service identities rather than adjacent governance, identity, evaluation, or gateway products.
- Current identity/provenance is source-backed when acquisitions or product absorption affect naming.
- Navigation matches validated materialized direct children.
