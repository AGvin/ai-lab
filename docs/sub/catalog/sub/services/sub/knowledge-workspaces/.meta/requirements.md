# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present `knowledge-workspaces/` as the services catalog index for hosted products whose primary work object is a durable source set, notebook, knowledge corpus, or governed knowledge base.
- Render the standard child-navigation block from the validated direct-child projection so every materialized canonical knowledge-workspace service appears exactly once.
- Keep hosted general conversational assistants under `assistant-workspaces/` even when they support files, projects, research, memory, or connectors.
- Keep distinct installable/self-managed knowledge-workspace products under the software catalog.
- Do not create standalone service entities for embedded capabilities of broader parent products unless an independently verified product/service identity exists.

## Validation

- The child-navigation block matches the validated materialized direct-child projection and every destination resolves to a canonical hosted service profile.
- Every child is source/corpus/notebook/knowledge-base-first rather than merely having knowledge features.
- The category does not duplicate general assistant or installable software identities.
