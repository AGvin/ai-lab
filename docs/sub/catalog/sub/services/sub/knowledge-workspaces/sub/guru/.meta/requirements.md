# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Guru as a hosted AI knowledge platform centered on a governed enterprise knowledge layer rather than a general-purpose assistant workspace.
- Preserve its core source/knowledge behavior: connected enterprise sources, unified knowledge indexing, permission-aware retrieval, governed knowledge quality, search, cited answers, and knowledge delivery to people and AI tools.
- Keep broader action/agent capabilities subordinate to the knowledge-layer product identity instead of reclassifying Guru solely because it can act in connected systems.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep connector counts, pricing, plans, and other mutable commercial/platform details source-backed when expanded.

## Validation

- The page presents governed organizational knowledge as Guru's primary work object.
- The service is not collapsed into Guru Technologies, Inc. or into a generic enterprise-search capability.
- The `entity-relations` block resolves to the canonical Guru Technologies, Inc. producer.
- Official Guru references match canonical entity metadata.
