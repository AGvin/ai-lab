# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Tettra as a hosted AI-powered internal knowledge-base and knowledge-management service.
- Preserve the knowledge base as the primary work object, including creating/importing documentation, AI-assisted search and answers, reusable Q&A, content verification, curation, and keeping organizational knowledge current.
- Keep the Kai assistant as a capability within Tettra rather than a separate product entity unless an independently verified product boundary emerges.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep pricing, integration coverage, AI feature limits, and other mutable details source-backed when expanded.

## Validation

- The page presents durable internal knowledge management as Tettra's primary product identity.
- Tettra is not collapsed into Tettra, Inc. or reduced to the Kai assistant capability.
- The `entity-relations` block resolves to the canonical Tettra, Inc. producer.
- Official Tettra references match canonical entity metadata.
