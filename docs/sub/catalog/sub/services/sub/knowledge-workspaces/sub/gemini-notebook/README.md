# Gemini Notebook

Gemini Notebook is Google's standalone AI-powered research and knowledge workspace, renamed from NotebookLM on July 16, 2026.

## Product boundary

The notebook and its persistent collection of project sources are the primary work object. Source-grounded chat, inline citations, notes, and source-derived artifacts remain core behavior. `NotebookLM` is the official former name rather than a separate product entity, and Gemini Notebook remains distinct from the broader Gemini assistant-workspace service.

Limits, plan availability, supported source types, and feature rollout details are freshness-sensitive.
