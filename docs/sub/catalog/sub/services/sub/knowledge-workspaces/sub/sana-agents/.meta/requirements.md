# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Sana Agents as Sana Labs AB's hosted enterprise AI-agent and knowledge workspace grounded in an organization's connected knowledge and data sources.
- Preserve its source-first knowledge behavior: enterprise integrations, synchronized company data, permission-aware access, enterprise search, and expert assistants/agents grounded in selected organizational knowledge.
- Acknowledge current multi-step agent, analysis, action, and content-generation capabilities without treating them as evidence that the source/knowledge workspace boundary is absent.
- Keep Sana Agents distinct from Sana Learn and from Sana Labs AB as the producer organization.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep pricing, connector counts, model availability, security certifications, and other mutable operational details source-backed when expanded.

## Validation

- The page presents company knowledge/data grounding as a defining product object while accurately acknowledging agentic breadth.
- Sana Agents, Sana Learn, and Sana Labs AB remain separate identities.
- The `entity-relations` block resolves to the canonical Sana Labs AB producer.
- Official Sana references match canonical entity metadata.
