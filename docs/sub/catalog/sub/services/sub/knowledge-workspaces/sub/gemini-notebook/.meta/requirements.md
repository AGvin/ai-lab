# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Gemini Notebook as Google's standalone AI-powered research and knowledge workspace, renamed from NotebookLM on 2026-07-16.
- Treat the notebook and its persistent collection of project sources as the primary work object; preserve source-grounded chat, inline citations, notes, and source-derived artifacts as core knowledge-workspace behavior.
- Preserve `NotebookLM` as the official former name without creating a duplicate product entity.
- Keep Gemini Notebook distinct from the broader Gemini assistant-workspace service even though notebooks can sync into Gemini apps and Google Search.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep mutable limits, plan availability, supported source types, and feature rollout details source-backed when expanded.

## Validation

- The page presents Gemini Notebook as a standalone source-grounded research product rather than a Gemini assistant feature or model identity.
- The 2026-07-16 rename is represented without implying a product discontinuity.
- The `entity-relations` block resolves to the canonical Google producer.
- Official Google references match canonical entity metadata.
