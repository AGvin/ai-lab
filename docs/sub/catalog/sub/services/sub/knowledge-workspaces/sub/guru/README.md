# Guru

Guru is a hosted AI knowledge platform centered on a governed enterprise knowledge layer.

## Product boundary

Guru connects and indexes enterprise sources for permission-aware retrieval, governed knowledge quality, search, cited answers, and knowledge delivery to people and AI tools. Broader action and agent capabilities remain subordinate to that knowledge-layer identity rather than turning Guru into a general assistant workspace.

Connector counts, pricing, plans, and other mutable commercial or platform details are freshness-sensitive.
