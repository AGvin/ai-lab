# Services

Canonical profiles for hosted or externally operated products whose primary identity is a service.

Installable or self-managed products belong under [`software/`](../software/). Durable model identity and model facts remain under Model Reference rather than being duplicated as service facts.

## Service categories

- [Agent Identity and Access](./sub/agent-identity-and-access/) — agent authentication, identity lifecycle, delegated authorization, scoped credential issuance, and access control.
- [Agent Integration Platforms](./sub/agent-integration-platforms/) — managed tool and application access for AI agents and applications.
- [AI Assets](./sub/ai-assets/) — hosted discovery, publishing, versioning, and collaboration around models, datasets, applications, and related AI assets.
- [AI Management Platforms](./sub/ai-management-platforms/) — organization-wide AI inventory, governance, policy, access, cost, risk, compliance, and administration.
- [AI Security](./sub/ai-security/) — protection of models, applications, agents, data flows, and AI supply chains.
- [Assistant Workspaces](./sub/assistant-workspaces/) — hosted conversational assistants and persistent workspaces.
- [Benchmarking](./sub/benchmarking/) — hosted benchmarking and public evaluation resources.
- [Data Generation and Curation](./sub/data-generation-and-curation/) — services for creating, labeling, curating, transforming, synthesizing, and reviewing AI data.
- [Data Infrastructure](./sub/data-infrastructure/) — producer-operated infrastructure for persistent operational AI data state.
- [Development](./sub/development/) — hosted development products, agent services, and development-security workflows.
- [Evaluation and Observability](./sub/evaluation-and-observability/) — tracing, inspection, evaluation, measurement, and improvement of AI applications and agents.
- [Gateways](./sub/gateways/) — hosted routing, governance, observability, and brokering of AI or API requests.
- [Infrastructure](./sub/infrastructure/) — hosted compute, deployment, and application-running services.
- [Knowledge Workspaces](./sub/knowledge-workspaces/) — hosted products centered on durable source sets, notebooks, knowledge corpora, or governed knowledge bases.
- [Model APIs](./sub/model-apis/) — remote API access to models, providers, and model-routing backends.
- [Web Data](./sub/web-data/) — web search, retrieval, extraction, crawling, mapping, research, and browser-derived data services.

Concrete service pages own provider/product-specific facts. Mutable availability, pricing, aliases, limits, terms, and API behavior require current source verification at the concrete node.
