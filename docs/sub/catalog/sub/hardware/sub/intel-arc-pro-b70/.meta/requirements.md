# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Intel Arc Pro B70 as Intel's launched professional discrete GPU based on the Xe2/Battlemage architecture, with AI/compute support including Intel XMX, oneAPI, OpenVINO, and Intel Extension for PyTorch.
- Keep workstation/professional GPU identity separate from Intel Gaudi accelerators, integrated Intel GPUs/NPUs, client CPUs, and complete workstation/server products.
- Treat memory, bandwidth, clocks, power limits, driver support, OS/runtime compatibility, AI framework support, pricing, and measured workload performance as freshness-sensitive.
- Do not generalize graphics or vendor TOPS figures into model-serving performance without exact runtime/model/precision evidence.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- Intel product status remains traceable to the first-party page as launched.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to Intel.
