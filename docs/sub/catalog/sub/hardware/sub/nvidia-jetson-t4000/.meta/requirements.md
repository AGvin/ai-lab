- Identify NVIDIA Jetson T4000 as the released 64 GB Blackwell-based Jetson Thor production module, announced as available on 2026-01-05 for physical-AI, robotics, and edge-AI deployment.

- Attribute NVIDIA efficiency/performance comparisons to NVIDIA and preserve the exact software/power/workload conditions.
