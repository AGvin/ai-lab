# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Qualcomm Dragonfly AI200 as Qualcomm's active rack-scale AI-inference accelerator product for generative and agentic AI workloads.
- Preserve current lifecycle exactly: the first-party product catalog/page marks AI200 Active and exposes Contact Sales; do not infer broader unrestricted availability from that state.
- Keep the AI200 accelerator/card identity distinct from complete rack configurations, future Dragonfly AI250/AI300 generations, Cloud AI100 Ultra, Dragonfly C1000 CPUs, and software/runtime layers.
- Treat card/rack memory, bandwidth, power/cooling, interconnect, supported model scales, software/runtime compatibility, commercial availability, pricing, and measured performance as freshness-sensitive.
- Attribute Qualcomm comparative efficiency/TCO claims to Qualcomm rather than treating them as neutral benchmark evidence.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.
