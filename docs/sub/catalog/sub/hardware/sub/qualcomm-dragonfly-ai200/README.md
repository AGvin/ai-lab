# Qualcomm Dragonfly AI200

Qualcomm Dragonfly AI200 is Qualcomm's data-center AI-inference accelerator product for generative and agentic workloads. The official product listing marks it Active and offers Contact Sales. The accelerator/card identity is separate from a complete rack configuration, other Dragonfly generations, CPU products and the software needed to run models. [AI200 product page](https://www.qualcomm.com/data-center/products/qualcomm-dragonfly-ai200) · [Data-center catalog](https://www.qualcomm.com/data-center/products).

## Scope and operating boundaries

Evaluate the offered hardware configuration against the intended model and workload. Relevant decisions include card and rack memory, interconnect, power and cooling, supported software/runtime versions and the model execution path. Product status and a sales contact do not establish unrestricted availability, inventory or a particular delivery date. [AI200 product page](https://www.qualcomm.com/data-center/products/qualcomm-dragonfly-ai200).

Keep Qualcomm's efficiency and total-cost comparisons tied to their stated assumptions. A vendor comparison is not an independent benchmark of the buyer's workload; request the exact configuration and validate it before procurement. Specifications, commercial terms and platform compatibility should be checked at the time of purchase. [AI200 product page](https://www.qualcomm.com/data-center/products/qualcomm-dragonfly-ai200).
