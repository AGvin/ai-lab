# NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Ti

NVIDIA Blackwell GeForce RTX 5070 Ti, distinct from the non-Ti RTX 5070 on the same family comparison page.

## Manufacturer specifications

- 16 GB GDDR7, 256-bit memory interface.
- NVIDIA specifies 8,960 CUDA cores and fifth-generation Tensor Cores.
- PCIe Gen 5 support; exact power and dimensions depend on partner board.

## AI workload and deployment boundaries

Keep Ti and non-Ti specifications in separate columns. Advertised AI TOPS are not measured serving latency; VRAM is local and not automatically pooled across cards.

The listed specifications are manufacturer-published claims reviewed on 2026-11-05. They are not independent AI workload benchmarks, purchase availability checks or full driver compatibility testing.
