# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present NVIDIA DGX Spark as NVIDIA's released compact personal AI supercomputer based on the Grace Blackwell architecture for local AI development, inference, fine-tuning, agentic workloads, and desktop-scale experimentation.
- Preserve the durable product boundary separately from partner-branded systems, DGX Station, DGX Cloud, discrete NVIDIA GPUs, and the broader DGX software/services portfolio.
- Treat unified-memory capacity, exact CPU/GPU specifications, model-size guidance, networking, DGX OS/software bundle, partner availability, price, shipping regions, performance, and clustering behavior as freshness-sensitive.
- Treat the announced 64GB configuration as a configuration/SKU of DGX Spark rather than a separate product identity; preserve its October 23, 2026 availability boundary and do not imply current availability before that date.
- Attribute model-size/performance claims to NVIDIA and preserve model/runtime/precision conditions where cited.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- DGX Spark remains a complete hardware-system identity rather than a GPU/SoC identity.
- Future configurations are not misreported as already shipping.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to NVIDIA.
