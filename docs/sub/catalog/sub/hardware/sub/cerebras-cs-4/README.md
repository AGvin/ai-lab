## Exact product scope

Cerebras CS-4 is an August 2026 rack-scale system comprising three WSE-3 Turbo wafer processors. The manufacturer's 750 PFLOPS compute, 129.6 PB/s wafer-memory bandwidth and 7.2 Tb/s I/O refer to the combined three-wafer system, not a single processor. Launch language promised initial shipments in Q3 2026, not verified general availability. Cerebras' speed comparisons are model-, context-, precision- and serving-configuration-dependent; no independent performance or procurement clearance is inferred.

- Produced by [Cerebras Systems Inc.](../../../producers/sub/c/sub/cerebras-systems-inc/).
