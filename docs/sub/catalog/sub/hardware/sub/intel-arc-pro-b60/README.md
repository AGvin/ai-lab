# Intel Arc Pro B60

Intel Xe2/Battlemage workstation discrete GPU, separate from B50 and the newer 32 GB B65.

## Manufacturer specifications

- 24 GB GDDR6, 192-bit memory bus, up to 456 GB/s bandwidth.
- 20 Xe-cores and 160 XMX engines.
- PCIe 5.0 x8; 200 W nominal board power with lower-power board variants.

## AI workload and deployment boundaries

Do not substitute B65 memory or PCIe figures. Real throughput depends on workload, firmware, cooling and supported software. Multiple discrete GPUs do not automatically pool VRAM.

The listed specifications are manufacturer-published claims reviewed on 2026-11-05. They are not independent AI workload benchmarks, purchase availability checks or full driver compatibility testing.
