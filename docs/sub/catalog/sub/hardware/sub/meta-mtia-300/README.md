# Meta MTIA 300

Meta MTIA 300 is a production generation of Meta Training and Inference Accelerator hardware documented by Meta in March 2026.

## Hardware boundary

MTIA 300 initially targets ranking and recommendation training and remains a concrete hardware product rather than a generic accelerator concept. Later MTIA 400, 450, and 500 roadmap or test generations are not implicitly stable production catalog entries; planned or scheduled deployment is not equivalent to current production status.

Hardware specifications, rack or network topology, software-stack compatibility, workload coverage, deployment scale, and performance comparisons are freshness-sensitive.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-06

Meta states MTIA 300 is in production for ranking/recommendation training, distinct from later MTIA 400/450/500 and their GenAI-oriented claims. Its first-party August 2026 technical report describes 216 GB HBM3E, integrated NIC chiplets and 16 offload message engines; Meta's interconnect and benchmark comparisons are its own workload-specific findings, not an independent general LLM training result.
