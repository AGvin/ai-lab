# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present NVIDIA DGX Station as NVIDIA's current deskside AI supercomputer based on the GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra Desktop Superchip for local development, fine-tuning, inference, and long-running agentic workloads.
- Preserve the complete-system boundary separately from DGX Spark, DGX data-center systems, DGX Cloud, discrete GPUs, GB300/B300 accelerator identities, and partner/OEM implementations.
- Preserve lifecycle accurately: current NVIDIA product material exposes DGX Station as orderable through OEM partners; do not conflate that with the separate DGX Station for Windows configuration announced for Q4 2026.
- Treat coherent-memory capacity, compute figures, networking, local model-scale guidance, supported OS/software stack, OEM configurations, availability, price, power, storage, optional RTX Pro GPU configurations, and multi-system scaling as freshness-sensitive.
- Attribute performance/model-capacity claims to NVIDIA and preserve model, precision, sparsity, and system-configuration conditions.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- DGX Station remains a complete deskside hardware-system identity rather than a GB300 chip or OEM workstation identity.
- Future Windows-specific availability is not misreported as already generally available.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to NVIDIA.
