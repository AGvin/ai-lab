# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Jalapeño as OpenAI's first Intelligence Processor, an LLM-inference accelerator co-developed with Broadcom and publicly unveiled in June 2026 after first-generation silicon was delivered to OpenAI leadership.
- Preserve OpenAI as the canonical product producer identity while documenting Broadcom's co-development/manufacturing role as source-backed provenance rather than inventing an unsupported symmetric producer relation.
- Keep early performance-per-watt claims explicitly vendor-reported and avoid treating planned gigawatt-scale deployment as current installed capacity.
- Treat future generations, deployment scale, performance figures, process/manufacturing details, and availability as mutable source-scoped facts.

## Validation

- The delivered/unveiled accelerator is not described as merely an announced future concept, but planned future deployment is not represented as already completed.
- Vendor performance claims are not converted into neutral cross-vendor benchmark conclusions.
- The accelerator is not conflated with complete OpenAI data-center systems or hosted inference services.
