# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present `hardware/` as the canonical catalog owner for concrete hardware used, evaluated, or referenced by AI Lab documentation.
- Cover stable hardware identities such as compute systems, accelerators, processors, modules, storage, networking, power, cooling, and other materially relevant components when concrete profiles are justified.
- Keep durable source-backed hardware facts with concrete hardware profiles; workload-specific fit, recommendations, benchmarks, and deployment guidance belong to their appropriate decision-support or evidence owners and should link back to canonical hardware identities.
- Keep the index honest about current materialization; do not imply a concrete hardware inventory exists when none is materialized.

## Validation

- The page contains no temporary-placeholder or RC wording.
- The page does not claim a concrete hardware inventory that is not materialized.
- Future listed hardware links resolve to canonical hardware nodes.
