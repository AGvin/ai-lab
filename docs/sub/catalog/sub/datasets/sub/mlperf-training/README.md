# MLPerf Training

## Relations

- Produced by: [MLCommons](../../../producers/sub/m/sub/mlcommons/)

MLPerf Training is MLCommons' open, peer-reviewed benchmark suite for measuring how quickly complete systems train models to specified quality targets.

## Scope and boundaries

The June 2026 v6.0 release covers a changing collection of training workloads, including language-model pretraining/fine-tuning, recommendation and image generation. Reference models and datasets, target quality, software and hardware configuration, rules and power measurements belong to the particular revision.

The suite is distinct from individual models, accelerators, frameworks, cloud platforms and vendor submissions. A performance record applies to the exact submitted system, workload and quality target; it is not a universal ranking of training systems or a claim that MLCommons produced the models used in the tests.
