# AIR-Bench 2024

**AIR-Bench 2024** — safety prompts across structured risk categories.

## Data and evaluation boundary

The first-party release reports 5,694 prompts, with risk labels from a four-tier taxonomy derived from eight government regulations and sixteen company policies. The benchmark dataset, HELM integration, policy sources and scoring implementations are separate artifacts; taxonomy alignment does not demonstrate legal or regulatory compliance.

The [pinned first-party source](https://github.com/stanford-crfm/air-bench-2024/blob/6d1d4a24d8350e4360e5763463f10233efa4b2a2/README.md) establishes the identity and release scope. Related model outputs, scoring programs, downloaded task materials and historical leaderboard measurements remain separate artifacts.

## Rights, limitations and safety

The pinned Apache-2.0 repository license applies only to material within its grant. Original policy documents, sensitive prompts, hosted dataset access and evaluator output may have separate terms. Do not treat policy-category labels as legal advice or automatically validated compliance.

No benchmark datasets were downloaded and no evaluator, model, untrusted code, harmful safety prompt or physical experiment was executed. No full rights audit or repository-wide CI was run.
