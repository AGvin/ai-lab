# FailSafeQA

**FailSafeQA** — Long-context financial QA robustness benchmark of 220 items, including incomplete, out-of-domain and erroneous query conditions.

## Data and evaluation boundary

The publisher card reports 220 benchmark records and fields for full contexts, OCR contexts, answers, normal queries, incomplete queries, out-of-domain queries, error queries and out-of-scope queries. Its task records, underlying source documents, OCR conversion, model answers and grading are separate.

The [original publisher dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Writer/FailSafeQA) was checked on 2026-11-05 (publisher last modified 2025-02-13). An immutable HF revision SHA was not available from connected metadata; the research reference below provides additional first-party identification, not a license for unrelated material.

## Rights, limitations and safety

The publisher lists MIT on the dataset card, but original financial document/source rights and model-generated outputs may have additional conditions. Evaluation outcomes do not establish investment advice, production safety or verified financial facts.

**Access:** public. **Publisher-listed license:** MIT (HF metadata). No records, models, generated code, audio, images or evaluators were downloaded/executed. No complete rights audit or scientific reproduction was performed.
