- No root LICENSE verified at the pinned commit; do not infer distribution, commercial reuse or derivative rights.
- Handle stereotype-related prompts sensitively, avoid extrapolating social group claims and account for training contamination.
- No code run, test download, bias score reproduction, dataset rights audit or whole-catalog clean pass performed.
