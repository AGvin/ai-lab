# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify AgentHarm as the **2024 Gray Swan AI / UK AI Safety Institute jointly developed benchmark** for whether tool-using LLM agents comply with harmful requests, maintain multi-step tool-use capabilities after attacks, and refuse appropriately. It is a concrete released evaluation dataset rather than a jailbreak technique, tool-harness implementation, active attack agent, managed service, or proof of overall system safety.
- Scope counts precisely. The original paper describes **110 harmful base behaviors, 440 augmented behavior cases, 11 harm categories**, with designated validation/public/private splits. The AISI-hosted public release discloses **44 of 66 public-test base behaviors (176 augmented cases)** and **8 of 11 validation base behaviors (32 augmented cases)**; do not equate original research scope, available harmful dataset slices, paired benign/control cases, separate chat view, and current Hub all-config row totals. Data and scoring revision must be pinned for each evaluation.
- Preserve original public benchmark identity including harmful and matched benign behaviors (to measure over-refusal), limited available tool functions, task-specific grading criteria, and a chat-only subset; these are not additional independently accepted catalog datasets. Distinguish already existing AgentDojo prompt-injection workflows (legitimate tasks attacked through untrusted tool/response input) and MLCommons AILuminate chatbot/system safety evaluations from AgentHarm's multi-step agent misuse focus.
- Retain **two original co-contributor identities** in reader prose: Gray Swan AI research and UK AI Safety Institute (renamed UK AI Security Institute). The existing institute producer receives one supported `produced-by` inverse; that relation does not claim sole authorship or fully established ownership of Gray Swan AI. No independent Gray Swan organization record is minted in this bounded cohort entry; its legal/corporate producer identity and ultimate ownership remain separately reviewable.
- Quote legal scope accurately: the original [publisher LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai-safety-institute/AgentHarm/blob/69e7a23c331a75d4e438a65233a4f8b459fe95c7/LICENSE) is **MIT wording plus an additional clause that prohibits uses other than improving AI safety and security**; Hub metadata says `license: other`. It is **not unrestricted MIT**, not a general-purpose commercial data grant and not interchangeable with the separately standard-MIT Inspect Evals software repository license.
- Preserve original source author attribution and the 2024 paper / ICLR 2025 context. The [original publisher card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai-safety-institute/AgentHarm) warns against using benchmark examples for training, includes a canary contamination marker, withholds a research jailbreak template, and cautions that benchmark grades do not establish general agent safety. Do not copy harmful prompt examples, operational attacks or withheld attack templates into this catalog.
- Scoring is version-sensitive. The maintained [Inspect Evals AgentHarm](https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_evals/tree/main/src/inspect_evals/agentharm) docs record 2026 grading fixes across versions **3-B through 8-B**, while labeling **6-B** its recommended version pending a broader review. Different judge model, task split, tool-call success, refusal detection, scoring rules, attack prompt, model, agent or test environment can change grades and invalidate cross-run comparisons; no benchmark was run or results reproduced here.
- Explicit safe use: malicious prompts/tools may represent fraud, harassment or cybercrime scenarios; use benign/synthetic test accounts and sandboxed tool mocks, isolate evaluation runtime and logs, limit credentials/network/model API spend, and prevent real-world execution, exfiltration or publication of unsafe completions. No payloads, agent jobs, or grader scripts were executed in this review. No comprehensive harm-content audit, legal suitability opinion, or ultimate-beneficial-ownership assessment is claimed.

## Validation

- Exactly one new `catalog/datasets/agentharm` original dataset node, with no new producer, harness, chat split, benign split, grading version, attack template or unrelated dataset acceptance.
- Reciprocal `catalog/datasets/agentharm` `produced-by` / `catalog/producers/u/uk-ai-security-institute` `produces` links resolve; Gray Swan's joint research attribution and separately reviewable producer identity are not suppressed.
- All new reader references and parent index navigation point to materialized canonical pages. No claims of complete source-dataset payload verification, unrestricted data rights, independent model execution, global schema/cache/render/CI, or clean full-eight-root research passes.
