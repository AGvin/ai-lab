# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- MedQA is a medical exam QA dataset, not a medical diagnosis service or clinical certification.
- The pinned official README identifies question banks from the US, Mainland China and Taiwan, JSONL train/dev/test partitions, and optional four-choice question variants.
- External English and Chinese textbook downloads and retrieval baseline implementations are separate resources, not additional MedQA benchmark identities.
- Specify regional source, language, split, choice format, retrieval setup, model revision and scorer for reproducible comparisons.
- The project root MIT LICENSE does not automatically grant rights to original exam questions, textbooks or hosted Google Drive data.
- Do not treat medical-exam accuracy as clinical safety, diagnosis or therapeutic guidance; no linked data download, model run, complete rights audit or clean pass was performed.

## Validation

- A unique frozen source-backed dataset identity and correctly indexed canonical profile.
- Source-data rights, code, scores and model services remain distinct; unperformed tests receive no acceptance credit.
