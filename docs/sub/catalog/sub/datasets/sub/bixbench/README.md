# BixBench

**BixBench** — Computational biology benchmark derived from published research notebooks, with distinct hosted data capsules, benchmark scoring scripts, and external research source rights.

## Data and evaluation boundary

The publisher describes 205 scientific questions derived from 60 published Jupyter notebooks and related data capsules. Tasks require multi-step analysis of biological datasets, computational tools and interpretation of research outcomes, with open-ended and multiple-choice answer formats.
The [pinned first-party README](https://github.com/Future-House/BixBench/blob/49311180bdacb324c596f2e07596c126f2004008/README.md) is the primary source; the dataset is distinct from model services and result leaderboards.

## Rights, limitations and safety

Apache-2.0 covers source material within its grant, not necessarily the notebooks' datasets, papers or downloaded capsules. Executing agent-generated Python, R or Bash requires strict sandboxing and compute/resource limits. Benchmark results do not validate wet-lab scientific procedures.

No benchmark datasets were downloaded and no models, generated programs or full repository CI were executed.
