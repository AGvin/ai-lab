# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Vending-Bench 2 as Andon Labs' current benchmark for long-term coherence in AI agents through a year-long simulated vending-machine business.
- Preserve Vending-Bench 2 as the successor to deprecated Vending-Bench 1 rather than materializing the older benchmark as the current canonical peer.
- Keep simulation rules, tool inventory, run duration, scoring, leaderboard models/results, baseline behavior, arena variant, and environment revisions freshness-sensitive.
- Keep Vending-Bench Arena as a multi-agent variant of the current benchmark unless its lifecycle becomes independently useful enough for a separate canonical node.
- Keep real-world Andon Market/Café/FM experiments separate from the benchmark identity.
- Attribute business/alignment observations to the exact benchmark/run/model revision rather than generalizing them into universal economic-agent behavior.

## Validation

- The node represents Vending-Bench 2, not deprecated Vending-Bench 1 or a real vending business.
- Live leaderboard results are not frozen as durable benchmark facts.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-08

- **Vending-Bench 2** is Andon Labs' original **November 2025** successor to its deprecated Vending-Bench 1, evaluating **one year of simulated vending-machine business management** with supplier search, simulated e-mail orders, storage transfers, price/inventory decisions and seasonally varying customer demand. The publisher scores the run on **final business bank balance**, not verified real-world sales or universal business-management quality.
- The public **Vending-Bench Arena** adds competing/cooperating vendors as a variant of Vending-Bench 2 and is not a separately credited canonical dataset here. The original case typically spans thousands of messages and extremely long model outputs; prompt budget, model/provider, tool API, simulator revision, starting inventory, trial aggregation, date horizon and model-version leaderboard must match for comparisons.
- Original publisher sources: https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench-2 ; https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench .
- Source credit qualifies one already materialized simulated business-agent benchmark and successor/version boundaries; it does not authorize actual supplier payments or live transactions, independent benchmark performance claims, missing reader creation, comprehensive data/privacy/security/legal checks, whole CI/render, clean or final gate.
