# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ZeroBench as one dataset family; keep question set, image source, evaluation protocol and leaderboard distinct.
- Record whether reporting pass@1, pass@5 or pass^5, exact model/prompt/decoding and benchmark version.
- Preserve dataset canary and contamination protection; do not claim the suite remains impossible to solve.
- Verify third-party image rights separately from MIT-covered source.
- No dataset images downloaded, model scored, ranking verified, rights audit or clean pass performed.
- Ensure a unique frozen benchmark identity, pinned publisher sources and valid category link.

## Validation

- Confirm source and license boundaries; do not infer rights to hosted data or unperformed runtime verification.
