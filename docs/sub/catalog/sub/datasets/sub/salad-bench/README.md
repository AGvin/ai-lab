# SALAD-Bench

**SALAD-Bench** — hierarchical multi-category LLM safety and refusal evaluation.

## Data and evaluation boundary

The publisher reports 21k base questions across six domains and 16 tasks, with hierarchical categories and QA/multiple-choice variants. Its separate SALAD-Data distribution, MD-Judge and MC-Judge scorers, and hosted leaderboard are not synonymous with the dataset identity.

The [pinned first-party README](https://github.com/OpenSafetyLab/SALAD-BENCH/blob/575d5369c5355c0ed76e27860a889aa2f3c3e0e5/README.md) is the identity and version source. Data, benchmark tasks, evaluator software, trained models and published leaderboards must be attributed separately.

## Rights, limitations and safety

The repository has Apache-2.0 terms for covered content, while separately hosted data/models and any third-party prompt material require their own terms checks. Unsafe benchmark questions are for controlled safety research, not instruction or training corpora.

No unsafe prompts, classifier model execution, data download or live safety evaluation was performed. No complete rights audit or repository-wide validation was run.
