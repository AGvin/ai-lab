# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present ApprenticeBench as a released benchmark for end-to-end job learning that combines long-horizon computer use, persistent/continual learning across tasks, and realistic business software workflows.
- Preserve its current benchmark boundary around a realistic accounting apprenticeship/job setting and the 100-task evaluation sequence; do not generalize a single-job benchmark into proof of arbitrary occupational competence.
- Keep Codex/Claude Code harnesses, CUA tools, memory-note strategy, evaluated frontier models, human-comparison protocol, and underlying business applications separate from the benchmark identity.
- Distinguish ApprenticeBench from static long-horizon task suites, general computer-use benchmarks, broad occupational benchmarks such as Agents' Last Exam, and business-workflow replicas such as RealReplicaBench by its explicit continual-learning/job-apprenticeship axis.
- Treat task inventory, evaluation harness, context limits, persistent-memory setup, human baselines, model results, and future job/domain expansions as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- ApprenticeBench remains a benchmark/dataset identity rather than an accounting agent, continual-learning framework, or production deployment product.
- Comparative readiness claims remain tied to the released benchmark design and evaluation configuration.

## Scoped original-source qualification — 2026-11-08

- **ApprenticeBench** is NeoCognition's 2026 original long-horizon computer-use and continual-learning evaluation in a simulated construction-company **accounts-payable job with Odoo ERP**. The public project defines **100 sequential tasks** across a November 2025–November 2026 simulated company timeline; the agent can inspect handbook, prior posted bills and its own task history.
- The primary interface is screen-driven computer use, with a separate API setting; mentorship and feedback decrease across the sequence. Cumulative success rate, token spend and the publisher's human/model results belong to the exact CUA/API setting, model, harness, reasoning configuration and non-resettable run, not a general proof of job readiness.
- Simulated accounting records and invoices do not authorize real financial access or prove rights to distribute all input assets. No real vendor communication, accounting transaction, model run or third-party content-rights check was performed.
- Original publisher release: https://apprenticebench.com/ ; https://neocognition.io/blog/apprentice-bench/ .
- Source credit covers one existing benchmark identity, public scenario/100-task scoring contract and limits only. No extra metadata or entity, benchmark or human-score reproduction, account access, rights/security/legal review, global CI/renderer, clean or final gate.
