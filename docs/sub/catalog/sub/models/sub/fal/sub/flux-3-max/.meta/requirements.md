- Preserve exact identity, canonical production and learned lineage. Render visible relations with resolving links.
- Keep hosted routes, request controls, weights and organization identities distinct; source all mutable claims.

## Model and route boundary

The [text route](https://fal.ai/models/blackforestlabs/flux-3-max/text-to-video) calls Max a post-trained FLUX 3 Video variant. Three endpoint workflows, audio switches and reasoning controls do not establish separate learned models. The blackforestlabs API namespace does not establish exclusive BFL production.

## Access, resolution and rights

The documented access is hosted inference. Inspected sources do not establish publicly downloadable Max weights or an Apache-2.0/FLUX Kommunity weight license. API output usage remains subject to [fal terms](https://fal.ai/legal/terms-of-service). Endpoint pages describe 720p generation and optional 1080p upscaling; do not label 1080p as native. Verify current endpoint schemas, processing/privacy behavior and rights for the exact use before submitting confidential inputs or publishing outputs.

## Evidence limitations

Do not merge their values into an intrinsic performance guarantee or apply preliminary image-to-video rankings to other workflows. Record exact source date, route, resolution and configuration before comparison; no inference or independent benchmark was performed. Announcement, page-update and promotion dates do not settle earliest public access. Mutable hosted revisions may change without new separately identified checkpoint releases. Current beneficial ownership and indirect stakes are unverified.
