# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify H3 Max as a **distinct post-trained model by fal Research** announced 2026-08-27, derived from the released MiniMax H3 family, not just an inference-optimization preset or an independent MiniMax original checkpoint.
- Preserve legal identity: fal Research is a publisher attribution, while the existing organization is fal (Features & Labels, Inc.). Model API paths under `minimax/h3-max` do not imply sole original production by MiniMax.
- Explain two provider-documented H3 Max workflows: text-to-video and image-to-video. A conditional image or end-frame parameter and changing endpoint modes do not automatically establish new learned model identities.
- Keep the original MiniMax H3 family's open-weight distribution and restrictive community terms separate from H3 Max's fal-hosted API rights. No first-party source inspected establishes H3 Max downloadable weights, a permissive model license, transfer of the original H3 weight license, or suitability for every geography.
- Treat fal-hosted output and inputs as subject to current [fal terms](https://fal.ai/legal/terms-of-service), applicable service/API supplements and model-specific rights. Protect credentials and consented reference media. Check usage rights, processing location/retention and pricing for the actual request.
- Distinguish fal's performance, Elo, throughput and quality comparisons from independently reproduced benchmarks; record dated model/route/resolution/context for decisions. Do not freeze launch discounts or generalize per-route support.
- Keep H3 Max Turbo claims separate until evidence establishes whether it has a distinct trained checkpoint. Do not create extra frozen identities solely for a speed/pricing tier.
- Render the `produced-by` relation to the existing fal legal producer, including its inverse `produces` relation. Link MiniMax H3 as upstream lineage in reader content without treating its family record as a concrete checkpoint.
- Source: [fal announcement](https://blog.fal.ai/introducing-h3-max-by-fal/) (2026-08-27), [text API](https://fal.ai/models/minimax/h3-max/text-to-video/api), [image API](https://fal.ai/models/minimax/h3-max/image-to-video/api), [MiniMax H3 original](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3), and [fal terms](https://fal.ai/legal/terms-of-service).

## Validation

- One model identity; source-backed learned derivative, separate from its serving API routes and original H3 family.
- New model and existing producer relations are reciprocal and reader links resolve.
- Original weights/license terms, independent rights audit, beneficial ownership, authenticated inference, provider benchmarks, repository-wide schema/cache/CI/render and clean/final gates remain unverified.
