# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Qwen3 as a model series within the Qwen family, not as a second model family or one concrete model.
- Expose canonical Qwen-family membership and Qwen Team producer relations.
- Preserve only series-level facts that are genuinely shared by the represented Qwen3 branch.
- Keep model-selection, hardware-fit, runtime-performance, and workload-suitability conclusions outside reference.
- Keep revision-specific behavior on version nodes rather than flattening it into the series or base model.

## Content Specification

- Use `Qwen3` as the page title.
- State that the series contains distinct dense and MoE model identities.
- Distinguish the initial Qwen3/2504 release from later Qwen3-2507 updates.
- Present thinking/non-thinking, multilingual, coding/reasoning, and tool-use statements as upstream-described series capabilities, not independent AI Lab evaluation.
- Keep context limits scoped to the exact represented release/model cards and avoid generalizing initial 32K/YaRN limits to later revisions.
- Keep total and active MoE parameter concepts distinct.
- Include the official Qwen3 repository, technical report, and model collection.
- Link the concrete-model index.

## Validation

- Concrete models, versions, and artifacts are not aliases of the Qwen3 series.
- Producer and parent-family relations resolve to canonical nodes.
- Version-specific facts are not silently generalized to all Qwen3 releases.
- Selection and deployment conclusions are not presented as intrinsic series facts.
