- Identify GPT-5.6 Sol as the flagship concrete tier of the GPT-5.6 generation.
- Preserve exact model/API identity and provider-documented tier capabilities from the legacy Sol page.
- Keep pricing, long-context pricing multipliers, selection guidance, and workload-specific reliability conclusions outside the canonical model profile.

## Content Specification

- Use `GPT-5.6 Sol` as the page title.
- Link the canonical GPT-5.6 generation.
- Preserve the model ID `gpt-5.6-sol`.
- State that the generation alias `gpt-5.6` routes to Sol in the documented source, while treating that alias as routing metadata rather than a separate model identity.

- Preserve text and image input, text output, 1,050,000-token context, 128,000-token maximum output, February 16, 2026 knowledge cutoff, and reasoning-effort controls `none`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, and `max`.

- Preserve that fine-tuning is not supported in the documented model profile.
- Include the official Sol model page and GPT-5.6 launch reference.

## Excluded Residual Content

Preserve outside this canonical model profile:

- token pricing and long-context pricing multipliers;
- recommendations to choose Sol based on reliability-versus-cost trade-offs;
- reproducibility guidance about alias versus explicit model ID beyond the identity caveat needed here;
- workload-specific quality, latency, tool-cost, and accepted-result conclusions.

## Validation

- Provider positioning is not presented as independent AI Lab quality evidence.
- Pricing is not treated as model identity.
- `gpt-5.6` is not represented as a separate concrete model entity.
- Hosted capability support is distinguished from architectural facts.
