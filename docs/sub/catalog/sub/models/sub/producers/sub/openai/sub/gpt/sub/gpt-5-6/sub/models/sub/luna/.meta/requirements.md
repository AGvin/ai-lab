# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify GPT-5.6 Luna as the fastest and most cost-efficient concrete tier of the GPT-5.6 generation in the documented provider positioning.
- Preserve exact model/API identity and provider-documented tier capabilities from the legacy Luna page.
- Keep pricing, rate limits, low-cost routing recommendations, hardware assumptions, and workload-specific accepted-result conclusions outside the canonical model profile.

## Content Specification

- Use `GPT-5.6 Luna` as the page title.
- Link the canonical GPT-5.6 generation.
- Preserve the model ID `gpt-5.6-luna`.
- Preserve Luna's provider positioning as the fastest and most cost-efficient hosted tier in the generation.
- Preserve text and image input, text output, 1,050,000-token context, 128,000-token maximum output, February 16, 2026 knowledge cutoff, and configurable reasoning-token support.
- Preserve provider-documented hosted capabilities including streaming, function calling, structured outputs, web search, file search, code interpreter, hosted shell, apply patch, computer use, MCP, and tool search.
- Preserve that fine-tuning is not supported.
- Preserve the important identity boundary that the cited model page does not publish downloadable Luna weights or a local hardware profile; do not infer VRAM requirements.
- Include the official Luna model page and GPT-5.6 launch reference.

## Excluded Residual Content

Preserve outside this canonical model profile:

- token pricing, cached-input pricing, output pricing, and long-context pricing multipliers;
- rate limits, regional availability, tool charges, and caching rules;
- recommendations for high-volume routing, classification, extraction, drafting, or tool-integrated workloads;
- comparisons against Terra, Sol, local models, or specialists;
- workload-specific reliability, quality ceiling, latency, omission risk, retries, verification cost, escalation frequency, and accepted-result cost.

## Validation

- Provider positioning is not presented as independent AI Lab quality evidence.
- Pricing and rate limits are not treated as model identity.
- No downloadable-weight or VRAM claim is inferred where the legacy official source did not provide one.
- Luna remains a separate concrete model entity from Sol and Terra.
