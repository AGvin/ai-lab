# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify FLUX.2 [klein] as the fast FLUX.2 model identity exposed by Black Forest Labs.
- Preserve the current size/license/artifact options as subvariants of the same named model unless upstream gives them independent durable product identities.
- Keep speed, quality, hardware-fit, license, open-weight, API, resolution, reference-image, and runtime claims freshness-sensitive.
- Keep FLUX.2 [klein] distinct from FLUX.2 [max], [pro], [flex], [dev], the FLUX.2 series, and BFL API/playground surfaces.
- Attribute provider comparisons to Black Forest Labs rather than independent AI Lab measurement.

## Validation

- Size/artifact options do not become peer canonical model identities by default.
- Hosted/API and open-weight behavior remain explicitly scoped to the applicable artifact or serving path.
