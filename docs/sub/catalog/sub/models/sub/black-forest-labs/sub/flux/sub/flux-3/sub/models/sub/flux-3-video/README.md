# FLUX 3 Video

FLUX 3 Video is Black Forest Labs' released FLUX 3 video-generation model with native audio. It supports text, image and video-conditioned workflows; the public API and separately negotiated weight access are distribution routes for the named model. [FLUX 3 Video release](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-video) [FLUX 3 Video product page](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-video).

## Identity and scope

BFL announced general availability on 2026-08-04. Keep text-to-video, reference images, keyframes and video/audio continuation tied to the documented workflow, rather than treating the initial FLUX 3 early-access state as current. [FLUX 3 Video release](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-video) [FLUX 3 Video product page](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-video).

## Use and limitations

The launch described clips up to 20 seconds at native 720p, with 1080p upscaling then available. Current product tooling also advertises 4K upscaling and editing; distinguish native generation, postprocessing and current endpoint limits. [FLUX 3 Video release](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-video) [FLUX 3 Video product page](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-video).

Hosted processing and private weight licensing have separate terms and deployment boundaries. Generated audio, dialogue and continuity need application review; provider demos and speed comparisons are not local measurements. [FLUX 3 Video release](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-video) [FLUX 3 Video product page](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-video).

FLUX 3 Max is a separately post-trained fal Research derivative developed in collaboration with BFL; preserve its distinct identity, hosted access and source-specific claims. [Original fal announcement](https://fal.ai/blog/introducing-flux-3-max-by-fal).
