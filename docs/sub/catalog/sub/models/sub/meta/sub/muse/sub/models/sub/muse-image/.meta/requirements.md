# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Muse Image as Meta's released image-generation/editing model introduced on 2026-07-07.
- Preserve its source-backed image generation, editing, multi-reference composition, agentic tool use, and self-refinement characteristics without treating internal ablations or point-in-time Arena ranks as intrinsic timeless properties.
- Keep Meta product integrations, search/tool access, watermark/detection product surfaces, and regional availability separate from trained-model identity.
- Keep Muse Video excluded while first-party material describes it as an early preview coming later.

## Validation

- Muse Image is not conflated with the Meta AI application or Content Seal verification tooling.
- Muse Video preview evidence does not create a released peer model.
