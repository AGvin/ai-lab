# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Muse Wonder as a model series within the Muse family, introduced with distinct Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full concrete models.
- Preserve shared agentic/coding/computer-use positioning only at the series level when first-party sources support it.
- Keep model-specific access, pricing, subscription requirements, context limits, benchmarks and product integration on concrete models or access owners.

## Validation

- Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full are not collapsed into aliases of one model.
- The Muse hosted personal-agent product is not treated as the model-series identity.
