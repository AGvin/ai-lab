# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Muse Glimmer 30B as Meta's released open-weight local-agent model rather than a hosted Muse product.
- Preserve source-backed 30B-class scale, text/image input, agentic/local-hardware role, and Apache-2.0 boundary with first-party attribution.
- Keep point-in-time benchmark superiority, quantization/runtime results, specific hardware fit, and provider availability outside timeless intrinsic identity.

## Validation

- Muse Glimmer remains a concrete Muse model and is not conflated with Muse Spark Open.
- Apache-2.0 and local/self-hosting facts are not inherited by other Muse models without their own sources.
