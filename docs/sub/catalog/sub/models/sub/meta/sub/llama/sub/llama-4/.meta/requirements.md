## Requirements

- Identify Llama 4 as a natively multimodal model series within the Llama family.
- Link released Scout and Maverick as distinct concrete models.
- Preserve Behemoth only as source-scoped historical teacher-model context while the current first-party evidence still says it was not released and was still training; do not materialize it as a released canonical child without newer authoritative release evidence.
