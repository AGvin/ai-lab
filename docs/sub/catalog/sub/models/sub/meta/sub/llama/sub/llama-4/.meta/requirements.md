# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Llama 4 as a natively multimodal model series within the Llama family.
- Link released Scout and Maverick as distinct concrete models.
- Link Behemoth as a separately announced teacher-model identity while preserving its unreleased/preview boundary from Meta's first-party announcement.
- Keep benchmark claims explicitly attributed to Meta and keep provider-dependent latency, quantization, hosting, and recommendation conclusions outside series identity.

## Validation

- Scout, Maverick, and Behemoth are not treated as aliases or size variants of one concrete model.
- Behemoth is never presented as a generally released downloadable model unless later first-party evidence establishes that lifecycle transition.
