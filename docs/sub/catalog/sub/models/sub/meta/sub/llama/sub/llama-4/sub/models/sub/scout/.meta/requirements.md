# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Llama 4 Scout as a released concrete Llama 4 model and preserve its natively multimodal identity.
- Preserve Meta-documented 17B active parameters, 16 experts, and long-context positioning only with explicit source scope; do not infer unpublished routing or capacity facts from names.
- Keep hardware efficiency claims attributed to Meta and distinguish architectural/model facts from quantization- or runtime-dependent deployment results.
- Preserve the user-supplied video only as community research evidence; do not use it as authority for official specifications or as proof of general image-analysis quality or latency.

## Validation

- Community impressions are clearly separated from official model facts.
- Provider/runtime speed and qualitative vision conclusions are not presented as timeless intrinsic properties.

## Verified card scope (2026-10-30)

- Meta's official card reports 109B total and 17B active parameters, 10M-token context, text/image input and text/code output. The release uses the custom Llama 4 Community License; access and use remain subject to its terms.
- Source: https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-4-Scout-17B-16E-Instruct
- Keep total and active counts separate; neither context capacity nor active count establishes runtime memory or throughput.
