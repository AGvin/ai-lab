# Aya Expanse

Aya Expanse is the multilingual text-model series released on 24 October 2024. Its represented 8B and 32B models are distinct trained identities within Aya. [Source 1](https://cohere.com/blog/aya-expanse-connecting-our-world) · [Source 2](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/aya-expanse-8b) · [Source 3](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/aya-expanse-32b)

## Release and model choice

Cohere introduced Aya Expanse on 24 October 2024. Both represented models generate text from text and are optimized for 23 languages. Their context windows differ substantially; the larger member also forms the explicitly identified language component of Aya Vision 32B. [Release date](https://cohere.com/blog/aya-expanse-connecting-our-world) · [8B model card](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/aya-expanse-8b) · [32B model card](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/aya-expanse-32b) · [Vision 32B lineage](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/aya-vision-32b)

## Access and operational scope

The current Cohere table lists the 32B Chat route as live and the 8B route as retired on 4 April 2026. Official weight repositories remain available under their access conditions. Weight licensing is CC-BY-NC-4.0 with Cohere Labs acceptable-use terms; hosted-service conditions belong to the selected provider route. [Provider status](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/models) · [Exact retirement notice](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/deprecations) · [Weight terms](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/aya-expanse-8b)
