# Aya

Aya is the multilingual research-model family led by Cohere Labs. The represented branches are Aya Expanse for text, Aya Vision for text-and-image understanding, and Tiny Aya for compact multilingual text models. [Primary source](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/aya)

## Family and research initiative

The Aya documentation groups three primary offerings, while the broader initiative also covers datasets, evaluations and research. Expanse, Vision and Tiny Aya remain distinct series within this catalog family. Each concrete record carries its own technical and operational facts. [Family documentation](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/aya) · [Aya initiative](https://cohere.com/research/aya)

## Selection and evaluation

Choose text-only generation, image-grounded text, or a compact regional/global model before comparing model sizes and serving routes. Evaluate the actual languages, scripts, translation directions and document types required by the application. Vendor benchmark results describe the reported evaluation setup and do not establish uniform quality across all languages or tasks. [Current model table](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/models) · [Example of task-specific multilingual evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11510v1)
