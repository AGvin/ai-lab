# Aya

Aya is the multilingual research-model family led by Cohere Labs. The represented branches are Aya 23 (earlier open research weights), Aya Expanse for text, Aya Vision for text-and-image understanding, and Tiny Aya for compact multilingual text models. [Primary source](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/aya)

## Family and research initiative

The current Aya documentation highlights Expanse, Vision and Tiny Aya, while the older 23 May 2024 Aya 23 research release is retained as a distinct historical series. The broader initiative also covers datasets, evaluations and research. Expanse, Vision, Tiny Aya and Aya 23 remain distinct branches within this catalog family. Each concrete record carries its own technical and operational facts. [Family documentation](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/aya) · [Aya initiative](https://cohere.com/research/aya)

## Selection and evaluation

Choose text-only generation, image-grounded text, or a compact regional/global model before comparing model sizes and serving routes. Evaluate the actual languages, scripts, translation directions and document types required by the application. Vendor benchmark results describe the reported evaluation setup and do not establish uniform quality across all languages or tasks. [Current model table](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/models) · [Example of task-specific multilingual evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11510v1)
