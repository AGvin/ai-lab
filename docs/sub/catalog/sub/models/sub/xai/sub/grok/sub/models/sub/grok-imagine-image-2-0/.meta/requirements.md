## Content specification

- Grok Imagine Image 2.0 is a Grok model for image generation and image editing from text and image inputs.
- The model reference documents image output with resolution and quality options. Multi-image editing accepts up to five source images. [Model reference](https://docs.x.ai/developers/models/grok-imagine-image-2.0); [Multi-image editing](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/images/multi-image-editing).

## Source-scoped operating boundaries

- The announced November 2, 2026 retirement of grok-imagine-image-quality is still a future change at this assessment. After that date, the old slug is scheduled to route to Image 2.0 at low quality; the older grok-imagine-image model is unaffected. [Migration guide](https://docs.x.ai/developers/migration/imagine-image-quality-nov-2).
- The auto quality setting can choose different quality levels for generation and editing. Set quality explicitly when repeatable cost and quality matter. [Migration guide](https://docs.x.ai/developers/migration/imagine-image-quality-nov-2).
