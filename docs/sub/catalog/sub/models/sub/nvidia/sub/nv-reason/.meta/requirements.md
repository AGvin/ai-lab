# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify NV-Reason as NVIDIA's medical-imaging reasoning model series spanning radiology-specific vision-language models such as NV-Reason-CXR-3B and NV-Reason-CT.
- Preserve modality and deployment boundaries at the concrete-model level: CXR handles chest X-rays while CT handles native 3D CT volumes; do not generalize architecture, training data, model size, licensing, or evaluation results across the series.
- Keep NVIDIA Clara/Medical AI ecosystem branding distinct from the trained-model series itself.
- Treat medical use restrictions, licenses, checkpoints, hardware/runtime guidance, training data, benchmark results, and clinical-validation claims as freshness-sensitive and source-attributed.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- NV-Reason remains a model series, not an autonomous diagnostic product or cleared clinical system.
- Producer provenance resolves bidirectionally to NVIDIA.
