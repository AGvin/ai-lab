# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning as the open concrete model released on 2026-08-11 for high-volume execution in long-running agents.
- Preserve the first-party distinction of 30B total parameters and 3B active parameters and its MoE architecture.
- Scope NVIDIA's smallest-member comparison to the 2026-08-11 announcement and its stated metric; do not treat it as an evergreen ranking across later releases.
- Treat speed, benchmark, and efficiency comparisons as NVIDIA claims and keep quantized checkpoints, runtime fit, and hosted-provider state outside intrinsic model identity.

## Validation

- Lightning is not treated as an alias, quantization, or version of Nemotron 3 Ultra.
