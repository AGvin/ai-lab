# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify NV-Reason-CT as NVIDIA's released open research 3D vision-language model for CT image analysis, structured report generation, question answering, and multi-step reasoning across chest and abdominal CT volumes.
- Preserve its native 3D vision-encoder boundary, Qwen3.5-4B language-model component, 3D CT/NIfTI input assumptions, training/evaluation data, hardware/runtime guidance, and benchmark results only from current first-party sources.
- Keep current NVIDIA wording explicit that NV-Reason-CT is a research/development foundation rather than an autonomous diagnostic system or cleared clinical product.
- Attribute CT-RATE and clinical-plausibility comparisons to NVIDIA/current cited research rather than treating them as independent AI Lab medical-performance conclusions.
- Treat checkpoints, license/terms, supported formats, preprocessing, runtime requirements, and benchmark values as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- NV-Reason-CT remains a concrete member of NV-Reason and distinct from NV-Reason-CXR-3B.
- Research/clinical-product boundaries remain explicit.
