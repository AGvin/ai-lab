# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify NV-Reason-CXR-3B as NVIDIA's released chest-X-ray vision-language model for structured radiology reasoning, report generation, and multi-step conversational analysis.
- Preserve its chest-X-ray-specific scope, checkpoint identity, Qwen2.5-VL-derived architecture, licensing, model size, training/evaluation methodology, and supported inference stack only from current first-party sources.
- Attribute clinical-study and benchmark claims to NVIDIA/current cited research and do not convert them into AI Lab medical-performance endorsements.
- Keep the model explicitly research/development-oriented and distinct from an autonomous or cleared diagnostic system.

## Validation

- NV-Reason-CXR-3B is a concrete member of NV-Reason and remains distinct from NV-Reason-CT.
- Medical-safety and lifecycle boundaries remain explicit.
