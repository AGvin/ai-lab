# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano Omni as a distinct released multimodal Nemotron 3 model with dedicated NVIDIA technical-report evidence.
- Preserve its multimodal identity and source-backed technical facts without collapsing it into the text-focused Nano sibling or treating it as a packaging artifact.
- Keep runtime packaging, quantizations, deployment backends, GPU fit, throughput, and hosted-provider availability outside intrinsic model identity unless separately materialized.

- Record the source-explicit Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B BF16 language backbone with reciprocal derived-from / has-derivative relations; do not substitute the separately released Nano Base checkpoint.
- Distinguish language-backbone lineage from the C-RADIOv4-H vision and Parakeet-TDT audio encoder components and from models used to improve training data.

## Validation

- Nemotron 3 Nano Omni remains a concrete model inside Nemotron 3.
- Technical-report claims are attributed to NVIDIA and are not generalized to other Nemotron 3 members.
