# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Embed as Cohere's embedding-model family spanning released Embed 4 and current Embed 5 variants.
- Preserve current concrete members separately: Embed 4 remains the prior multimodal generation, while `embed-v5.0-pro` and `embed-v5.0-fast` are current Embed 5 variants with different retrieval-quality/latency targets.
- Keep vector-database products, Compass retrieval product identity, embedding API mechanics, pricing, provider availability, batching, output encodings, and retrieval decision guidance outside intrinsic model-family identity.
- Keep shared embedding-space claims and model-specific modality/context/dimension behavior with the applicable concrete member/current first-party evidence.

## Validation

- Embed 4 and Embed 5 concrete members resolve without collapsing Pro/Fast into deployment tiers of one endpoint.
- The family is not represented as a vector-search service or API endpoint.
