# ERNIE

ERNIE is Baidu's foundation-model family. The represented ERNIE 5.0 and 5.1 entries are distinct trained-model identities; dated or descriptive Qianfan serving names are not automatically additional models. [Primary source](https://ernie.baidu.com/blog/)

## Family and lineage

The catalog represents ERNIE 5.0 and ERNIE 5.1 as separate trained models. Baidu describes 5.1 as derived from the elastic sub-network foundation of 5.0 followed by further post-training; that lineage is recorded on the two members. [5.0 technical description](https://ernie.baidu.com/blog/posts/ernie5.0/) · [5.1 release and lineage](https://ernie.baidu.com/blog/posts/ernie-5.1-0508-release/)

## Access and evaluation

The chosen Qianfan endpoint determines current serving limits and available interfaces. A preview or thinking label on one route does not define the lifecycle of the entire ERNIE family. Compare release-specific outputs with the same tools, retrieval and prompts; do not transfer a member's architectural modality support to every hosted interface. [Current serving catalog](https://cloud.baidu.com/doc/qianfan/s/rmh4stp0j)
