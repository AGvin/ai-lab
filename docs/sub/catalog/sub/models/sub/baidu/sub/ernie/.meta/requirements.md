# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ERNIE as Baidu's foundation-model family and preserve exact current model releases as concrete child identities.
- Materialize ERNIE 5.0 and ERNIE 5.1 as distinct trained releases; do not treat service aliases or dated Qianfan snapshots as peer model identities.
- Keep ERNIE image/video specialization, Qianfan hosting, search/product integrations, pricing, and endpoint lifecycle outside the general family unless independently selected.
- Treat model availability, context/output limits, hosted endpoint aliases, benchmark claims, and retirement state as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Producer provenance resolves to Baidu.
- Preview labels are not generalized to stable ERNIE releases.
