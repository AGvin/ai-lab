# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ERNIE 5.1 as Baidu's stable model released in May 2026 and keep it distinct from the preceding 5.1 Preview and ERNIE 5.0.
- Preserve first-party positioning around agentic post-training, reasoning, knowledge, search, and efficiency only as Baidu claims rather than independent AI Lab evaluation.
- Treat endpoint names, context/output limits, pricing, benchmark rankings, and provider availability as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Membership resolves to ERNIE.
- Preview and stable release identities are not conflated.
