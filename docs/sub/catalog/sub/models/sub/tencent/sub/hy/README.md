# Tencent Hy

**Tencent Hy** is a model family produced by **Tencent Hy Team**, represented in AI Lab by two separately released concrete models: [Hy3](./sub/models/sub/hy3/) (the stable July 2026 release) and [Hy4 preview](./sub/models/sub/hy4-preview/) (released and open-sourced in August 2026). The earlier Hy3 preview is historical release context, not an alias of the accepted stable Hy3 checkpoint or a materialized additional sibling here.

## Models and lifecycle

- [Tencent Hy3](./sub/models/sub/hy3/) — stable post-preview release, source-reported 295B total / 21B active parameters, up to 256K context, Apache-2.0 weights
- [Tencent Hy4 preview](./sub/models/sub/hy4-preview/) — available open-source preview checkpoint, source-reported 770B total / 49B active parameters, long-context positioning and known preview limitations

The family link is **producer provenance**, not a claim that Tencent's applications, cloud APIs, subscription pricing or third-party integrations are additional model identities. Publisher benchmarks and rankings are not independent AI Lab evidence. No generation-level intermediate series is materialized for just these two concrete checkpoints.
