# Stable Diffusion 3.5

Stable Diffusion 3.5 is a released Stability AI model series comprising Large, Large Turbo and Medium. The variants share a series identity while remaining separate trained models with different capacity and inference trade-offs.

## Variant selection

Stability describes Large as its 8.1-billion-parameter variant, Large Turbo as a distilled variant intended for four-step generation, and Medium as a smaller 2.5-billion-parameter model. These are provider descriptions, not an independent guarantee of quality or speed on a particular device. Use each variant’s model card for the exact architecture and inference setup.

## Distribution and use

The series is included in Stability’s current Community/Enterprise licensing program. Hosted APIs and partner services have additional service conditions; downloadable weights retain their own license and any distribution access requirements. Optimized or quantized builds are artifacts of a selected model, not extra peer models in the series. Compare memory and output quality with matching resolution, encoders, precision, sampler and runtime.
