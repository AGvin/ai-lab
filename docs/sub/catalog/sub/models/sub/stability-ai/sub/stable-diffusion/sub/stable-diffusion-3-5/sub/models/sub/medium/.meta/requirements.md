# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium as the released smaller concrete member of Stable Diffusion 3.5.
- Keep model scale, license, artifact, runtime compatibility, precision/quantization, memory, speed, and quality claims freshness-sensitive.
- Do not infer universal consumer-hardware fit from provider positioning; validate exact artifact/runtime/hardware combinations.
- Keep optimized distributions separate from model identity.

- Render the identity, provenance and operational context recorded in canonical `entity.identity_note`; keep version-sensitive claims and official resources aligned with the cited first-party sources.

- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations, ordered by explicit numeric order, target canonical name and target ID; resolve every visible target to its reader.

## Validation

- Hardware-fit claims remain exact to the evaluated artifact/runtime/device.
