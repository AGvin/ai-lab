# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Stable Diffusion 3.5 as Stability AI's released open model series comprising Large, Large Turbo, and Medium variants.
- Preserve the open-weight/community-license boundary and current core-model status only while supported by current Stability AI sources.
- Keep variant-specific architecture/size, speed, quality, license, inference code, quantization/runtime optimizations, and hardware fit with the concrete variant.
- Keep API/partner-hosting surfaces and TensorRT/other optimized artifacts distinct from trained-model identities.
- Attribute prompt-adherence, diversity, quality, speed, and hardware comparisons to Stability AI or the cited evaluator.

## Validation

- Stable Diffusion 3.5 remains a series, not one checkpoint.
- Runtime-optimized artifacts do not become duplicate peer models.
