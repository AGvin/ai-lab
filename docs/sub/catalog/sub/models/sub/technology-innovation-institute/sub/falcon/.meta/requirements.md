# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Falcon as TII's foundation-model family and preserve materially distinct current releases as concrete children.
- Materialize Falcon H1R 7B for compact reasoning and Falcon Perception 600M for grounded vision/segmentation as current representative releases.
- Treat Falcon-H1 Arabic, Falcon-H1-Tiny-R, Falcon-E, older Falcon 2/3 checkpoints, and other specializations as family-level current/historical surfaces unless a separate AI Lab decision-use case requires concrete ownership.
- Keep hosted demos, benchmark rankings, deployment providers, and runtime integrations outside durable family identity.
- Treat release inventory, licenses, benchmarks, context/modalities, and deployment support as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Producer provenance resolves to Technology Innovation Institute.
- Specialized Falcon checkpoints are not duplicated as peer families solely because they have branded names.
