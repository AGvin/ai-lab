# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Gemini 3.7 Flash as the stable concrete `gemini-3.7-flash` model released to GA on 2026-08-13.
- Preserve model-specific capabilities and limits from current Google documentation rather than copying facts from Gemini 3.6 Flash or Gemini 3.8 Flash.
- Keep pricing, quotas, API rollout state, and hosted-service availability freshness-sensitive and outside intrinsic model identity.

## Validation

- Gemini 3.7 Flash remains a concrete member of Gemini rather than a separate family.
- Stable model identity is not conflated with mutable Gemini API or Google AI Studio behavior.
