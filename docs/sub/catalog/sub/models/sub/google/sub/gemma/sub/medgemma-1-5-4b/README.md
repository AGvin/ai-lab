# MedGemma 1.5 4B

MedGemma 1.5 is the 4B multimodal instruction-tuned medical developer model released as google/medgemma-1.5-4b-it under Health AI Developer Foundations terms.

## Relations

- Member of: [Gemma](../../)

## Source-scoped operating boundaries

- The 1.5 release is available as a 4B multimodal instruction model; its terms are Health AI Developer Foundations terms. It has not been evaluated or optimized for multi-turn applications. [Release-specific model card](https://huggingface.co/google/medgemma-1.5-4b-it).
- Google requires validation for the developer's intended use case, including after prompt engineering or other adaptation. Published medical benchmarks do not establish that a downstream clinical workflow is validated or approved. [Development and validation guidance](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medgemma).
