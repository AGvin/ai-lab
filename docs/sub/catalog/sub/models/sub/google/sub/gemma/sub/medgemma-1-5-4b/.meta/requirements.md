# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify MedGemma 1.5 4B as Google's released medical text-and-imaging Gemma model for healthcare-AI development.
- Preserve current 2D/high-dimensional medical-imaging interpretation and text/multimodal medical reasoning scope only while supported by first-party sources.
- Keep MedGemma 1.5 distinct from older MedGemma releases, generic Gemma, hosted clinical applications, medical-device products, and downstream fine-tuned healthcare systems.
- Preserve Google's boundary that MedGemma is a development starting point requiring use-case-specific validation/adaptation; do not present it as a clinically validated diagnostic system.
- Treat medical modality support, artifacts, licenses, runtime/fine-tuning recipes, benchmark results, hardware fit, and deployment routes as freshness-sensitive.
- Attribute medical benchmark/performance claims to Google/Google DeepMind or the cited evaluator.

## Validation

- The node remains the concrete MedGemma 1.5 4B model rather than a medical-device approval claim.
- Clinical safety, efficacy, and regulatory status are never inferred from benchmark capability.
