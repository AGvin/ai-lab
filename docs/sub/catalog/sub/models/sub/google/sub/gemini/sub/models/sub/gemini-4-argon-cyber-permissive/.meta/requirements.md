## Source-scoped operating boundaries

- The model is GA when accessed directly through Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform and is a Google Frontier Cyber Model under the cited service terms. Access requires a separate application; approval for Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber does not carry over. [Identity, access, and terms](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/gemini/4-argon#gemini-4-argon-cyber-permissive).
- The documented differences from ordinary Argon are HIGH default thinking, no computer use, and global/us regions rather than global/us/eu. Do not infer unsupported controls from the general Argon or earlier Cyber names. [Variant comparison](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/gemini/4-argon#gemini-4-argon-cyber-permissive).
- The CodeMender integration remains under Pre-GA terms even though direct model access is GA. Defensive use remains subject to the applicable access policy and authorized application actions. [Integration status](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/gemini/4-argon#gemini-4-argon-cyber-permissive).
