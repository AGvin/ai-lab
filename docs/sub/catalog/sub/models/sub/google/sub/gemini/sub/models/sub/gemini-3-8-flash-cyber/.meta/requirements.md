# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber as the cybersecurity-specialized Gemini 3.8 model variant announced by Google, distinct from the general Gemini 3.8 Flash model.
- Preserve the restricted-access boundary: current access is for trusted defenders through Google's Fairwind Program rather than general Gemini API availability.
- Keep cybersecurity specialization, access policy, and any current benchmark claims source-backed and time-scoped where mutable.
- Do not infer general-purpose superiority or ordinary public availability from the model's family membership.

## Validation

- Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is not collapsed into the base Gemini 3.8 Flash identity.
- Restricted access is not mislabeled as preview or experimental lifecycle status without source evidence.
- Mutable access and benchmark claims are not presented as timeless intrinsic facts.
