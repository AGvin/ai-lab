# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify AlphaFold as the structural-biology AI model family associated with Google DeepMind, with AlphaFold 3 co-developed with Isomorphic Labs.
- Preserve model identity separately from AlphaFold Server, AlphaFold Protein Structure Database, Model Garden/Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform deployment, research papers, and downstream drug-discovery workflows.
- Materialize concrete AlphaFold generations only when their independent identity and current reader value justify it; the current catalog focuses on AlphaFold 3.
- Keep scientific/clinical scope precise: structure-prediction capability does not establish clinical diagnostic approval or therapeutic efficacy.
- Treat downloadable artifacts, licenses, access gates, compute/runtime requirements, hosted availability, and commercial-use conditions as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- AlphaFold remains a model family, not the AlphaFold Server or database.
- Producer provenance allows multiple validated co-developers.
- Clinical claims are not inferred from research capability.
