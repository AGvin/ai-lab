# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present K2 Horizon as IFM's fully open 2026 foundation-model fleet/family and keep the six primary released trained-model identities distinct from later quantizations, `Uno` derivatives, or packaging artifacts.
- Preserve total versus active parameters for MoE models; do not infer active counts beyond current model-card evidence.
- Keep license, context, architecture, training-data release state, model-card specifications, and benchmark/performance claims source-backed and freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- The six primary released K2 Horizon models each resolve back to this family through `member-of`.
- GGUF/quantized artifacts are not represented as separate trained models solely because packaging differs.

## Family rendering clarification

- Render the September 2026 IFM family identity, six primary trained models, and the distinction from Uno derivatives and quantized distributions using the official release and collection references.
- Attribute openness to the publisher and direct readers to each model card's artifact inventory for actual release scope; do not imply all planned artifacts are available.
- Context and training properties vary by member. Do not project one member's context window to the whole family.
