# Documentation Requirements

## Identity and provenance

- Identify this node as a collaboration-oriented model-domain view for jointly released IBM-NASA research models, not as a synthetic producer organization.
- Preserve explicit producer relations on concrete models rather than inferring exclusive or universal co-authorship from namespace placement.
- Keep IBM-only Granite models, NASA organizational identity, datasets, TerraTorch software, papers, demonstrations, and downstream task models with their own canonical identities.

## Operational decision boundaries

- The collaboration namespace carries no shared parameter count, architecture, license, deployment contract, benchmark result, or operational approval.
- Use the exact concrete model record and its current first-party sources for modality, data, runtime, licensing, intended-use, and limitation claims.
- New IBM-NASA releases are discovery candidates until separately qualified; publishing them under the same Hugging Face organization does not automatically make them members of this materialized catalog view.

## Validation

- The node remains collaboration/model-domain navigation rather than an organization or trained-model identity.
- Reader navigation resolves to the currently materialized NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model without duplicating dataset, software, paper, or downstream-model identities.
- No runtime, benchmark reproduction, clean-pass, or final-gate credit is implied by this documentation assessment.
