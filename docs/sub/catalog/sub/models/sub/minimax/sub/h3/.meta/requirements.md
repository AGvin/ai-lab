# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present H3 as the multi-checkpoint family/system, not a single trained checkpoint; no automatic extra credit for its components.
- Preserve release exclusions, separate upstream encoder provenance and custom license/territorial/commercial boundaries from the local entity note.
- Display PROVENANCE/OWNERSHIP REVIEW PENDING below title and link the policy. No licensing or vendor eligibility clearance.
- Link the original model card, license and producer. Check reference-media authorization and prevent misleading impersonation before any generative workflow.

## Validation

- Family and component distinctions remain explicit; no 2K local-only guarantee.
- Producer relation has its reciprocal inverse; source-scoped review is not runtime, legal or whole-catalog validation.
