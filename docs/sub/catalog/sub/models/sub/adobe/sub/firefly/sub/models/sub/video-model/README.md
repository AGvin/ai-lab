# Firefly Video Model

Firefly Video Model is Adobe's video-generation model, made generally available on 24 April 2025 after a public beta. It generates video from text or image inputs and remains distinct from partner video models offered through Firefly. [Primary source](https://news.adobe.com/news/2025/04/adobe-revolutionizes-ai-assisted-creativity-firefly)

## Identity and capabilities

Adobe's 24 April 2025 announcement made Firefly Video Model generally available and described video generation from text prompts and images. The model is distinct from both Firefly's editing tools and the video models that other providers expose inside the app. [Model release and provenance](https://news.adobe.com/news/2025/04/adobe-revolutionizes-ai-assisted-creativity-firefly)

## Workflow limits and evaluation

Adobe's style-preset guide, updated 18 August 2026, selects Firefly Video and offers 540p, 720p and 1080p for a five-second, 24-FPS workflow. Treat these as that workflow's settings. Check the current surface before relying on duration, resolution, reference controls, API support or credit costs. Evaluate motion, temporal consistency and reference fidelity, and review asset rights and generated content.
