# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify GPT-Rosalind as OpenAI's life-sciences model series/family for scientific research workflows across biology, drug discovery, chemistry, genomics, and related translational research.
- Preserve the lifecycle boundary: introduced in research preview in April 2026, updated in June 2026, and moved out of research preview on 2026-09-11 for eligible organizations globally through trusted access.
- Link concrete GPT-Rosalind releases only when OpenAI exposes a sufficiently stable first-party model identity; current public safety documentation identifies GPT-Rosalind-5.5 as a concrete model in the series.
- Keep GPT-Rosalind model identity distinct from Rosalind Workbench, life-sciences plugins, Codex integrations, trusted-access program policy, and the broader Rosalind product/brand surface.
- Treat benchmark and partner-evaluation results as scoped evaluation evidence, not timeless intrinsic capability guarantees.

## Validation

- Current released/trusted-access status is not described as research preview.
- Restricted eligibility is not confused with prerelease lifecycle status.
- Product/workspace/plugin identities are not collapsed into the model family.
- Concrete children are backed by explicit first-party model naming rather than inferred from product updates.

## Source-scoped operating boundaries

- A subscription alone does not grant access. Organizational approval determines permitted workspaces and products; API use needs separate approval where offered [Rosalind access documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001193-gpt-rosalind-for-life-sciences-research).
- Rosalind Workbench and life-sciences plugins are application and tool layers. Research outputs and benchmark results remain conditioned on the model version and evaluated workflow [Rosalind model update](https://openai.com/index/introducing-new-capabilities-to-gpt-rosalind/).
