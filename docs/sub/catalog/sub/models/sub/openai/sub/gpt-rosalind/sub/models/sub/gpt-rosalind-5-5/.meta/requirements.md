# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify GPT-Rosalind-5.5 as the concrete GPT-Rosalind model explicitly named by OpenAI's 2026-06-03 deployment-safety documentation and associated life-sciences capability update.
- Preserve its relationship to the GPT-Rosalind family and source-backed life-sciences research role.
- Keep benchmark scores, token-efficiency comparisons, trusted-access eligibility, and tool/plugin execution layers scoped to their applicable evaluation or deployment evidence.

## Validation

- GPT-Rosalind-5.5 is not conflated with the broader GPT-5.5 general-purpose family solely because the life-sciences update references GPT-5.5 capabilities.
- Evaluation claims remain scoped to the published model/update and protocol.

## Source-scoped operating boundaries

- The release uses a trusted-access and responsible-deployment approach. Access is scoped to approved organizations and users; buying a subscription does not establish authorization to use the model or its connected tools [Rosalind access documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001193-gpt-rosalind-for-life-sciences-research).
- The system card records the release’s research-preview conditions. The September 11 family update changes the broader Rosalind availability statement; it does not prove that every current Rosalind product route resolves to this exact version [Rosalind lifecycle update](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-rosalind/).
