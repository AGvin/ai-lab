# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify the exact Moonshot AI Kimi K3 model as a concrete Kimi-family model, not as a provider service, model series, version alias, or weight artifact.
- Preserve the source-backed July 16, 2026 model release and distinguish it from the July 27, 2026 public full-weight release where weight availability timing is relevant.
- Identify Kimi K3 as an open-weight native multimodal agentic model; do not relabel its custom Kimi K3 License as Apache/MIT-style or OSI open source.
- Preserve the source-backed 2.8T total parameters and 104B activated parameters separately; never use activated parameters as a proxy for storage, memory residency, or local deployment fit.
- Preserve the source-backed MoE architecture context, including Kimi Delta Attention (KDA), Gated MLA/Attention Residuals, sparse expert activation, native vision, and the 1,048,576-token context window at a stable level.
- Preserve the published quantization-aware-training weight/activation formats only as intrinsic model-distribution facts where current first-party sources support them; do not infer runtime support or memory fit from the format names alone.
- Preserve that the released weights use the Kimi K3 License and summarize material commercial/service conditions from the exact license when licensing affects a reader decision; link the official license/repository rather than paraphrasing it as unrestricted open source.
- Treat Moonshot AI benchmark and capability claims for long-horizon coding, agentic knowledge work, reasoning, multimodality, and terminal/tool orchestration as provider evidence, not independent AI Lab proof of task superiority or complete-loop reliability.
- Keep Kimi.com, Kimi Work, Kimi Code, Kimi API, certified/third-party inference providers, pricing, top-up requirements, rate limits, capacity, availability, reasoning-effort controls, and other hosted/runtime surfaces outside immutable model identity; when used in selection guidance, re-check them at decision time.
- Do not encode the owner's earlier observation that access slots were exhausted as durable availability state; current official access must be revalidated when a hosted-access decision is made.
- Keep task recommendations, benchmark comparisons, local-resource fit, provider economics, and accepted-result evidence in their corresponding selection/evidence owners.

## Validation

- Kimi K3 is not confused with the broader Kimi family, a hosted Kimi product, or a third-party inference endpoint.
- Total and active parameter counts remain distinct and no hardware-fit conclusion is inferred from the 104B active figure.
- Custom-license conditions are not collapsed into an `open-source` label.
- Provider benchmark/agentic claims are not presented as AI Lab measured results.
- Mutable hosted availability, pricing, quotas, and account-tier behavior are not stored as timeless model facts.
