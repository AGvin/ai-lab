# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Kimi as Moonshot AI's model family and keep concrete Kimi releases/models in materialized child model nodes.
- Link the canonical Moonshot AI producer and the materialized Kimi K3 model.
- Do not invent an intermediate K3 series/version layer when current upstream identity treats Kimi K3 as the concrete released model.
- Keep Kimi K3 architecture, scale, modality, context, license, benchmark evidence, access surfaces, and selection implications on their appropriate model/evidence/selection owners.

## Validation

- Kimi K3 is not flattened into the producer view or misrepresented as an artifact/version of another Kimi model.
- No provider benchmark claim or mutable hosted availability is stored as a family-level recommendation.
