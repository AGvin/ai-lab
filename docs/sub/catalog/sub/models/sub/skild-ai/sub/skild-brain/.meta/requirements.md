# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Skild Brain as Skild AI's omni-bodied robotics foundation-model family and preserve its producer provenance to Skild AI.
- Describe cross-embodiment robotics, manipulation, navigation, and locomotion as family-level scope without generalizing concrete S1 behavior to every family member.
- Keep customer deployments, robot hardware, training infrastructure, and NVIDIA collaboration facts separate from model-family identity.
- Use the canonical `models/` collection when the optional series level is omitted.

## Validation

- Skild Brain remains a model family rather than a robotics hardware platform or managed service.
- Concrete S1 claims are not generalized to the whole family.
- The `has-member` relation resolves to the canonical S1 model entity.
