# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Provide model-domain navigation for Skild AI model families represented in the catalog.
- Keep the canonical Skild AI organization identity under `catalog/producers/` rather than duplicating company content here.
- Keep robotics applications, deployed robot platforms, NVIDIA infrastructure, and customer-specific deployments outside the model-domain ownership view unless they acquire independent catalog identities.

## Content Specification

- Use `Skild AI models` as the page title.
- Link the Skild Brain family as the canonical model-family owner for S1.
- Use first-party Skild AI material for model identity and release-state claims.

## Validation

- Skild AI company identity and Skild Brain model-family identity remain distinct.
- Model-domain navigation resolves to canonical family/model nodes.
