# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Clef as Cloudflare's separately trained, released family of System One-style decision models, launched October 1, 2026 and extended by Clef-omni on October 9, 2026.
- Distinguish typed `noul`, `choice`, and `score` question answering with per-option probabilities from unconstrained natural-language text generation.
- Preserve independently released family members: Clef (27B), Clef-flash (9B), and Clef-omni (30B total / 3B active MoE with native audio/video input).
- Do not treat current Workers AI per-token pricing, context-window limits, AI Gateway routing, latency optimization, or provider aliases as immutable intrinsic model identity.
- Distinguish model weights and self-hosted capability from narrower Cloudflare-hosted configuration. Vendor benchmark/speed/quality claims remain attributed first-party claims.
- Keep Clef separate from independently produced TypeSafe Jev, Perplexity's decision models, and AWS Strands Decider.
- Preserve the canonical Cloudflare producer relation, inverse provenance, and family member links.

## Validation

- Each selected family member is a separately released model, not a mutable serving tier or alias.
- Family `has-member` and model `member-of` relations resolve bidirectionally.
- Model producer provenance resolves to Cloudflare.
- Published scores, speeds, and licensing are not presented as independent AI Lab benchmark findings.
