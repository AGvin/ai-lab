# Cloudflare models

Cloudflare models is the navigation view for the Cloudflare-trained models represented in this catalog. The current released scope is the Clef decision-model family; Workers AI also hosts models produced by other organizations. [Source 1](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-10-01-clef-workers-ai/) · [Source 2](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/)

## Represented scope

Cloudflare introduced its first trained decision models, Clef and Clef-flash, on 1 October 2026 and added Clef-omni on 9 October. Their canonical family and member pages explain the represented model identities. The Workers AI model catalog includes other producers' models as well. [Initial release](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-10-01-clef-workers-ai/) · [Omni release](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-10-09-clef-omni-workers-ai/) · [Workers AI catalog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/)

## Choosing an access route

Model training and model hosting have different owners and constraints. Use the selected member's official card for its weights and implementation, and the current Workers AI model page for hosted inputs, context and usage terms. Keep the Cloudflare company profile, AI Gateway and the announced fine-tuning platform separate from these trained models. [Model training and platform scope](https://blog.cloudflare.com/clef-decision-models/)
