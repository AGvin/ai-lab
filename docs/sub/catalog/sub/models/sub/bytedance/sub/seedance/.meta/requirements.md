# Documentation Requirements

## Identity and provenance

- Define Seedance as a ByteDance Seed audiovisual/video model family; 2.5 is a distinct released model, not a new Dreamina/Jimeng or Doubao application identity. [Seed models](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/models) [2.5 official launch](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5).
- Preserve the canonical ByteDance Seed producer reciprocal relation and exact member-of/has-member pair for Seedance 2.5. Do not convert provider API route identifiers into extra model records.
- ByteDance's own launch describes Seedance 2.5 as building upon the unified joint audio-video generation architecture of Seedance 2.0; it does not establish downloadable weights or identical model internals. Avoid asserting unpublished parameter counts or independent reproducibility.

## Validation

- The family links one materialized Seedance 2.5 model triplet and retains accurate publisher and route boundaries.
- A separate family support identity does not earn a frozen candidate gate; only the released exact model may earn its two acceptance gates.
