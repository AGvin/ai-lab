# Seedance 2.5

**Seedance 2.5** is ByteDance Seed's audiovisual video-generation model, [announced on 31 July 2026](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5). It is a distinct named model in the Seedance family, not a serving route, a consumer video editor or a Seedream image-generation variant.

## Capabilities reported by the publisher

- Single-pass audio-video generation of **up to 30 seconds**, with multi-round extensions for longer storytelling.
- Composite reference input described as up to 30 images, 10 video clips and 10 audio clips; model/host processing of each input remains product- and route-dependent.
- Timestamp-based targeted editing, camera changes, reference-motion control and compositing tools, including green-screen workflows.

These are official launch descriptions, not independent AI Lab measurements. ByteDance describes building on Seedance 2.0's unified joint audiovisual generation architecture; that does **not** establish the same training checkpoint or public model weights. [Official model page](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance2_5).

## Availability and rights boundaries

The model is offered through publisher-branded experiences such as Dreamina/Jimeng and Doubao. BytePlus ModelArk also lists the provider route `dreamina-seedance-2-5-260628`; this dated route label is not a new model identity, nor a guarantee of account/region eligibility. Check current access terms, model IDs and quotas before integration. [BytePlus release announcements](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/modelark/model-release-announcement).

Video, image and audio reference assets may involve personal likeness and voice rights or third-party copyright. Use consented media, verify service retention and output permissions and avoid sensitive uploads without an approved data-processing contract. No public downloadable checkpoint or license for Seedance 2.5 was established by the checked sources; API use is not weight redistribution authority.
