Voxtral is Mistral AI’s audio-model family spanning audio understanding, transcription and speech generation.

## Scope and status

Its represented models have different input/output directions and operating contracts: Small understands audio, Mini Transcribe handles offline transcription, Realtime streams transcription, and TTS generates speech. [Voxtral Small model card](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/voxtral-small-25-07) [Voxtral Mini Transcribe 2 model card](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/voxtral-mini-transcribe-26-02) [Voxtral Realtime model card](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/voxtral-mini-transcribe-realtime-26-02) [Voxtral TTS model card](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/voxtral-tts-26-03)

## Scope and boundaries

Do not transfer diarization, system-prompt support, licensing or weight availability between members. A transcription label or synthesized voice is not proof of a speaker’s identity.
