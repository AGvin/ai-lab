# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Fable as a Claude model series for Anthropic's highest-capability long-horizon reasoning and agentic-work tier.
- Keep the series distinct from the closely related invite-only Mythos line even when current releases share capabilities.
- Link Claude Fable 5.1 as the currently materialized concrete Fable model.
- Keep mutable capability, pricing, availability, and retirement facts source-backed at the concrete-model level.

## Validation

- Fable remains a series within the Claude family rather than a separate model family.
- Mythos is not collapsed into Fable.
- Concrete release facts are not generalized across every Fable generation.
