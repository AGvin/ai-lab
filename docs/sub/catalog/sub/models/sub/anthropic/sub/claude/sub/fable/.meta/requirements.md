# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Fable as a Claude model series positioned by Anthropic for demanding reasoning and long-horizon agentic work; do not impose a permanent capability ranking across Claude series.
- Keep the Fable and restricted-access Mythos series distinct while accurately documenting the shared underlying model and different safeguards of their 5.1 releases from current Anthropic sources.
- Link Claude Fable 5.1 as the currently materialized concrete Fable model.
- Keep mutable capability, pricing, availability, and retirement facts source-backed at the concrete-model level.

## Validation

- Fable remains a series within the Claude family rather than a separate model family.
- Mythos is not collapsed into Fable.
- Concrete release facts are not generalized across every Fable generation.
