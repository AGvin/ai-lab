# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present this scenario for a small team, channel, community, or content business that repeatedly creates posts, images, scripts, memes, or related media and shares brand/review responsibilities.
- Preserve managed assistants and approved hosted image/media tools as the lowest-administration starting route when manual repetition is still acceptable.
- Preserve an API workflow as an upgrade when repeated work justifies automation; evaluate suitable text/multimodal models such as Gemini 3.6 Flash or GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra only as current candidates linked to their canonical model owners, with mutable price/access rechecked at decision time.
- Use OpenRouter or another routing intermediary only when multi-provider access/fallback materially helps the workflow; apply the shared data-boundary rule and do not treat the intermediary as a model or privacy-neutral layer.
- Preserve a local preparation/review route with a measured compact multimodal model such as Gemma 4 E4B Instruct when private drafts, documents, images, or bounded media review justify local processing.
- Preserve a hybrid production route with a measured local text/multimodal model plus a separately evaluated image-generation specialist such as FLUX.1-schnell or temporary GPU only when the complete workflow improves accepted-result cost and production quality.
- Treat a 24 GB-class GPU only as legacy conservative planning context for a media worker, not as a universal minimum or proof of fit; bind any local/media fit claim to exact model artifact, runtime, resolution, batch, precision, and measured memory.
- Require team-level brand review, moderation where applicable, rights/licensing/provenance checks, disclosure policy, correction workflow, and human acceptance before publication when those controls are material.
- Include generated volume, retries, review time, model switching, media-worker lifecycle, and correction cost in accepted-result economics rather than comparing only token/image request prices.
- Keep the generic `small-business-team` route separate and unmaterialized until distinct source-backed content exists; this scenario owns repeated content/creative production, not all small-business AI use.

## Validation

- The scenario is a shared content/creative workflow rather than a generic business or individual creator route.
- Hosted/API candidates are not presented as permanent rankings and mutable access/pricing is rechecked when material.
- Local/media hardware classes are not treated as universal fit guarantees.
- Rights, brand, moderation/disclosure, and human-review needs are represented where they materially affect production acceptance.
