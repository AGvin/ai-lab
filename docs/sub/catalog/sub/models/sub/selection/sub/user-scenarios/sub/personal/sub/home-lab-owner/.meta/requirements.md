# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present this scenario for an individual who operates or plans persistent AI services on a workstation, server, NAS-adjacent host, or other home-lab infrastructure and accepts ongoing systems administration.
- Preserve the legacy resident-generalist route with Qwen3 14B as an evaluation hypothesis for a measured local service; require exact artifact, quantization/precision, runtime, context, concurrency, auxiliary components, and peak-memory evidence before treating it as resident-fit.
- Preserve a compact multimodal lane with Gemma 4 E2B/E4B Instruct when bounded image, document, UI, or short-audio tasks justify a separate service; account for the memory and operational cost of multiple resident services.
- Preserve Qwen3 30B-A3B as a larger sequential route only when unloading/reloading or otherwise scheduling the larger model is measured to provide accepted-result value that justifies switching and recovery overhead.
- Preserve temporary cloud GPU or hosted specialist escalation for workloads that are too large, too occasional, or operationally unsuitable for permanent local residency.
- Treat uptime, remote access, service exposure, authentication, updates, storage, backups, observability, power draw, heat/noise where relevant, failure recovery, and operator time as material constraints in the route decision.
- Distinguish this scenario from `ai-enthusiast/`: persistent service operation and reliability are primary here, while experimentation breadth and frequent stack switching are primary there.
- State that self-hosting is not automatically cheaper than managed access after electricity, storage, administration, failures, upgrades, and operator time are counted.
- Keep detailed network/security architecture, hardware purchasing, orchestration, and service scheduling in their canonical owners; record them here only as constraints that materially affect model selection.
- Escalate or change the portfolio when specialist workloads, concurrency, context, quality ceiling, privacy boundary, power/maintenance cost, or service reliability make the current resident route inefficient.
- Link named models, runtimes, infrastructure software, and hosted services to canonical catalog owners instead of duplicating their full profiles.

## Validation

- The scenario requires persistent operational concerns and is not a duplicate of the experimentation-first AI enthusiast route.
- Resident-fit claims are bound to exact artifacts/runtime/context/concurrency evidence rather than nominal VRAM or active-parameter counts.
- Total cost includes meaningful operator and infrastructure costs.
- Cloud specialist escalation includes lifecycle, billing, and data-boundary considerations.
