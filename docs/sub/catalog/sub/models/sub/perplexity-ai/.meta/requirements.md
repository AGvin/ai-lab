# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Provide model-domain navigation for trained model identities produced or post-trained by Perplexity AI.
- Keep the canonical Perplexity AI organization identity under `catalog/producers/` rather than duplicating company or product content here.
- Keep Perplexity's hosted workspace, Computer/Portable Computer products, APIs, cloud routes, and third-party base models with their respective canonical owners.
