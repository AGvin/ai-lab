# Granite Speech 4.1

IBM speech-model family represented here by the specialist Granite Speech 4.1 2B Plus checkpoint for rich transcription, speaker attribution and word timestamps.

## Boundaries

The Plus checkpoint does not inherit punctuation or capitalization capabilities from other Granite Speech models. Its October 2026 in-place update is not a separately verified version identity. Other general base and NAR variants are outside this materialized scope. Pin runtime and checkpoint; evaluate transcription and timing on representative recordings. Published weights use Apache-2.0.
