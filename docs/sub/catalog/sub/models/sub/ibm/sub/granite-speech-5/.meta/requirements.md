# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Granite Speech 5.0 as IBM's current compact automatic-speech-recognition family released in August 2026.
- Keep the Apache-2.0 commercial-use TurboCTC checkpoint and the explicitly non-commercial NC checkpoint as distinct canonical releases because their licensing and training-data boundaries differ materially.
- Treat language coverage, benchmark results, runtime support, and deployment integrations as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Licensing differences between the two 470M checkpoints are preserved.
- Producer provenance resolves to IBM.
