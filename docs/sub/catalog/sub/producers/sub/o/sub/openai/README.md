# OpenAI

OpenAI is the producer organization represented across models, services, developer software, Agent Skills, specifications, research datasets, and hardware in this catalog. Its published structure distinguishes the OpenAI Foundation from the controlled OpenAI Group PBC. [Organizational structure](https://openai.com/our-structure/).

## Scope and operating boundaries

Corporate control, product provenance, and the contracting party are different questions. The applicable service agreement and jurisdiction determine the legal service provider; the producer name does not establish one universal contract, data policy, or access route. [Services agreement](https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/); [European terms](https://openai.com/policies/eu-terms-of-use/).

OpenAI publishes hosted models and downloadable open weights. The documented gpt-oss weights are not served through the OpenAI API or ChatGPT; local and third-party deployments retain their own operating responsibilities. [Open-weight FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11870455-openai-open-weight-models-gpt-oss).

Lifecycle notices apply to the exact service and model identifiers they name. A deprecation announcement and a shutdown date are distinct events. Repository, model, dataset, and service licenses or use conditions also remain artifact-specific. [API deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations).

Promptfoo is now part of OpenAI but remains a distinct producer identity for its software. Hardware attribution also preserves collaboration: OpenAI describes designing Jalapeño with implementation and production partners. [Promptfoo organization](https://www.promptfoo.dev/about/); [Jalapeño announcement](https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/).
