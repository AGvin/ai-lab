# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify OpenRouter, Inc. as the organization operating the OpenRouter hosted model-routing/API service.
- Keep the producer identity distinct from the OpenRouter service and from third-party model providers routed through it.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current official site, Terms, and Privacy references establishing the operator identity.
- Keep unrelated corporate, funding, staffing, pricing, and mutable service details outside this concise producer page.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse OpenRouter, Inc., the OpenRouter service, and routed model providers into one entity.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.

## Producer decision boundaries

- Separate the routing company, hosted API, inference provider, model author, and selected endpoint.
- Do not convert router-level privacy settings into a universal claim about upstream logging, retention, training, or jurisdiction.
- Render the official-resources block from canonical references supporting the producer identity and stated boundaries.

These boundaries are grounded in the first-party references recorded in the current entity. Detailed product behavior remains with each related artifact.
