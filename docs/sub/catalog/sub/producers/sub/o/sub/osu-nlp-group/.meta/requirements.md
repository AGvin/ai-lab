# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Ohio State NLP Group as the first-party maintaining research group of Mind2Web benchmarks, distinct from Ohio State University as a whole and from commercial model vendors.
- Keep original offline action-trace benchmark ownership with Mind2Web; preserve named coauthors and their other university or industry affiliations when describing later related work.
- Render standard validated producer relations and preserve exact reciprocal benchmark references.
- Keep independently published live Online-Mind2Web and agentic-search Mind2Web 2 identities separate when their canonical records are materialized.

## Validation

- The group publishes the official `OSU-NLP-Group/Mind2Web` repository.
- Every declared `produces` relation has a matching dataset `produced-by` inverse.
