# Ohio State NLP Group

Ohio State NLP Group is the research team publishing the represented Mind2Web projects through the OSU-NLP-Group organization. Original Mind2Web, Online-Mind2Web, and Mind2Web 2 are separately released research artifacts. [Group organization](https://github.com/OSU-NLP-Group); [Mind2Web repository](https://github.com/OSU-NLP-Group/Mind2Web).

## Scope and operating boundaries

Project maintenance does not imply that every author belongs to the same institution. Online-Mind2Web explicitly includes Ohio State and University of California, Berkeley coauthors. The research team is also distinct from the university as a legal or contracting entity. [Online-Mind2Web repository](https://github.com/OSU-NLP-Group/Online-Mind2Web).

The datasets retain different evaluation protocols, access arrangements, and licensing scopes. Publication of research tasks is not permission to use third-party accounts or perform arbitrary actions on live websites. [Mind2Web 2 repository](https://github.com/OSU-NLP-Group/Mind2Web-2).
