# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Timothy Jaeryang Baek (`tjbck`) as the creator and original producer of Open WebUI.
- Keep creator provenance distinct from the current Open WebUI Inc. copyright/ownership layer and from community contributions.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party profile and upstream project references.

## Validation

- Timothy Jaeryang Baek is represented as Open WebUI's creator/origin producer without implying that all current rights are held personally.
- Open WebUI Inc. remains a distinct canonical organization when current ownership/copyright is discussed.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
