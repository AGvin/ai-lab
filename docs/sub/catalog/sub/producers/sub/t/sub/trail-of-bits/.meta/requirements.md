# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Trail of Bits as an independently identifiable security research and engineering firm founded in 2012, not a trained AI model, a managed inference provider, or a duplicate of its published skills.
- Ground organizational identity and security focus in Trail of Bits' official website, company background and first-party `trailofbits/skills` repository.
- Attribute the published **Trail of Bits Skills** collection/marketplace to the organization through a single reciprocal `produces` / `produced-by` pair. Do not count separately indexed or bundled plugin directories as additional accepted producer identities.
- Keep distinct the company's professional security engagements, the public skills/plugin collection and any unrelated target codebases or products under assessment; publishing an audit workflow does not authorize use against third parties.
- Describe licensing and executable/plugin permissions only for the collection's independently maintained node, not as a blanket license to other Trail of Bits publications or client reports.
- Avoid unsupported legal-entity suffixes, current headcounts, financial metrics, and a claim that every plugin author is an employee.

## Validation

- [First-party company background](https://trailofbits.com/about/), [services](https://trailofbits.com/services), and [repository](https://github.com/trailofbits/skills) support identity and provenance.
- Exactly one linked collection identity and inverse relation; the Producers T index lists the node once.
- No security scan, installation, third-party authorization, full source-license audit or whole-catalog clean pass is claimed.
