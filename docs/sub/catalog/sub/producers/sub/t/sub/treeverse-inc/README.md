# Treeverse, Inc.

Treeverse, Inc. is the legal company behind lakeFS. Following lakeFS's November 2025 acquisition of DVC from Iterative.ai, it is the represented current owner and maintainer of DVC; Dmitry Petrov's original creator role and DVC's separate project identity are retained.

## Operating boundary

Use current Treeverse/DVC maintenance channels and confirm the publisher of installed extensions and updates. The acquisition announcement describes DVC as continuing with its own community and website, and identifies a transfer of the VS Code extension to Treeverse. That transition does not merge DVC with lakeFS or imply that historical Iterative commercial services and contracts transfer unchanged.
