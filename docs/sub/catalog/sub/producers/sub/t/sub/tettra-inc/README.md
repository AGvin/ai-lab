# Tettra, Inc.

Tettra, Inc. is the company identified as operating Tettra's hosted knowledge-management service, APIs and integrations. The organization remains distinct from the Tettra service and its Kai assistant capability.

## Operating boundary

Review the applicable agreement before importing workplace content or enabling integrations. Tettra's publicly posted terms, dated May 14, 2018, restrict protected health information and broadly defined personal identifying information in customer content; they also say that enabled integrations authorize access to connected accounts. Check current contractual terms, team permissions and permitted data categories rather than inferring suitability from the company or AI branding.
