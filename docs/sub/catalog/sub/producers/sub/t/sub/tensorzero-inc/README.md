# TensorZero, Inc.

TensorZero, Inc. is the company identified in TensorZero's enterprise terms and the historical direct producer of the TensorZero software.

## Company and product boundary

Historical product provenance and enterprise terms do not establish current upstream updates, security fixes, support or a hosted service. This software lifecycle evidence does not establish the company's legal dissolution.
