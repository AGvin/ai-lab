# TIGER AI Lab

TIGER AI Lab is the Text and Image GEnerative Research lab whose official organization describes its University of Waterloo base. The lab publishes the represented MMLU-Pro benchmark through its first-party repository and the linked TIGER-Lab dataset distribution.

## Operating boundary

Separate research authorship and benchmark maintenance from third-party model submissions and live leaderboard operation. Reproduction requires the chosen dataset revision, prompt and answer-extraction protocol; original MMLU and MMLU-Pro scores are not interchangeable. The official MMLU-Pro dataset card records its license, while other lab datasets and software require their own licensing checks.
