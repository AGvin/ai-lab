# Elementl, Inc.

Elementl, Inc., doing business as Dagster Labs, is the company behind the Dagster open-source project and Dagster+. The company identity remains distinct from the Dagster software and managed-service product layers.

The July 2026 Prefect acquisition transition is separate lifecycle context and does not change the canonical company relation into an ownership claim while first-party close-status wording remains ambiguous.
