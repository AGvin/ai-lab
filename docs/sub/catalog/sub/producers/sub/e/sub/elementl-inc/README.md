# Elementl, Inc.

Elementl, Inc., doing business as Dagster Labs, is the company behind the Dagster open-source project and Dagster+. The company identity remains distinct from the Dagster software and managed-service product layers.

Prefect announced its acquisition of Dagster in July 2026 and said Dagster and Dagster+ would continue to be supported. Elementl, Inc. remains the producing identity documented here and is distinct from Element Labs, Inc., the company behind LM Studio.
