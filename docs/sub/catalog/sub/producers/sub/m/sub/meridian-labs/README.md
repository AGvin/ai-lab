# Meridian Labs

Meridian Labs is an independent AI-evaluation nonprofit, the current development home of Petri and Bloom, and a credited co-developer of Inspect AI with the UK AI Security Institute.

## Producer boundary

Anthropic's original production of Petri and Bloom remains separate from their subsequent development at Meridian. Inspect's co-development credit does not establish sole ownership or an additional maintenance relation; evaluation software provenance does not guarantee a model's safety or transfer responsibility for running untrusted evaluation workloads.
