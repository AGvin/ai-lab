# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Mistral AI as the canonical producer organization for the documented Mistral models.
- Preserve the Mistral AI model-domain view as navigation and link the Mistral Small family through the `produces` relation.
- Keep model facts, hosted endpoints, pricing, service features, and deployment guidance in their corresponding owners.
- Include official site, documentation, and official Hugging Face organization as research references.

## Validation

- The producer page does not duplicate Mistral Small model profiles.
- Model-domain and family links resolve to canonical catalog nodes.
- Mutable service state is not generalized into producer identity.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
