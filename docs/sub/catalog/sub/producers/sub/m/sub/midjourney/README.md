# Midjourney

Midjourney is the independent, community-funded research lab that develops the Midjourney image-generation models and operates their hosted creative product.

## Producer boundary

The organization, model family, and web/Discord product surfaces have separate identities. Niji is a specialized series developed with Spellbrush, so the Midjourney producer relationship does not imply sole authorship of every collaborative model. Version behavior, availability, subscription/GPU accounting, personalization and output-use terms belong to the applicable model or service release. Access to the hosted product does not itself establish downloadable weights, an open-source model license, or unrestricted rights to every input or output.
