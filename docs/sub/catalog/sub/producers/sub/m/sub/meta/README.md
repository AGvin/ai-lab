# Meta

Meta is the technology company behind the Llama and Muse model families, the Meta AI assistant and Muse personal-agent services, MTIA accelerators, and the Faiss library.

## Producer boundary

These are distinct research, software, hardware, and hosted-product responsibilities. Llama releases use their own community licenses; Faiss uses an MIT software license. Neither establishes the terms for Muse or Meta AI, whose account, data, connector, and action permissions belong to the specific service. The MTIA program is developed with Broadcom, and production use inside Meta does not establish a retail accelerator offering. A producer relation records Meta's role without making every contributor, third-party integration, or downstream model deployment Meta-owned or Meta-operated.
