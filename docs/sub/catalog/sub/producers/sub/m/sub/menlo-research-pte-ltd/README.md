# Menlo Research Pte Ltd

Menlo Research Pte Ltd, branded as Menlo Research, is the organization producing Jan. The legal organization remains distinct from the Jan software identity and community contributors.

## Producer boundary

Jan's published privacy policy, last updated January 16, 2025, names this company for the desktop app and website. That policy is scoped to those services; it does not make Menlo the author of connected third-party models or extend local-model data-handling claims to every cloud provider or newer agent product.
