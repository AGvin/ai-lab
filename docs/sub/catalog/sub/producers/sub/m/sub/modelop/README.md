# ModelOp

ModelOp is the company and producer behind ModelOp Enterprise AI Command Center, the represented enterprise AI-management software.

## Producer boundary

The platform manages AI assets from multiple sources; that role does not make ModelOp the producer of customer-built or third-party models. Product deployment, integrations, access controls, support and evidence for governance outcomes belong to the software and customer operating context, not to the producer relation alone.
