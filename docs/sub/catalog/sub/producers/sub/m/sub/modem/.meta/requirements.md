- Identify Modem as the organization sponsoring/publishing the Hunk project represented by official Modem and Hunk sources.
- Do not invent a legal-company suffix not established by current authoritative sources.
- Keep Modem distinct from the Hunk software entity.
- Link the canonical Hunk code-review tool profile from the physically materialized `produces` relation when the reciprocal Hunk `produced-by` relation resolves successfully.

- Hunk and Modem remain separate identities.
- The Modem/Hunk `produces` / `produced-by` relation pair is physically present at both endpoints and semantically consistent.
