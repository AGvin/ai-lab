# MOSTLY AI Solutions MP GmbH

MOSTLY AI Solutions MP GmbH is the historical company producer behind the MOSTLY AI Data Intelligence Platform. The company account records a 2017 origin in Vienna, and its 2025 releases describe the separate SDK and data-intelligence environment.

## Producer boundary

The July 11, 2025 public handbook establishes the historical company name, not independently verified present corporate status. Syntho announced acquisition of the MOSTLY AI brand on June 9, 2026; that brand transaction does not by itself establish original software authorship, software ownership, or support obligations for earlier deployments. The separately released SDK license does not cover every platform component.
