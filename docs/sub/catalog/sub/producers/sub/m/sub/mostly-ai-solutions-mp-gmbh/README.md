# MOSTLY AI Solutions MP GmbH

MOSTLY AI is the company behind the historical MOSTLY AI Data Intelligence Platform. Its company account traces its origins to Vienna in 2017; its January and July 2025 accounts describe the Enterprise Platform, the separate SDK release, and the data-intelligence environment it built.

The public company handbook page dated July 11, 2025 identifies MOSTLY AI Solutions MP GmbH. This is dated legal-name evidence rather than an independent confirmation of present corporate status. Syntho's later acquisition of the MOSTLY AI brand is separate from original software production.
