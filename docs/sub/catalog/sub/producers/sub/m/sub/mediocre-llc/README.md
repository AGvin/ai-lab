# Mediocre, LLC

Mediocre, LLC is the current corporate maintainer and steward of the Chatbox AI product surface. This role remains distinct from Benn Huang's original producer/creator role and does not imply unverified legal ownership.

## Producer boundary

Chatbox's official support page identifies Mediocre, LLC as its operator and distinguishes the free client from optional paid model access. Similar product names and unrelated billing merchants are not this operator; support and billing responsibility should follow the exact product site and receipt, while third-party model-provider terms remain separate.
