# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Meta as the canonical producer organization for its represented Llama model family, Meta AI products, accelerator hardware, and Faiss vector-search software.
- Keep Meta AI assistant/product surfaces distinct from Llama model identities.
- Include current first-party Meta sources sufficient to establish organization and Llama provenance.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated relation state.

## Validation

- Llama, represented Meta products and hardware, and Faiss `produced-by` relations have matching `produces` inverses here.
- Product or model specifications are not duplicated into the producer profile.
