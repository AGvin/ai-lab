- Identify MLC Community as the canonical open-community producer identity for MLC LLM.
- Preserve early multi-institution project provenance: first-party history says MLC LLM was initiated by contributors from CMU Catalyst, UW SAMPL, SJTU, OctoML, and the MLC community rather than by one sole company.
- Do not convert contributor affiliations or supporting organizations into sole ownership or maintenance claims without a stronger current contract.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current MLC community and MLC LLM provenance references.

- The page does not invent sole-company ownership or maintenance of MLC LLM.
