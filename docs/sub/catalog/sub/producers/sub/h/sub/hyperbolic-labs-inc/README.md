# Hyperbolic Labs, Inc.

**Hyperbolic Labs, Inc.** is the legal company named as owner and operator of the Hyperbolic platform in the [March 24, 2025 official Terms of Service](https://www.hyperbolic.ai/terms). The current **Hyperbolic** brand presents GPU compute, orchestration and [Flux Inference](../../../../../services/sub/model-apis/sub/hyperbolic-flux-inference/) as distinct offerings.

## Company, product and legal scope

Hyperbolic's [October 21, 2026 announcement](https://www.hyperbolic.ai/blog/flux-inference-api) introduced **Flux** as its new hosted model-inference service built on the Forge compute control plane. The [October 27, 2026 company funding announcement](https://www.hyperbolic.ai/blog/a1-funding) identifies the active business and investors. These public materials do not amount to a complete ownership register, current beneficial-control or indirect national-origin audit, and older terms do not replace the exact present-day agreement or supplemental terms for any account.

The company offers hosted APIs and compute infrastructure; serving or routing requests to third-party models does not make it the original developer of those weights. Models externally served by other providers can introduce additional security, rights and jurisdictional dependencies.
