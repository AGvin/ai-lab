# HiddenLayer

HiddenLayer is the organization behind the HiddenLayer AI Security Platform. Its [company page](https://www.hiddenlayer.com/about-us) establishes the vendor identity, and the [platform page](https://www.hiddenlayer.com/platform) describes its security capabilities.

The vendor, security platform and protected AI systems retain separate identities. Product capabilities do not establish ownership of customer models or a universal protection guarantee.
