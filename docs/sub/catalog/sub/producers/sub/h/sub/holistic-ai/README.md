# Holistic AI

Holistic AI is the organization behind the Holistic AI Governance Platform. Its [company page](https://www.holisticai.com/about) and [product page](https://www.holisticai.com/ai-governance-platform) establish that vendor and product relationship.

The platform's discovery, testing and governance workflows remain separate from the AI systems they assess. The producer profile does not establish ownership of connected systems or automatic compliance for every deployment.
