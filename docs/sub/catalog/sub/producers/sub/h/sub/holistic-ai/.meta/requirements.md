# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Holistic AI as the canonical producer organization for the represented Holistic AI Governance Platform service.
- Keep concrete platform modules, governance features, compliance mappings, integrations, and deployment claims with the service entity rather than duplicating them in the producer profile.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

- The `produces` relation is the inverse of the Holistic AI Governance Platform `produced-by` relation.
