# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ZenML GmbH as the company that creates and maintains the open-source ZenML project.
- Keep the company identity distinct from the ZenML software identity and from commercial ZenML Pro/Cloud surfaces.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party ZenML company and documentation references.

## Validation

- Producer/maintenance responsibility is not used to collapse open-source ZenML and commercial product layers.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
