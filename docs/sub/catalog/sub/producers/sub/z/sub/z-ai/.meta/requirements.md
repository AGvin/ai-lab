# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Z.AI as the canonical producer identity for the represented GLM models published through current Z.AI documentation.
- Preserve the Z.AI model-domain view as navigation without duplicating model-owned facts.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep hosted platform, subscription, pricing, quota, and tool-integration facts outside the producer identity unless they are needed as current service documentation.
- Do not encode an additional Zhipu AI corporate relation from legacy wording without current authoritative evidence for the exact relationship.
- Include current Z.AI company and developer documentation as authoritative references.

## Validation

- The producer page does not duplicate GLM model facts.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
- Model-domain navigation resolves to its canonical catalog node.
- Mutable service properties are not presented as stable producer identity.
- Legacy naming is not converted into an unsupported canonical relation.
