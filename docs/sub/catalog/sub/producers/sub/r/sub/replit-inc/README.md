# Replit, Inc.

Replit, Inc. is the company named in Replit's terms and the producer of the represented Replit Agent experience. Agent works within the Replit development environment to plan, build and revise applications; generated applications, third-party model services and the surrounding hosting platform have separate roles. [Terms](https://replit.com/terms-of-service) · [Agent guide](https://docs.replit.com/learn/build-with-agent).

## Scope and operating boundaries

Review generated changes, tests and checkpoints before deploying an application or granting access to external data. Replit's terms updated August 3, 2026 direct Pro and Enterprise customers to a commercial agreement, so the applicable contract depends on the purchased offering. The producer identity alone does not establish an application's security, its third-party dependencies or the data permissions available to an agent. [Agent guide](https://docs.replit.com/learn/build-with-agent) · [Applicable agreements](https://replit.com/terms-of-service).
