# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Restate GmbH as the company behind the Restate source-available durable application runtime.
- Keep the company identity distinct from the self-managed Restate runtime and from Restate Cloud or other managed service surfaces.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party Restate legal and project-history references.

## Validation

- Restate GmbH is not conflated with the canonical Restate software entity or its hosted-service boundary.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.

## Source-backed identity and operating scope

The reader must preserve the following boundaries and their source basis.

Restate GmbH is the company identified in Restate's imprint and associated with the durable application runtime. The runtime repository's current license names both Restate Software, Inc. and Restate GmbH as licensors. The self-managed runtime, managed Restate Cloud and managed BYOC deployment remain distinct delivery arrangements. [Imprint](https://restate.dev/imprint) · [Runtime license](https://github.com/restatedev/restate/blob/main/LICENSE) · [Deployment options](https://docs.restate.dev/get-restate).

The server is source-available under BSL 1.1, with an additional use grant and a change to Apache-2.0 four years after a release. The grant permits production use subject to its restriction on a public Restate platform service; assess the exact release and intended offering against that text. Do not treat current server code as unconditionally Apache-licensed because it is publicly readable or because a different component uses another license. [Runtime license](https://github.com/restatedev/restate/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Reader projection

- Render every non-hidden current-entity relation in its semantic group, ordered by explicit order, canonical target display name and target ID. Preserve source/contributor, organizational and product distinctions.
- Include the official resources from canonical references as readable links. Navigation to a related catalog entry must resolve to its reader, not to metadata.
- Keep changing version, plan, availability and policy details qualified by the cited source; no general offline, license or privacy guarantee follows from producer attribution.
