# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Restate GmbH as the company behind the Restate open-source durable application runtime.
- Keep the company identity distinct from the self-managed Restate runtime and from Restate Cloud or other managed service surfaces.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party Restate legal and project-history references.

## Validation

- Restate GmbH is not conflated with the canonical Restate software entity or its hosted-service boundary.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
