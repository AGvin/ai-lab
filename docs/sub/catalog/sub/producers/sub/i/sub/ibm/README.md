# IBM

IBM is the corporate producer of the AI services and models represented below, including collaborative research artifacts.

IBM Research remains a separately represented research-group identity. The NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model retains its collaborative provenance. Model licenses, capabilities and deployment terms belong to their exact profiles; offering a third-party model through watsonx does not make IBM its author.
