# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify IBM as the corporate producer of the represented AI services and models, including collaborative research artifacts; do not reduce its portfolio to commercial services.
- Distinguish IBM corporate attribution from the separately represented IBM Research group and from collaborators such as NASA. Retain specific co-producer provenance without implying sole ownership.
- Keep model-version licenses, capabilities and deployment conditions with the exact product or model profile; access through watsonx does not transfer third-party model authorship.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.

## Validation

- Every rendered relation resolves to its canonical destination and has the exact reciprocal relation.
- Preserve IBM and IBM Research as separate canonical producer identities.
