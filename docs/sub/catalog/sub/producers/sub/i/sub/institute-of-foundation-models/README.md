# Institute of Foundation Models

The Institute of Foundation Models (IFM) at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) develops the K2 Horizon model family.

The university affiliation is current institutional context. It does not by itself establish a separate corporate legal identity or additional ownership relations. Model capabilities, licensing and deployment conditions belong to the individual model profiles.
