# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify InfiniFlow Inc. as the RAGFlow software producer and first-party identified RAGFlow hosted-service operator, grounded in the original `infiniflow/ragflow` GitHub repository and the publisher's `ragflow.io/about-service` operator disclosure.
- Separate the InfiniFlow organization, the self-hostable RAGFlow open-source framework, and the distinct operated `cloud.ragflow.io` hosted service. Only the released software is materialized by this package; company ownership does not automatically create a second cloud catalog record.
- State the vendor's published registered address **only if needed for legal scope**; do not infer business headquarters, employee nationality, effective control or beneficial ownership from a registered service address. Historical/current direct or indirect Russian stakes or control remain unverified and should not be asserted or cleared without documentary evidence.
- Do not confuse the software author's name or repository contributors with registered legal beneficial owners. Preserve the original first-party identity/operational distinction and the exact reciprocal `produces` edge.
- Avoid treating repository Apache-2.0 grant as a license to imported datasets, hosted RAGFlow Cloud, third-party model APIs or other external services.

## Validation

- The primary organization identifier is `catalog/producers/i/infiniflow-inc` and belongs exactly once to the I index.
- Its `produces` link resolves to the independently identifiable `catalog/software/application-frameworks/data-and-retrieval/ragflow` node and has a matching inverse.
- The operator source does not substitute for an official beneficial-owner share register or sanctions finding.
