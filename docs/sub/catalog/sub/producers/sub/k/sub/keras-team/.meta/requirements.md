# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Keras Team as the upstream team publishing and maintaining the Keras framework and independently represented Keras ecosystem libraries.
- Keep Keras team/project identity separate from Google/TensorFlow organizations and from backend frameworks used by Keras.
- Keep KerasHub and other separately branded Keras projects distinct when independently materialized.
- Render the standard entity-relations block from the validated current-entity relation projection and include official Keras and primary project repositories.

## Validation

- Producer relations resolve to the canonical software identities and each produced-by edge is reciprocal.
- Backend-provider relationships are not converted into Keras producer ownership.
- The entity-relations block matches the validated projection and every rendered destination resolves.
