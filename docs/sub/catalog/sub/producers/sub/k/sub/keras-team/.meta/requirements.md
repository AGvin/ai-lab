# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Keras Team as the upstream team publishing and maintaining the Keras framework.
- Keep Keras team/project identity separate from Google/TensorFlow organizations and from backend frameworks used by Keras.
- Keep KerasHub and other separately branded Keras projects distinct when independently materialized.

## Validation

- Producer relation resolves to the canonical Keras framework.
- Backend-provider relationships are not converted into Keras producer ownership.
