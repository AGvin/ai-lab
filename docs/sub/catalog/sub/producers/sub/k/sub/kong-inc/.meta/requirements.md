# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Kong Inc. as the organization behind Kong Gateway and the separately represented Kong AI Gateway.
- Keep the producer organization distinct from Kong Gateway software, Konnect, and other separately operated Kong product/service surfaces.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party legal, product, and repository references supporting provenance.
- Keep AI Gateway's Konnect-managed control plane distinct from its self-managed data plane and upstream AI providers; corporate production does not establish the operator of every deployment.

## Validation

- Kong Inc., Kong Gateway, and Konnect are not collapsed into one canonical entity.
- The producer relation does not imply hosted operation of every Kong Gateway deployment.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
