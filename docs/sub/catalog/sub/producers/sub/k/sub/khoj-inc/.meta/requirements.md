# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Khoj Inc. as the organization producing Khoj; distinguish producer continuity from the availability of the former hosted service.
- Keep the organization distinct from self-hostable Khoj software and the historical Khoj Cloud operating surface.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party Khoj legal and product references.
- Use the first-party April 15, 2026 sunset notice for Khoj Cloud lifecycle; older legal, repository, or documentation text that still advertises hosted access does not override that notice.

## Validation

- The organization and software identities are not collapsed.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
