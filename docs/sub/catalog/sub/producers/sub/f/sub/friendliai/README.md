# FriendliAI

**FriendliAI** develops inference infrastructure and operates [Friendli Model APIs](../../../../../services/sub/model-apis/sub/friendli-model-apis/), a hosted serverless model API. The company's [official brand guide](https://friendli.ai/brand-resources) distinguishes the company name from product names such as **Friendli Model APIs**, **Friendli Dedicated Endpoints** and **Friendli Container**.

## Corporate and product boundary

FriendliAI's [current contact page](https://friendli.ai/contact) describes **FriendliAI Corp.** with a San Francisco presence and a Seoul, Korea hub. Its [May 11, 2026 office announcement](https://friendli.ai/blog/friendliai-sf-office) describes an expansion in San Francisco and origins in research at Seoul National University. These public statements do not constitute a complete legal-entity, beneficial-ownership, jurisdictional-control or sanctions audit. Older FriendliAI Inc. references in separate container terms are not a substitute for the exact current hosted-service contract.

The organization operates the inference infrastructure; third-party model publishers retain their separate original-model provenance and licensing. Serving a model does not transfer original model training authorship, nor does it establish an upstream model's lawful licensing or trustworthy origin.
