# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Solo.io as the original producer of the agentgateway open-source project.
- Preserve current AAIF stewardship separately from historical Solo.io origin.
- Keep Solo Enterprise for agentgateway, kgateway, Istio-related products, and other Solo commercial offerings distinct from the organization profile and from the canonical open-source agentgateway identity.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

- Render the identity, provenance and operational context recorded in canonical `entity.identity_note`; keep version-sensitive claims and official resources aligned with the cited first-party sources.

## Validation

- `produces` is the inverse of agentgateway `produced-by`.
- Current stewardship is not incorrectly rewritten as Solo.io ownership after the project donation.
