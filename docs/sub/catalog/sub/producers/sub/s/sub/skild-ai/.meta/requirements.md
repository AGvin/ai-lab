# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Skild AI as the producer of the Skild Brain model family and its S1 model.
- Keep company/funding/deployment history subordinate to producer identity; model capabilities belong with canonical model entities.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation

- `produces` is the inverse of Skild Brain `produced-by`.
- Do not conflate Skild AI with NVIDIA merely because S1 is trained and deployed using NVIDIA infrastructure.
