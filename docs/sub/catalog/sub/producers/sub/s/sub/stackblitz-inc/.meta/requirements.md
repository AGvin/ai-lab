# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify StackBlitz, Inc. as the organization behind StackBlitz and the Bolt hosted application-building service.
- Preserve the distinction between the producer organization, StackBlitz development-environment products, and the canonical Bolt service entity.
- Link the canonical Bolt development-agent service profile through the `produces` relation.
- Include official StackBlitz website, privacy-policy, and Bolt documentation references sufficient to establish organization and product provenance.
- Keep broader corporate, staffing, pricing, and mutable commercial details outside this concise producer profile unless they become relevant to represented catalog entities.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse StackBlitz, Inc. and Bolt into one entity.
- The Bolt link resolves to the canonical hosted development-service node.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
