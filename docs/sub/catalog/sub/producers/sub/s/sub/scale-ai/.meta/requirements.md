# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Scale AI as the producer identity for Scale Data Engine and the first-party SWE-bench Pro benchmark release.
- Keep producer identity distinct from individual service modules and expert/data-production workflows.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

- Render the identity, provenance and operational context recorded in canonical `entity.identity_note`; keep version-sensitive claims and official resources aligned with the cited first-party sources.

## Validation

- Each `produces` relation is the exact inverse of its Scale Data Engine or SWE-bench Pro `produced-by` endpoint.
