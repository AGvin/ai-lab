# Salesforce

Salesforce is the organization producing Agentforce, its agent platform built on Salesforce Platform. The Agentforce product page establishes this provenance; the company identity remains broader than any individual agent, application or packaged workflow.

## Producer boundary

Agentforce connects business data, metadata and tools under the customer’s Salesforce configuration. Data 360/Data Cloud, MuleSoft, Slack and other Salesforce applications remain separate product surfaces. Assess subscriptions, enabled features, connector permissions and the actions an agent can perform for the intended deployment; a Salesforce relationship alone does not establish entitlement to every Agentforce capability.
