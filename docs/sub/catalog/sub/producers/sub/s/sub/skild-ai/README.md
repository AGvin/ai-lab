# Skild AI

Skild AI develops the represented Skild Brain robotics model family and its S1 model. Its technical accounts describe an in-house robotics effort, including S1’s learning from video demonstrations; the company remains distinct from its models and from robot manufacturers or compute suppliers.

## Producer boundary

Evaluate a concrete Skild model and deployment together with its robot hardware, control interfaces, operating limits and validation process. Demonstrations and architectural descriptions alone do not establish unrestricted weight access or a safety case for every environment. NVIDIA infrastructure use does not make NVIDIA the model producer. Availability, integration support and commercial conditions belong to the relevant model release and deployment agreement.
