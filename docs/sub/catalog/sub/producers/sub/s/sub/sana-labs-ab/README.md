# Sana Labs AB

Sana Labs AB is the organization identified in Sana’s current privacy notice and the producer identity for Sana Agents. Workday announced completion of its acquisition of Sana on November 4, 2025. Sana Labs AB, the Sana Agents service, Sana Learn and the Workday parent remain distinct identities.

## Producer boundary

The April 2026 privacy notice distinguishes customer processing from Sana’s controller role and describes limited joint-controller activities with Workday. Sana Agents product terms supplement the applicable master agreement and order, including third-party AI processing and restrictions on affiliate use. Confirm the service order, data-processing terms and connected-system permissions for a deployment; the acquisition does not make every Workday product part of Sana Agents.
