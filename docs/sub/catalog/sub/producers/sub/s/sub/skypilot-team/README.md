# SkyPilot Team

SkyPilot Team is the producer and maintainer identity for SkyPilot’s open-source compute orchestration software. The official history records its origin in UC Berkeley’s Sky Computing Lab and distinguishes the evolving team and commercial platform from that academic origin.

## Producer boundary

SkyPilot orchestrates infrastructure supplied or operated by others. Cloud vendors, Kubernetes and Slurm projects, GPU providers and model publishers retain their own identities and terms. The open-source software and commercial SkyPilot Platform have separate deployment and support choices. Operators must control cloud credentials, resource permissions, cluster access and spend; selecting infrastructure through SkyPilot does not transfer its ownership to the team.
