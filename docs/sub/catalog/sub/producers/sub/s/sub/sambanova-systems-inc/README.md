# SambaNova Systems, Inc.

**SambaNova Systems, Inc.** is an AI inference infrastructure and systems company founded in **2017** and headquartered in **San Jose, California**. Its [October 27, 2026 corporate announcement](https://sambanova.ai/press/sambanova-and-oracle-collaborate-to-bring-high-performance-inference-to-oracle-cloud-infrastructure) describes dedicated dataflow hardware, software and cloud services.

## Product and provenance boundary

SambaNova offers the **SambaCloud** remote model-inference API separately from self-managed or dedicated **SambaStack** environments and its own RDU chips. A customer choosing SambaCloud may access third-party trained models; the corresponding original model identity stays with those developers, not SambaNova.

The October 2026 collaboration with Oracle describes **planned infrastructure capacity**, not a production availability guarantee. Product-specific pricing, data handling, contracts, availability, licensing and deployment obligations need to be assessed at the exact service level. The separate [SambaStack on-premises agreement](https://sambanova.ai/sambastack-on-premises-terms-of-service) identifies the legal company but must not automatically be applied to SambaCloud cloud customers.

A complete current ultimate beneficial ownership, indirect holdings, geographic control or sanctions review was not carried out for this record.
