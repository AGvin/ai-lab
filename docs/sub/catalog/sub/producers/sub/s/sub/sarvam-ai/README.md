# Sarvam AI

Sarvam AI is the producer of the represented Sarvam model family. Its March 2026 release identifies the 30B and 105B models as an in-house training effort; current website terms identify the operating company as Axonwise Private Limited, doing business as Sarvam AI.

## Producer boundary

The released 30B/105B weights and their Apache 2.0 distribution are distinct from hosted APIs, Indus, Samvaad and Sarvam’s speech, translation and document products. Check the exact checkpoint license, runtime and hardware for local deployment, and the applicable account terms for hosted access. IndiaAI compute support and NVIDIA collaboration establish infrastructure contributions, not replacement model authorship.
