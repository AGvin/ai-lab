# Syntho

Syntho is the synthetic-data company that announced its acquisition of the MOSTLY AI brand on June 9, 2026, with the announced branding “MOSTLY AI, powered by Syntho.” The announcement concerns the brand and preserves the earlier company and product history as separate provenance.

## Producer boundary

The brand announcement does not establish acquisition of every historical MOSTLY AI software asset, platform, SDK or contract. No software-production or ownership relation is inferred here. For a purchase or migration, identify the actual platform, licensor, deployment agreement and support obligations from its own documentation and contract; a shared brand alone does not establish compatibility, data migration or continued service.
