# Snorkel AI

Snorkel AI is the organization producing Snorkel Flow, its platform for data-centric AI development. The current enterprise page identifies the product and its integration with existing data, models and infrastructure; those integrations remain separate from Snorkel’s producer identity.

## Producer boundary

Snorkel Flow is a commercial platform with deployment options that include customer infrastructure and Snorkel-hosted service. Its Kubernetes-based deployment and SDK do not imply that the full product shares the license of an open-source Snorkel library. Confirm the contracted deployment, data access, labeling workflows, model connections and support scope. Product version, security configuration and data-processing terms determine the operational boundary.
