# Scaleway SAS

**Scaleway SAS** is the French cloud and AI services company identified by its [official legal notice](https://www.scaleway.com/en/legal-notice/), which gives SIREN **433 115 904** and a registered office in Paris. Its [About Scaleway page](https://www.scaleway.com/en/about-us/) identifies Scaleway as a subsidiary of the **iliad Group**. These first-party statements establish the public company/operator identity, not an exhaustive present-day corporate-control or sanctions audit.

## Platform and service provenance

The company operates [Scaleway Generative APIs](../../../../../services/sub/model-apis/sub/scaleway-generative-apis/), a shared/serverless model API with separately available dedicated inference deployments. Those commercial deployment options and the former **Managed Inference** name are distinct from the independent source of any hosted model's trained weights.

The service provider hosts selected models and publishes account, API, billing and privacy controls. It does not thereby become the original developer of all underlying Qwen, DeepSeek, GLM, Meta or other third-party models; each has separate model licensing, original provenance, origin and potential supply-chain or ownership risks.

## Legal, data and ownership limits

The current [Generative APIs privacy documentation](https://www.scaleway.com/en/docs/generative-apis/reference-content/data-privacy/) distinguishes default zero retention from exceptional content capture of problematic requests up to two weeks, and longer-term anonymized operational metadata. French registration and a vendor's European hosting claims do not by themselves settle contract, data residency, all subsidiaries, indirect beneficial control or applicable sanctions. Confirm current product agreement/DPA and model rights before regulated or confidential use. No independent complete compliance, company ownership or API runtime audit was conducted.
