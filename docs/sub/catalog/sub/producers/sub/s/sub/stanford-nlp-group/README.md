# Stanford NLP Group

Stanford NLP Group is a research group at Stanford University that explicitly lists DSPy among the software it develops. DSPy is published through stanfordnlp and has a wider research-author and contributor history; the research group, university and software project are distinct.

## Producer boundary

DSPy’s MIT source distribution and its optimization methods are separate from model access, evaluation data and application deployment. Its license copyright names Stanford Future Data Systems, which should be preserved alongside the broader research provenance rather than treated as proof of exclusive group authorship. Users choose model endpoints, metrics and data; review those providers’ terms and the cost and privacy of optimization runs independently.
