# Solo.io

Solo.io is the original producer of agentgateway. Solo’s August 2025 announcement documents its donation to the Linux Foundation, and the project’s June 2026 announcement establishes current Agentic AI Foundation stewardship.

## Producer boundary

The original producer relationship and current foundation maintenance describe different roles. Solo Enterprise for agentgateway and other Solo products have their own support and commercial conditions; they are not interchangeable with the independently governed open-source project. Review the chosen distribution, upstream license, authentication policies and provider or tool credentials for a deployment. Donation does not transfer authorship of connected agents, models or services to Solo.
