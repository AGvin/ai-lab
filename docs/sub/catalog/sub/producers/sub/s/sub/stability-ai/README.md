# Stability AI

Stability AI is the producer of the represented Stable Diffusion family. Its release material identifies Stable Diffusion 3.5 and the Large, Large Turbo and Medium variants; hosting partners, runtimes and community fine-tunes remain distinct from original model production.

## Producer boundary

Licensing must be checked for the exact release and distribution. The 3.5 suite is included in Stability’s Community/Enterprise licensing program, whose commercial eligibility depends on organization revenue and applicable terms. This does not assign one license to every historical Stable Diffusion release. Local weights, Stability’s API and partner-hosted endpoints have separate hardware, processing, access and service conditions; model availability alone does not guarantee a deployment fits the intended workload.
