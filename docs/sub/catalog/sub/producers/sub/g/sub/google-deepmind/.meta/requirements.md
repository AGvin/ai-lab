# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Google DeepMind as a separate canonical producer organization when official model material attributes authorship or development specifically to Google DeepMind.
- Keep broader Google organization identity separate rather than silently collapsing Google DeepMind provenance into Google.
- Preserve Google DeepMind as a structural part of Google through the physically materialized `part-of` relation when the reciprocal Google `has-part` relation resolves successfully.
- Link Gemini and Gemma as represented model families with explicit Google DeepMind provenance through their physically materialized `produces` relations.
- Include the official Google DeepMind site, model-card index, and official Google organizational announcement supporting the structural relation.

## Content Specification

- Use `Google DeepMind` as the page title.
- Describe the organization concisely as the canonical producer identity for model provenance attributed specifically to Google DeepMind.
- Link the canonical Google producer from the physical `part-of` relation and link Gemini and Gemma from producer relations.
- Preserve the broader Google provenance boundary without interpreting `part-of` as ownership.

## Validation

- Google DeepMind and Google remain distinct canonical organization identities.
- The Google/Google DeepMind `has-part` / `part-of` relation pair is physically present at both endpoints and semantically consistent.
- The Google DeepMind/Gemini and Google DeepMind/Gemma `produces` / `produced-by` relation pairs are physically present at both endpoints and semantically consistent.
- The page does not duplicate Gemini, Gemma, or concrete-model facts.
- All related canonical links resolve successfully.
