# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Galileo Technologies, Inc. as the organization behind the Galileo AI evaluation, observability, and reliability platform.
- Keep producer identity distinct from the Galileo product and from named evaluators/models such as Luna where those remain product capabilities rather than separately selected catalog entities.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection and include the current official resources.
- Keep funding, staffing, customer metrics, and mutable corporate/commercial details out unless they become materially relevant to represented catalog entities.

- Distinguish Cisco's April 9, 2026 acquisition-intent announcement from its May 22 update confirming completion. Record completion as confirmed in that update without inferring an exact closing date or assignment of every customer contract.
- Retain the Galileo legal producer and platform identities; a corporate-affiliation statement does not make Cisco the original producer of every Galileo capability.

## Validation

- The organization and Galileo product are not collapsed into one entity.
- Product capabilities are not promoted into producer identities.
