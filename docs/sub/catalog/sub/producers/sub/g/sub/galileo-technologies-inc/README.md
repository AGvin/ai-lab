# Galileo Technologies, Inc.

Galileo Technologies, Inc. is the company identified in the [Galileo terms](https://galileo.ai/terms-of-service). It produces the Galileo evaluation, observability and guardrail platform described in the [product documentation](https://docs.galileo.ai/what-is-galileo).

The [official site](https://galileo.ai/) describes Galileo as part of Cisco. Cisco's [May 22, 2026 update](https://blogs.cisco.com/news/cisco-announces-the-intent-to-acquire-galileo) confirmed completion of its acquisition of Galileo, following the April 9 intent announcement. May 22 is the update date; the source does not state the exact closing date or assignment of every customer contract. The Galileo product and legal producer retain separate identities.
