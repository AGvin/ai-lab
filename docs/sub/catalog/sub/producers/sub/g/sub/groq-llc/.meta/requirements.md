# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Groq LLC as the organization behind GroqCloud and related Groq cloud services.
- Keep producer identity distinct from GroqCloud as a hosted service and from third-party model producers whose models are served through GroqCloud.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.
- Include current official Groq site, Privacy Policy, and Groq Services Agreement references that establish producer and service ownership.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse Groq LLC with GroqCloud or third-party hosted models.
- The `entity-relations` block matches validated current-entity relations and the produced service destination resolves.
- Legal naming remains grounded in current first-party policy/contract material.
