# Gray Swan AI

[Gray Swan AI](https://www.grayswan.ai/about) is an AI safety and security research organization developing adversarial evaluation and runtime control products for deployed models and agents. Its offerings include automated behavioral testing and enforcement, while its researchers contribute to academic research on AI security.

## AgentHarm research collaboration

Gray Swan AI and the **UK AI Safety Institute** (now UK AI Security Institute) jointly introduced [AgentHarm](../../../../../datasets/sub/agentharm/) in October 2024. The benchmark evaluates how tool-using AI agents respond to harmful multi-stage tasks, including refusal behavior. This original research is jointly attributed and does not establish that Gray Swan is the exclusive owner, operator or maintainer of all later grading implementations.

The AgentHarm dataset is **not unrestricted MIT**: the original publisher license contains an additional safety/security-purpose limitation. Research attribution does not override its license, permit using harmful test tasks against real systems, or demonstrate general safety certification.

## Product and security boundaries

Gray Swan's enterprise assessment and runtime-security offerings are separate from its research datasets and experimental benchmarks. Results depend on actual model versions, deployment context, evaluation scope and controls. Confirm permissions, provider processing terms, monitoring telemetry and privacy before using automated assessments; keep any malicious scenarios within an authorized sandbox.
