# ggml.ai

ggml.ai is the founding team and company behind llama.cpp. Its [company page](https://ggml.ai/) records the 2026 acquisition by Hugging Face, and the [team's announcement](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/19759) describes its continuing full-time development and maintenance role.

The announcement preserves the projects' open, community-driven development and technical autonomy. Company membership, software production and maintenance remain separate relationships.
