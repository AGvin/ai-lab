# ggml.ai

ggml.ai is the founding team/company behind llama.cpp and remains its full-time maintainer and support team. Hugging Face acquired ggml.ai in 2026, while first-party transition material states that the ggml and llama.cpp projects remain open, community-driven, and technically autonomous.

Company ownership, software production, software maintenance, and project/community autonomy remain distinct concepts.

## Produces and maintains

- [llama.cpp](../../../../../software/sub/inference-runtimes/sub/inference-engines/sub/llama-cpp/) — open-source local inference engine produced and maintained by ggml.ai.

## Owned by

- [Hugging Face, Inc.](../../../h/sub/hugging-face-inc/) — current owner of ggml.ai; this does not imply direct ownership or technical control of the llama.cpp project.
