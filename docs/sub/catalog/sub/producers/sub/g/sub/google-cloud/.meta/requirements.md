- Identify Google Cloud as one of the organizations that founded llm-d.
- Keep Google Cloud's llm-d founding role distinct from the broader canonical Google producer identity and from llm-d's current CNCF Sandbox governance.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current Google Cloud identity material and first-party llm-d provenance evidence.

## Validation

- Google Cloud is represented as a llm-d founding producer without implying sole ownership or sole current maintenance.
- The Google Cloud actor is not collapsed into unrelated Google products or services.
