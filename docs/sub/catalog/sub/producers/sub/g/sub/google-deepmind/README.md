Google DeepMind is represented as a separate canonical producer organization for model provenance that official model material attributes specifically to Google DeepMind.

## Models

- [Gemma](../../../../../models/sub/reference/sub/producers/sub/google/sub/gemma/) — open model family developed by Google DeepMind and other teams across Google.

## Provenance boundary

Google DeepMind and Google remain separate canonical organization identities. Current official Gemma documentation attributes Gemma development to Google DeepMind and other teams across Google, while current Gemma model cards identify Google DeepMind as author.
