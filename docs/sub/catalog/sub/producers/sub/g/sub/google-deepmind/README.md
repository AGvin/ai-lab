# Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind is the research organization formed from DeepMind and the Google Brain team, as described in Google's [organizational announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/april-ai-update/). Its [model cards](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/) and project sources preserve provenance attributed specifically to that unit.

Models, datasets and research software retain separate identities. The [Haiku project's Flax guidance](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm-haiku) records Flax's original Google Brain development and current Google DeepMind development. Structural inclusion in Google is distinct from a corporate ownership relation.
