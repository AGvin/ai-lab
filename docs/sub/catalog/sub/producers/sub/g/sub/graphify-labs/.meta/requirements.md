# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Graphify Labs as the producer organization behind the Graphify software product.
- Preserve `Graphify Labs` as the public organization identity supported by the official Graphify and GitHub organization surfaces; do not invent a legal suffix or corporate form without a current authoritative legal source.
- Keep the producer organization distinct from the Graphify software entity and from any future independently verified hosted or enterprise service entity.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Keep broader team, funding, customer, pricing, popularity, and other mutable company details outside this concise producer profile unless they become relevant to represented catalog entities.
- Include the current official Graphify company/about surface and GitHub organization from canonical entity metadata.

## Validation

- The page distinguishes Graphify Labs from the Graphify software product.
- No unsupported legal company form is asserted.
- The `produces` relation resolves to Graphify and is matched by Graphify's inverse `produced-by` relation.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
