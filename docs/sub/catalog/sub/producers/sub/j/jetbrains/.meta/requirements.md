# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify JetBrains as curator and distributor of its officially filtered and verified Agent Skills collection, not as the original author of every upstream SKILL.md package. Preserve upstream attribution and link/source metadata; independent upstream collections remain their own canonical owners.
- Keep producer-specific repositories, distribution channels and skill inventory freshness-sensitive; distinguish verified first-party guidance from untrusted agent-generated instructions.

## Validation

- The `produces` link reciprocates the collection's `produced-by`.
- Product ownership and upstream source authorship are not inaccurately conflated.
