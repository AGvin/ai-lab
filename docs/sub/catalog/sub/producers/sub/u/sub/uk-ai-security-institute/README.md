# UK AI Security Institute

The UK AI Security Institute is a UK government research organization within the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology. Inspect AI's official site attributes the evaluation framework's development to the institute and Meridian Labs.

## Producer and operating boundary

The institute's producer role does not make evaluated models or third-party benchmarks government products. An Inspect result depends on its task, model, solver, scorer, tools and execution conditions; it is not a general certification of model safety.
The institute also jointly developed the original 2024 [AgentHarm](../../../../../datasets/sub/agentharm/) evaluation dataset with Gray Swan AI researchers. It is not solely government-authored: copyright and original paper credit both organizations, and the dataset's safety/security-purpose-restricted license differs from Inspect AI's permissive software license. The AgentHarm result depends on released split, model/tool environment and grader revision and is not a system-wide safety certification.
