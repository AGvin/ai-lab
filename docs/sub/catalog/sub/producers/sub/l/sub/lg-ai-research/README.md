# LG AI Research

LG AI Research is the research organization producing the K-EXAONE model family. The official K-EXAONE release repository establishes model provenance.

The organization is distinct from other LG group companies, hosted applications, exact checkpoints, and third-party model conversions or deployments. Model access and licensing must be checked for the specific release; producer identity does not provide one license for every artifact.
