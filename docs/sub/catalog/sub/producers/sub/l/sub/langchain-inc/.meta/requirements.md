# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify LangChain Inc. as the organization behind the LangChain, LangGraph, and Deep Agents open-source projects and the related LangSmith platform.
- Keep producer identity distinct from individual software/framework and hosted-platform identities.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include official LangChain website, legal terms, and GitHub organization references sufficient to establish provenance.
- Keep funding, staffing, pricing, platform limits, and other mutable corporate or commercial details outside this concise producer profile unless they become relevant to represented catalog entities.
- Keep the open-source framework licenses separate from LangSmith platform rights; the self-hosted LangSmith offering is governed by its Enterprise licensing and deployment contract.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse LangChain Inc., LangChain, LangGraph, and LangSmith into one catalog entity.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
