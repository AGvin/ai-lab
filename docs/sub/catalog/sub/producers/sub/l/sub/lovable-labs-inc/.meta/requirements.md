# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Lovable Labs Incorporated as the organization operating the Lovable application-building platform and related services.
- Keep producer identity distinct from the Lovable hosted development-agent service entity and from customer-generated applications/code.
- Link the canonical Lovable development-agent service profile through the `produces` relation.
- Include current official Lovable site, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy references sufficient to establish provenance and legal identity.
- Keep broader staffing, funding, pricing, and mutable corporate details outside this concise producer profile unless they become relevant to represented catalog entities.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse Lovable Labs Incorporated, the Lovable service, and customer-created applications into one entity.
- The Lovable link resolves to the canonical hosted development-service node.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
