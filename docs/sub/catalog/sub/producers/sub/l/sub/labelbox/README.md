# Labelbox

Labelbox is the organization behind the Labelbox AI data-development platform. Its producer identity is distinct from the managed service, customer datasets, and external expert or model providers integrated into its workflows.

Data storage and access depend on the selected service configuration. The producer relation does not establish ownership of customer datasets or external providers.
