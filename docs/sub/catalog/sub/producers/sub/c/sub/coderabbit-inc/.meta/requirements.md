# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify CodeRabbit Inc. as the organization behind the CodeRabbit code-review and development-workflow product.
- Keep the producer organization distinct from the CodeRabbit software/tool profile.
- Link the canonical CodeRabbit code-review-tool profile from the physically materialized `produces` relation when the reciprocal CodeRabbit `produced-by` relation resolves successfully.
- Include official CodeRabbit site, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy references sufficient to establish provenance and current legal identity.
- Keep broader corporate, staffing, funding, pricing, and other mutable business details outside this concise producer profile unless they become relevant to represented catalog entities.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse CodeRabbit Inc. and CodeRabbit into one entity.
- The CodeRabbit Inc./CodeRabbit `produces` / `produced-by` relation pair is physically present at both endpoints and semantically consistent.
- The CodeRabbit link resolves to the canonical code-review-tool node.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
