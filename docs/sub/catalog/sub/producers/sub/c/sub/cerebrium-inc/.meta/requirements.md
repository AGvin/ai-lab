# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Cerebrium's terms identify Cerebrium Inc. as the service operator. Its documentation and company page connect that identity to the Cerebrium infrastructure platform.
- The producer is the company; the catalog service is serverless compute infrastructure for deployed AI applications. Running user-selected code or models does not make the operator their original producer.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection; preserve the company and its represented product identities.
- Include official legal and product documentation references sufficient to establish the represented producer role.
- Keep release-specific specifications, pricing, deployment entitlements, service limits and detailed operating instructions in the relevant product entries.

## Validation

- The page uses the canonical producer identity and does not duplicate a company under its product or trading name.
- Every rendered relation matches entity metadata and resolves to an existing canonical target with the required inverse relation.
- Producer validation does not automatically validate the versions, deployments or full portfolio of its represented products.
- Official resource links match the current canonical entity metadata.
