# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Credo AI as the producer identity for Credo AI Platform.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

- Identify the organization as the vendor behind the separately represented Credo AI Platform, using the official company and product pages.
- Describe inventory, assessment and governance functions as platform capabilities without asserting blanket certification or guaranteed compliance for every customer deployment.
- Keep producer identity distinct from the models, agents, vendors and customer AI systems assessed by the platform.

## Validation

- `produces` is the inverse of Credo AI Platform `produced-by`.
