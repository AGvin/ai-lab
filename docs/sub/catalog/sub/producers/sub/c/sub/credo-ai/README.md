# Credo AI

Credo AI is the organization behind the Credo AI Platform. Its [company page](https://www.credo.ai/company/aboutus) establishes that role, and the [product page](https://www.credo.ai/product) describes the platform's AI inventory, assessment, monitoring and governance workflows.

The company and platform retain separate catalog identities. Governance workflow support does not make the producer the owner of assessed AI systems or establish universal compliance for every deployment.
