# Documentation Requirements

- Identify ClearML Inc. as the company producing and maintaining the ClearML open-source AI/MLOps platform.
- Keep the company identity distinct from the ClearML software identity and from commercial/hosted product layers.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party ClearML site, legal, repository, and company references.

- Corporate producer/maintenance responsibility is not used to collapse open-source and commercial/hosted surfaces.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
