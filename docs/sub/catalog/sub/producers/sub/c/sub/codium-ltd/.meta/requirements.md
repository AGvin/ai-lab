## Requirements

- Identify Codium Ltd. as the legal organization operating under the Qodo brand.
- Keep the producer organization distinct from the Qodo code-review software/service product and from individual review-agent or integration surfaces.
- Link the canonical Qodo code-review-tool profile from the physically materialized `produces` relation when the reciprocal Qodo `produced-by` relation resolves successfully.

- Keep unrelated corporate, staffing, funding, pricing, and other mutable organization details outside this concise producer profile.

- The page does not collapse Codium Ltd. and Qodo into one entity.
- The Codium Ltd./Qodo `produces` / `produced-by` relation pair is physically present at both endpoints and semantically consistent.
- The Qodo link resolves to the canonical code-review-tool profile.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
