# ByteDance Seed

ByteDance Seed is the first-party research team publishing the Seedream image-generation and Seedance audiovisual video-generation model families represented in the catalog.

The team identity, the model family, hosted endpoints, downstream applications, and partner distribution are separate concerns. Distribution through a third party does not change Seedream’s producer.
