# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements
- Identify Browserbase, Inc. from current first-party service terms and preserve the exact producer relation to the Browserbase service.
- Keep the company distinct from its hosted browser infrastructure, customer agents, browser engines and model providers.
- Do not conflate Browserbase with Browser Use or Browserless or create duplicate producers for interface and deployment variations.
- Render the validated produces/produced-by relations to the existing canonical Browserbase service and separately packaged Stagehand agent framework.
- Establish the separate Browserbase-maintained Stagehand SDK authorship with the first-party repository and 2026 product changelog; the SDK is not an additional Browserbase-hosted service identity.

## Validation
- The legal identity is supported by current official terms and the reciprocal service relation resolves.
- The producer reader distinguishes service operation from authorship of customer agents or upstream browser/model projects.
