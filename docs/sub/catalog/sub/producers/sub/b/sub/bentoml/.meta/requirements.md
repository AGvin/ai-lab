# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify BentoML as the first-party organization/publishing identity behind the BentoML software platform.
- Keep producer identity distinct from BentoML software and from separately operated hosted delivery such as BentoCloud.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.
- Include the official BentoML site and first-party repository as identity/research references.
- Preserve the February 2026 announcement that BentoML joined Modular through a strategic product acquisition; distinguish this dated affiliation from the originating publishing identity and retain the Apache-2.0 project boundary.
- Include the first-party BentoML and Modular acquisition announcements without inferring an unsupported legal suffix or changing product provenance solely because of the corporate transition.
- Preserve the February 2026 BentoML team/product acquisition by Modular and the announced open-source continuity without converting that account into an unsupported legal-entity owned-by edge.
- If current affiliation is described, include the completed July 2026 acquisition of Modular by Qualcomm as separate, dated corporate context; it does not replace BentoML’s original software provenance.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse producer, self-managed software, and hosted-service identities.
- The `entity-relations` block matches validated current-entity relations and the produced software destination resolves.
- Official resource links match canonical entity metadata.
- The reader distinguishes original publishing provenance, the team/product transaction, and Modular’s later corporate acquisition.
