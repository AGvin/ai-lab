# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify ByteDance Seed as the canonical producer team for the represented Seedream model family.
- Keep ByteDance product surfaces, hosted endpoints, downstream integrations, and partner distributions separate from model provenance.
- Preserve current first-party naming without inferring a stronger legal/organizational relation than the source establishes.

## Validation

- Seedream provenance resolves bidirectionally.
- Hosting/distribution does not overwrite producer identity.
