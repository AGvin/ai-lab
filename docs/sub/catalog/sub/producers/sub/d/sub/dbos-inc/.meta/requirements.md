# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify DBOS, Inc. as the company behind the DBOS open-source durable-execution software family.
- Keep the company identity distinct from the self-managed DBOS libraries and from DBOS Cloud or other hosted service surfaces.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current first-party DBOS company, legal, and product-history references.

## Validation

- DBOS, Inc. is not conflated with the DBOS software entity or its hosted-service boundary.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
