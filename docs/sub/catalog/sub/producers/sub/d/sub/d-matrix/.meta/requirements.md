# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify d-Matrix as the organization developing and publishing the Corsair inference hardware platform and supporting software.
- Keep the company distinct from the Corsair product, the models run on it, and customer or partner deployments.
- Keep hardware specifications, runtime compatibility, availability, and reported performance on the linked hardware record.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.
- Include the selected official company, identity, and product documentation references that support this boundary.

## Validation

- The company identity and its represented producing or operating role are supported by current first-party sources.
- Every visible relation resolves to the canonical target and has the matching inverse; relation type and target are preserved.
