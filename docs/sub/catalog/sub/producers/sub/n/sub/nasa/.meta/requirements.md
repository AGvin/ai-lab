# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements
- Identify NASA as a producer/collaborator for NASA-released AI artifacts represented in the catalog.
- Keep mission/dataset facts with their canonical artifacts and do not infer exclusive authorship for joint NASA-industry research releases.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.

## Validation
- `produces` includes the inverse of the Lunar Foundation Model `produced-by` relation.
