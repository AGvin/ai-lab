# NASA

NASA is the National Aeronautics and Space Administration. Its announcement attributes the lunar foundation model to a collaboration with IBM Research and academic institutions. [NASA announcement](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/artificial-intelligence-lunar-foundation-model/).

## Scope and operating boundaries

NASA mission data, the jointly released model, and a downstream deployment are separate artifacts and responsibilities. Agency participation does not establish exclusive authorship or ownership, nor does a research release establish validation for operational mission decisions. [Joint release](https://research.ibm.com/blog/nasa-ibm-lunar-foundation-model).
