# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Netflix, Inc. as Metaflow's origin organization, where Metaflow was developed internally before Netflix open-sourced it in 2019.
- Keep Netflix origin provenance distinct from later community use and from third-party/commercial hosted Metaflow products.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current Netflix legal identity and first-party Metaflow origin-history references.

## Validation

- Netflix is not presented as the only current deployment environment or as an operator of unrelated third-party Metaflow services.
- Origin provenance and later ecosystem/commercial layers are not conflated.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
