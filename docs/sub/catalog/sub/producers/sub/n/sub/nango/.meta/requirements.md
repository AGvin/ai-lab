# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Nango as the canonical first-party organization/brand steward for its actual published products, without assuming ownership of third-party tools, apps, or cloud APIs integrated by them.
- Preserve distinct consumer software identities and exact inverse producer relations for Nango's source-available integration platform.
- Keep producer references anchored to official domains and maintainer repositories, with plan/licensing/deployment rights verified at first-party sources.

## Validation

- Every `produces` target has a matching exact `produced-by` inverse at its canonical software identity.
- No duplicate company producer is created for separate deployment modes or SDK package distributions.

## Producer decision boundaries

- Identify the company named in the service terms without creating a duplicate producer for its brand or deployment options.
- Distinguish Nango-operated BYOC from customer-operated self-hosting, and preserve the source-available license and enterprise agreement boundaries.
- Render the official-resources block from canonical references supporting the producer identity and stated boundaries.

These boundaries are grounded in the first-party references recorded in the current entity. Detailed product behavior remains with each related artifact.
