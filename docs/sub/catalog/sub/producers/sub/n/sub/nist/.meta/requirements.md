# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Record one original **National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)** organization/publisher, the non-regulatory scientific standards agency within the **U.S. Department of Commerce**, as described on its first-party About page. Not a private corporation, commercial AI model author or external accreditation body.
- Original source-backing includes NIST **AI 100-1 / AI RMF 1.0** published January 26, 2023 and separate **NIST AI 600-1 / GenAI Profile** published July 26, 2024. Create exactly two `produces` relations to those independent canonical standards/guide artifacts with exact `produced-by` inverses.
- Respect NIST's current announcement that **AI RMF 1.0 is being revised**, without inventing a published successor, declaring AI 600-1 a new framework version, or treating the living Playbook as another accepted 2024 publication.
- Distinguish **voluntary risk-management guidance** from binding legal regulation, mandatory certifications, original model training, compliance validation or endorsement of third-party commercial offerings.
- Federal agency affiliation is not a private/shareholder beneficial-ownership claim. Current U.S. executive order legal force and international compliance must be assessed separately from the publication's original policy motivation.
- Cite current NIST About and AI RMF official hub and both dated publication records; keep original report dates/version identifiers immutable.

## Validation

- One unique NIST original producer, two distinct published spec relations with inverse verification.
- No repeat/duplicate AI 100-1 versus AI 600-1 identity; no legal/certification implications invented.
- Scoped metadata and link checks do not constitute complete schema, rendered-reader, current legal or CI verification.
