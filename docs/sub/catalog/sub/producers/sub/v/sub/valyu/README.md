# Valyu

Valyu operates a hosted retrieval and research platform combining open-web information with specialized and permissioned sources. Its Search, Contents, Answer and DeepResearch workflows belong to the service rather than the producer organization.

## Producer and operating boundary

Access to licensed material, permitted reuse of outputs and citations, processing terms and retention depend on the source and customer agreement. Valyu supplies retrieval and synthesis; it does not thereby author the underlying sources, and its published benchmark claims are first-party evidence.
