# Vast.ai Inc.

Vast.ai Inc. is the organization identified by the platform's published terms for the Vast.ai compute marketplace. Customers rent capacity offered by independent hosts; the company and the hosted marketplace remain separate catalog identities.

## Producer and operating boundary

Host verification concerns particular machines and operating conditions. Physical security, compliance, networking and reliability can differ between offers and service tiers. Review the selected host and contract instead of treating marketplace availability as a universal workload or security guarantee.
