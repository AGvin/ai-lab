# Vibrant Labs

Vibrant Labs is the organization behind Ragas and its current vibrantlabsai upstream repository. The company's account of its founders connects the organization to Ragas while also describing broader research work.

## Producer and operating boundary

Ragas combines model-based and traditional evaluation metrics and test-data workflows. Judge selection, prompts, datasets and provider processing affect the resulting evidence; a toolkit score is not independent quality certification. The producer profile does not absorb every benchmark or third-party model used in an evaluation.
