## Requirements

- Identify Anyscale, Inc. as the organization that introduced and developed the early Ray Serve serving library within the Ray project.
- Keep Ray Serve product provenance distinct from current Ray Foundation / PyTorch Foundation neutral governance and from Anyscale's separately operated commercial services.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current Anyscale legal identity and first-party Ray Serve provenance evidence.

## Validation

- Anyscale is represented as Ray Serve's producer without implying sole current ownership or governance of Ray.
- Ray Foundation hosting and Anyscale commercial operation are not conflated with Ray Serve software identity.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
