# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Andon Labs as the producer of the represented real-world/agentic evaluation benchmarks.
- Keep benchmark tasks, current leaderboards, evaluated model scores, autonomous-organization experiments, commercial products, and research collaborations with their applicable owners rather than duplicating them into the producer profile.
- Preserve current first-party evaluation/publication sources and only validated producer relations.
- Treat live leaderboard contents and current real-world demonstrations as freshness-sensitive.

## Validation

- Andon Labs remains distinct from Anthropic and other model providers that collaborate on or are evaluated by its benchmarks.
- Every produced benchmark relation resolves to a canonical dataset/benchmark node.
