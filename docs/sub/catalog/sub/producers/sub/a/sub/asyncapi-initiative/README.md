# AsyncAPI Initiative

The AsyncAPI Initiative publishes and maintains the AsyncAPI Specification and its surrounding open-source ecosystem under the Linux Foundation umbrella.

The specification originated at Hitch with Fran Méndez and later developed into an initiative with open governance. The initiative, the normative specification, tools, and messaging transports such as MQTT or Kafka remain separate identities.
