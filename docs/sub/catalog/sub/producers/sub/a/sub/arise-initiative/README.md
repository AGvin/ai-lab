# ARISE Initiative

ARISE Initiative — Advancing Robot Intelligence through Simulated Environments — is the project/community publishing identity behind robosuite.

The official project account records Stanford SVL’s origin role and contributions involving Stanford SVL, UT RPL, and NVIDIA GEAR. Those laboratory and contributor roles do not establish exclusive ownership by one institution or a separately incorporated ARISE company.
