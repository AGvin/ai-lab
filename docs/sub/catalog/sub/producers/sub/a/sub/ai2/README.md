# Ai2

**Ai2 (Allen Institute for AI)** is a Seattle-based nonprofit AI research institute founded by Paul Allen in 2014. The [official About page](https://allenai.org/about) describes its research work across open models, datasets, robotics, scientific discovery and related AI infrastructure.

## Producer boundary

Ai2 publishes the original **2018 Ai2 Reasoning Challenge (ARC)**, **2018 OpenBookQA** **2019 DROP** and **2021 Qasper** datasets. DROP requires discrete reasoning over reading passages; it is not another ARC science-question variant. This benchmark is not ARC-AGI and does not belong to the separately represented ARC Prize Foundation. Original research authors, partner laboratories, question copyright, software distributions and downstream mirrors retain their distinct ownership and licensing boundaries.

Ai2's public descriptions do not by themselves establish a complete current indirect ownership or sanctions audit; the publisher identity is not a blanket declaration about all third-party material in its datasets.
