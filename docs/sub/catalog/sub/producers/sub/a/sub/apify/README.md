# Apify

Apify is the first-party operator of the hosted Apify web-data platform.

The organization and its hosted service are distinct from individual Actors, SDKs, customer workflows, and the websites supplying data. The marketplace contains both first-party and third-party Actors; publishing or operating the platform does not transfer the authorship of every Actor to Apify.
