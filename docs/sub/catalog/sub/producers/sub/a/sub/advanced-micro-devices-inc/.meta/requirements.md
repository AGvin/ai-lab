# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) as the producer of the represented processors, accelerators, developer hardware, and AMD software projects.
- Keep the producer identity distinct from individual accelerator products, ROCm/runtime software, complete server platforms, cloud services, and third-party systems that integrate AMD hardware.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from validated current-entity relations.
- Include current first-party AMD site and legal/privacy references that establish the organization identity.
- Cover the represented processor, accelerator, developer-platform and software relations, rather than limiting AMD identity to the Instinct family.

## Validation

- The page does not collapse AMD with one product family or with ROCm software.
- The `entity-relations` block matches validated current-entity relations and the produced hardware destination resolves.
- Legal naming remains grounded in current AMD first-party material.
- The producer reader includes every validated AMD relation, with hardware and software identities kept distinct.
