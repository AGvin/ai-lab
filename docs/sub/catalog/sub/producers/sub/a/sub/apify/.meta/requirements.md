# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Apify as the first-party operator of its respective hosted web-data service, without assuming ownership of all external data, evaluated models, customers or third-party integrations.
- Distinguish the producing organization/brand from the independently cataloged service and from individual SDK, marketplace, API processor or distribution-channel components.
- Preserve the official first-party identity source, published product/lifecycle context and exact reciprocal producer relation.
- Keep first-party platform operation distinct from the authorship of individual third-party Actors and the ownership of collected source content.

## Validation

- Publisher and service refer to the same first-party website and maintained service interface.
- The exact `produces` relation reciprocates the matching service's `produced-by` relation.
