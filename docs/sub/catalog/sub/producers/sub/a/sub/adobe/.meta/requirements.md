# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Adobe as the producer of the represented Adobe Firefly model family.
- Keep the Firefly app, Creative Cloud integrations, Firefly Services APIs, partner-model hosting, custom models, plans, and credits separate from Adobe-trained model identity.
- Preserve the distinction between Adobe Firefly models and third-party models surfaced inside Firefly.
- Use first-party Firefly material to distinguish Adobe-trained models from the partner models offered through Firefly.

## Validation

- Firefly provenance resolves to Adobe.
- Partner models in Adobe products retain upstream producer identity.
