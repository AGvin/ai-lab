# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Alibaba Cloud as a canonical producer organization in the catalog.
- Explain its organizational relationship to Alibaba Group, Qwen Team, and Wan Team using the validated first-party hierarchy rather than inferred legal ownership.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.

## Content Specification

- Use `Alibaba Cloud` as the page title.
- Start with a concise description focused on its role in the documented producer hierarchy.
- Include the official Alibaba Cloud site and the authoritative Alibaba Group business page supporting the organizational context.
- Preserve Qwen Team and Wan Team as distinct has-part relationships, grounded in their first-party organization profiles, with exact part-of inverses.
- Keep Alibaba Cloud Skills and the Model Studio service separate from team identities; third-party models offered through Model Studio retain their own producers.

## Validation

- The page does not render `owned-by`, `owns`, `parent-of`, or `child-of` unless separately supported by validated evidence.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
- The description does not duplicate detailed Qwen model-family documentation.
