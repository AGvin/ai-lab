## Source-backed identity and operating scope

The reader must preserve the following boundaries and their source basis.

Peter Steinberger, also known as steipete, is the original creator associated with OpenClaw. The current upstream README credits him together with the community and describes the independent OpenClaw Foundation as the organization supporting the core team and signing releases. Creator provenance, community contribution and foundation governance remain distinct. [OpenClaw repository](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw).

OpenClaw's MIT software license does not determine the permissions of its connected services or models. Deployment requires choosing the operator trust boundary: its security documentation assumes one trusted operator or mutually trusting team, and recommends separate gateways or hosts for users who do not trust one another. [Repository](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) · [Gateway trust model](https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/security/trust-model).
