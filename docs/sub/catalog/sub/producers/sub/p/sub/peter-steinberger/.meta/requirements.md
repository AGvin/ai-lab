# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Peter Steinberger (`steipete`) as the original creator/producer of OpenClaw together with the project's community.
- Keep creator provenance distinct from current OpenClaw Foundation governance/stewardship and from the wider contributor community.
- Render the standard `entity-relations` block from the validated current-entity relation projection.
- Include current upstream OpenClaw repository/release references.

## Validation

- Peter Steinberger is not presented as the current governance organization or as the sole author of all community contributions.
- Creator provenance and current foundation stewardship remain distinct.
- The `entity-relations` block matches the validated current-entity relation projection and every rendered destination resolves to a canonical node.
