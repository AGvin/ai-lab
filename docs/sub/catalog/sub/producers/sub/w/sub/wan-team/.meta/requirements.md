# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Identify Wan Team as the Alibaba Cloud model team behind the Wan large-scale generative-model family.
- Preserve the validated organizational relation to Alibaba Cloud without inventing a stronger ownership/legal relation than first-party sources establish.
- Keep concrete model versions, hosted Model Studio serving, creative tools, pricing, and deployment details with their model/service owners rather than duplicating them in the producer profile.
- Preserve the team's official Wan site and GitHub organization as identity/provenance evidence.
- Render the standard entity-relations block from validated current relations.

## Validation

- Wan Team remains distinct from Alibaba Cloud as the broader organization and from Alibaba Cloud Model Studio as a service.
- Every produced-model relation resolves to a canonical model node.
- Model capability details are not duplicated into the producer profile.

## Model Navigation

- Keep the produced-model navigation synchronized with the current canonical relation projection as model-series entries are added; retain capability, license, deployment, and data-handling detail on the linked model pages.

## Producer evidence and reader projection

- Render the canonical entity name as the title and the source-backed `identity_note` as concise identity and operating-boundary prose.
- Preserve the existing ID, type and aliases. Historical authorship, current stewardship, corporate ownership and service operation must remain distinct where the note distinguishes them.
- Render every visible validated canonical relation exactly once through `entity-relations`, with canonical target names and resolving relative links. Distinct relation types to one target remain distinct rows.
- Render the canonical official references with descriptive labels; keep release, licensing, access and data-processing claims bounded to the cited sources.
- Keep detailed model, software, service and benchmark behavior on the related owner pages; do not infer new catalog membership from a provider inventory.

## Review checks

- The complete reader agrees with the canonical note and relation projection, including operational caveats.
- Every direct relation has its exact canonical inverse and every rendered destination resolves to its canonical owner.
