# Weights & Biases, Inc.

Weights & Biases, Inc. is the original producer of W&B Weave and has been owned by CoreWeave since the acquisition completed on 5 May 2025. Corporate ownership and current CoreWeave Forge branding remain distinct from Weave's product provenance.

## Producer and operating boundary

Current Weave documentation names CoreWeave Agent Lens as its successor and says both already receive agent-tracing data. That statement does not establish migration of every SDK or evaluation feature. Review trace content, destinations and retention for the selected hosted, dedicated or self-managed deployment.
