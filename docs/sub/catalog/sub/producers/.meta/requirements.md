# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Introduce the canonical producer catalog and provide the standard child-navigation block for the available alphabetical producer indexes.
- Describe producer scope without implying that every producer has the same organizational or product role.

## Content Specification

- Use `Producers` as the page title.
- Explain concisely that canonical producer profiles may represent organizations, teams, individuals, and other stable producing, publishing, maintaining, or operating identities.

## Validation

- The child-navigation block matches the validated materialized direct-child projection and every destination resolves to a canonical alphabetical producer index.
- The page contains no temporary-summary or placeholder wording.
- The page does not duplicate individual producer profiles.
