# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Reasoning and Decision Making as the learning area for general methods of deriving conclusions, searching possibilities, constructing plans, satisfying constraints, and choosing actions independently from one model, agent, product, or workflow implementation.
- Keep agent-specific planning/execution control with Agents and Automation; link generic reasoning/planning prerequisites rather than duplicating them in the agent subtree.
- Keep model training optimization with Machine Learning/Models and system resource optimization with AI Engineering; this area owns solution/search/decision methods rather than every use of the word optimization.
- Explain that the current materialized subset focuses on `planning-and-scheduling/` because planner/executor legacy material has source-backed general planning pedagogy ready for migration.
- Do not imply that unmaterialized selected reasoning branches are absent from the logical architecture; standard navigation reflects only physical children.
- Apply progressive depth from intuitive problem/goal/state/action models through formal representations, algorithms/constraints/uncertainty, practical consequences, failure modes, and evaluation where relevant.
- Keep concrete model/provider/tool behavior, mutable framework APIs, and dated benchmark/evidence claims with their catalog/evidence owners rather than freezing them as general reasoning truth.

## Validation

- General planning/reasoning remains implementation-independent and is not defined as an agent-only capability.
- Current navigation exposes only materialized selected children.
- The area does not absorb model-training optimization, application infrastructure, or concrete framework behavior merely because those systems use planning or search.
