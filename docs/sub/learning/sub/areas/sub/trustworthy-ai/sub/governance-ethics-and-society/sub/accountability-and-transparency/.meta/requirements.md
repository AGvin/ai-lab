# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Teach Accountability and Transparency as making consequential AI-assisted work inspectable enough to understand what evidence, tools, state transitions, approvals, and responsible decisions supported an outcome.
- Prefer structured plans, workflow state, tool calls/results, retrieved evidence, calculations, validation results, approvals, and source references as audit artifacts rather than treating verbose reasoning-like prose as proof of internal model causation.
- Check material intermediate claims under the same evidence standard as final conclusions; a detailed narrative can still contain speculation or errors.
- Use concise explanations of assumptions, evidence, calculations, and conclusions when they improve review while avoiding unnecessary exposure of sensitive internal/context material.
- Keep project-specific audit policy, regulatory requirements, retention rules, and concrete logging systems with their current authoritative owners.

## Validation

- A reasoning narrative is not represented as a faithful causal trace of model computation.
- Auditability relies on externally inspectable evidence where possible.
- Transparency does not require unrestricted disclosure of sensitive prompt/context material.
