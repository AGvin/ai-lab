# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Teach Privacy as protection of personal, confidential, proprietary, or otherwise sensitive information across AI data, prompts/context, training, inference, logs, traces, tools, and outputs.
- Keep security attacks with Security and dataset-specific rights/privacy workflow with Data and Knowledge while linking overlapping concerns.
- This source-backed package materializes `data-privacy/`; other selected privacy siblings remain logical until substantive content justifies physical creation.
- Minimize unnecessary exposure and retention of sensitive material, especially in prompts, visible reasoning-like traces, logs, intermediate artifacts, and debugging data.
- Keep concrete legal requirements, organizational policies, retention periods, provider data handling, and product privacy controls with their current authoritative owners.

## Validation

- Privacy is treated as an end-to-end information-flow concern rather than only a training-data property.
- More verbose traces are not assumed to be better when they expose unnecessary sensitive content.
- Current navigation exposes only materialized selected children.
