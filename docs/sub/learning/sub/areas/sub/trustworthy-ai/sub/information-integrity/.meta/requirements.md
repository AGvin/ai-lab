# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Information Integrity as learning about truthfulness, grounding, attribution, provenance, evidence quality, and manipulation risks in AI-generated information.
- Materialize `hallucinations/`, `grounding/`, and `citations-and-provenance/` because each has independent source-backed legacy material; leave other selected children unmaterialized until standalone content exists.
- Keep generic retrieval mechanics with Data and Knowledge, formal evaluation methodology with Evaluation and Research, and concrete evidence records with their evidence owners.
- Teach claim support and attribution at the granularity required by the risk: source identity/version, specific support location, direct versus derived support, conflicts, uncertainty, and source authority/freshness where material.
- Separate factual evidence from instruction authority; content used as evidence does not automatically gain control over application behavior.

## Validation

- Hallucination, grounding, citation, provenance, retrieval, and source authority are related but not collapsed into one concept.
- Evidence content is not treated as application instruction authority merely because it is retrieved.
- Only source-backed selected children are physically materialized.
