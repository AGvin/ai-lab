# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Teach Governance, Ethics, and Society as the organizational and socio-technical layer for responsibility, policy, accountability, transparency, standards, and broader impacts of AI use.
- Keep concrete laws, standards, organization-specific policies, and compliance obligations with their current authoritative sources while teaching how such controls affect practice.
- This source-backed package materializes `accountability-and-transparency/`; other selected governance/ethics siblings remain logical until substantive content justifies physical creation.
- Prefer inspectable evidence, decision records, approvals, provenance, and explicit responsibility over treating model-generated rationale as proof of internal causation.

## Validation

- Governance learning does not invent normative legal requirements.
- Accountability is supported by externally inspectable artifacts where material.
- Current navigation exposes only materialized selected children.
