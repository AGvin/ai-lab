# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Evaluation and Research as the learning area for defining claims, measuring model/system behavior, designing evaluation, choosing judgment methods, benchmarking, experimentation, and research practice.
- Keep reusable canonical evaluation semantics with `concepts/evaluation-and-measurement/`; this area teaches how to apply them rigorously and interpret evidence without turning learning pages into benchmark-result records.
- Explain that the current materialized subset focuses on `evaluation-foundations/` because legacy neural-network and evaluation sources contain source-backed validity, reliability, calibration, and interpretation guidance ready for migration.
- Do not imply that unmaterialized selected groups for evaluation design/methods, model/system evaluation, benchmarking, experimentation, or research practice are absent from the logical architecture; standard navigation reflects only physical children.
- Begin evaluation from the claim or decision that evidence must support. Metrics, scores, datasets, confidence values, benchmarks, and judge outputs are meaningful only relative to an explicit target behavior, population/workload, acceptance criterion, and uncertainty boundary.
- Distinguish evaluation methodology from concrete evidence. Benchmark runs, measured results, dated comparisons, traces, and experiment outputs remain with evidence/project owners.
- Treat model/system/provider behavior as potentially mutable. Current comparative claims require date/version/configuration-bounded evidence rather than timeless learning assertions.

## Validation

- Evaluation learning explains how to establish evidence, not the concrete current result of one benchmark or experiment.
- Current navigation exposes only materialized selected children.
- Metrics and confidence scores are not treated as conclusions without validity/reliability/calibration context.
- Mutable result claims remain evidence-owned.
