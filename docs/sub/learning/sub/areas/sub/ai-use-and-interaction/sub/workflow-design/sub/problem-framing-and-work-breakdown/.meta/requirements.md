# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Teach Problem Framing and Work Breakdown as decomposing difficult AI-assisted work into independently understandable and checkable subproblems when that improves correctness, diagnosis, ownership, or review.
- Make material assumptions, calculations, evidence needs, intermediate outputs, and final acceptance criteria explicit enough to verify without requiring unrestricted internal reasoning prose.
- Prefer deterministic or externally verifiable tools for arithmetic, code execution, retrieval, validation, or other operations where tool evidence is stronger than additional free-form reasoning text.
- Preserve structured plans, state, evidence, calculations, approvals, or other externally inspectable artifacts when later review/audit is required; route accountability/privacy depth to Trustworthy AI.
- Keep formal task-decomposition algorithms with Reasoning and agent-specific decomposition with Agents and Automation.

## Validation

- Decomposition produces useful verification seams rather than ornamental extra steps.
- Material intermediate claims remain evidence-checkable.
- The workflow does not require unrestricted reasoning traces as a condition of auditability.
