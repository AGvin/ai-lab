# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Knowledge Representation as learning about explicit structured representations of entities, facts, relationships, qualifiers, schemas, and reasoning-relevant knowledge used by retrieval or decision systems.
- Materialize `knowledge-graphs/` because it has independent source-backed legacy material; leave other selected ontology/structured-knowledge/graph-reasoning children logical until they have standalone content.
- Keep generic conceptual semantics with canonical concepts and concrete graph/database products with Catalog/Evidence owners.
- Preserve distinctions among asserted, imported, extracted, inferred, and generated information when they affect trust, validation, or lifecycle decisions.
- Teach representation choices around the actual queries/decisions they must support rather than maximizing structural complexity.

## Validation

- Structured knowledge is not assumed to require a graph for every workload.
- Source/status/provenance distinctions remain visible when material.
- Only source-backed selected children are physically materialized.
