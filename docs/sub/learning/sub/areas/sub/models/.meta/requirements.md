# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present Models as the learning area for model anatomy and architectures, modalities and tasks, inference and generation, adaptation and post-training, training and pretraining, distributed training, and model-level optimization/efficiency.
- Keep general machine-learning paradigms/theory with the Machine Learning learning area and broader deployment/application/infrastructure lifecycle with AI Engineering; link those areas rather than duplicating their ownership.
- Keep identifiable model/version/artifact facts, concrete runtime support, mutable compatibility, and formal specification contracts with their catalog/specification/evidence owners.
- Explain that the current materialized subset includes `architectures-and-representations/`, `inference-and-generation/`, `adaptation-and-post-training/`, `training-and-pretraining/`, and `optimization-and-efficiency/` where source-backed migration material is available.
- Do not imply that unmaterialized selected Models branches are absent from the logical learning architecture; standard navigation reflects only physical children.
- Apply progressive depth from model-runtime orientation through mechanics, practical use, design/configuration choices, trade-offs, failure modes, evaluation/debugging, and advanced/current material where relevant.

## Validation

- The area remains pedagogical rather than a duplicate model catalog or concept ontology.
- Current navigation exposes only materialized children.
- Formal format definitions and mutable model/runtime compatibility are not promoted into timeless learning truth.
