# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Teach Audio and Music Generation as producing non-TTS audio such as sound effects, ambience, music, continuation, generative reconstruction, and augmentation material while keeping speech-specific tasks separate.
- Use prototype sound design, music ideation, backing tracks, character-like synthetic audio, continuation, reconstruction, and dataset augmentation as workflow examples rather than universal capabilities.
- Before publication, evaluate the properties that matter to the workflow: long-form structure, rhythm, lyrics or speech intelligibility where present, speaker/instrument identity, artifacts, loudness, clipping, and mastering may require separate review or post-processing.
- Do not assume generated audio is automatically free of rights, attribution, provenance, or consent obligations; verify the concrete model/data/license and intended commercial or publication context.
- Obtain appropriate permission before using identifiable or cloned voices and keep those voice-specific governance concerns linked to TTS/voice-use guidance.
- Keep current model capabilities, concrete prompt/control syntax, licensing terms, and product behavior source-backed outside timeless task learning.

## Validation

- Plausible sound is not treated as sufficient publication-quality evidence.
- Rights/consent/provenance checks remain explicit for generated material.
- General audio generation is not conflated with text-to-speech.
