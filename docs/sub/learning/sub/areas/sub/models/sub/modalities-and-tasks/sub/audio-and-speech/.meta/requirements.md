# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Teach Audio and Speech as the modality/task group for speech recognition, speech synthesis, and broader generative audio workflows, separate from concrete architecture families.
- Keep reusable audio representations, model architectures, training methods, and runtime mechanics with their general owners and link them as prerequisites.
- Treat source recordings, transcripts, synthetic speech, and generated audio as distinct artifacts with their own verification, retention, consent, rights, and provenance considerations.
- Teach task quality through properties such as word/name/number accuracy, pronunciation, intelligibility, speaker or instrument identity, timing, artifacts, long-form structure, loudness, and clipping where relevant.
- Keep concrete recognizer/synthesizer controls, current runtime support, licensing terms, provider behavior, and product-specific syntax source-backed outside timeless learning truth.

## Validation

- Natural-sounding output is not treated as proof of semantic correctness.
- Automatically generated transcripts are not assumed verbatim ground truth.
- Voice identity and generated-audio publication workflows include explicit consent/rights boundaries where applicable.
