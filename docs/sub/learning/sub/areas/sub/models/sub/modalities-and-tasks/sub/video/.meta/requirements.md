# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Teach Video as the modality/task group for temporally ordered visual understanding and generation, separate from architecture-family mechanics.
- This source-backed package materializes `video-generation/`; `video-understanding/` remains selected logically until substantive content justifies physical creation.
- Explain that video quality includes temporal consistency in addition to per-frame visual quality: identity/object persistence, geometry, motion, lighting, text, continuity, transitions, and camera behavior can fail across time even when individual frames look plausible.
- Keep concrete model duration limits, frame rates, conditioning controls, runtime support, licensing, and benchmark evidence source-backed outside timeless task learning.
