# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present `learning/` as AI Lab's pedagogical ownership root: it teaches subjects through progressive explanation, examples, practice, evaluation, and deeper study while linking canonical concept, catalog, specification, and evidence owners rather than replacing them.
- Present the currently materialized `areas/` child as the subject-oriented canonical learning corpus selected by the AI Lab learning architecture.
- Do not imply that reader role, profession, expertise level, `Essential`/`Useful`/`Specialized`, or certification is a separate canonical content tree.
- Keep learning paths intentionally deferred. Do not materialize or describe a current `paths/`, `curriculum/`, role, level, or certification hierarchy until separately selected by the owner.
- Explain that a learning subject can be one page or a deeper subtree when independent learning outcomes and substantial content justify decomposition.
- Keep the landing page concise and render standard direct-child navigation only from materialized children when reader-facing rendering is activated.

## Validation

- `learning/` is clearly pedagogical and does not become a second canonical owner for mutable product facts, formal specifications, benchmark results, or project policy.
- Only materialized direct children are presented as implemented navigation.
- Deferred learning paths are not invented from the selected area structure.
