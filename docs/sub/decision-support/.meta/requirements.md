
# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Own derived reader-oriented comparisons, selections, recommendations, scenarios, portfolios, and bounded trade-off material whose primary purpose is helping a reader make a decision.
- Keep canonical entity facts in `catalog/`, reusable abstract subjects in `concepts/`, pedagogy in `learning/`, and source evidence/results with their selected evidence owner.
- Group known decision targets under `selection/`; keep combined-context journeys under sibling `scenarios/`.
- Link canonical facts and evidence instead of copying them into a second factual source.
- Keep applicability, assumptions, uncertainty, verification conditions, and re-evaluation triggers explicit when mutable facts materially affect a decision.

## Validation

- `selection/` and `scenarios/` remain semantically distinct starting journeys.
- Decision-support pages do not become alternate canonical entity profiles or evidence records.
- Empty child trees are not materialized for symmetry.
