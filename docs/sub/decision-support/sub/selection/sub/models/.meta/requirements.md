
# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Own model-specific selection through the materialized `decision-guides/` and `hardware/` journeys.
- Use `decision-guides/` when the reader already knows the task, need, material model constraint, or portfolio decision.
- Use `hardware/` when hardware is already owned/fixed and the primary question is which models are practically viable on it; do not turn this into hardware purchasing guidance.
- Route combined personal, professional, team, or organization situations to sibling `decision-support/scenarios/` rather than duplicating scenarios under model selection.
- Define assignment, acceptance criteria, constraints, exact candidate identity, evidence basis, material runtime/deployment assumptions, trade-offs, and re-evaluation triggers for concrete recommendations.
- Link canonical model facts from `catalog/models/` and preserve provider/evidence boundaries.
- Prefer the least complex and least costly plausible route that satisfies the defined acceptance contract; escalate only when evidence or another material constraint justifies it.

## Validation

- `decision-guides/` and `hardware/` answer distinct starting questions.
- No user-scenario child is materialized under model selection.
- Canonical model/hardware/software/service facts are linked rather than duplicated.
