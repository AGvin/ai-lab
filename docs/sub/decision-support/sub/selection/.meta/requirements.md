
# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Group decision support where the class of thing being selected is already known.
- Keep currently materialized model-selection content under `models/`.
- Treat `software/` as a logically selected future decision target, but do not materialize it or infer deeper software-selection children until its researched taxonomy is owner-approved.
- Do not add services, hardware, solutions, or another target merely for symmetry.

## Validation

- Every physical selection target has substantive content or navigation value.
- Selection pages link canonical entity owners and evidence rather than duplicating them.
