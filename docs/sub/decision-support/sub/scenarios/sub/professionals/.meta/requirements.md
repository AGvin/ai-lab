# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present `professionals/` for one professional choosing a model route for their own work where role-specific tasks, confidentiality, client or employer data boundaries, tools, hardware, review obligations, or delivery quality materially change the decision.
- Distinguish this group from personal use when professional policy, approved services, client separation, auditability, qualified review, or work-product risk introduces materially different constraints.
- Distinguish this group from team scenarios when the decision is primarily owned and operated by one professional rather than a shared multi-person workflow with common budgets, datasets, repositories, or review responsibilities.
- Evaluate local, hosted, API, self-hosted, and hybrid routes according to the professional's actual environment and data class; do not assume that professional work requires enterprise infrastructure or that local processing is sufficient merely because the model runs locally.
- Treat professional hardware such as business laptops, local GPUs, and Apple unified-memory systems as measured constraints; hardware acquisition and deployment architecture remain outside this group unless they are frozen evidence conditions for model selection.
- Navigate only materialized direct-child scenarios and keep detailed model routes, task evidence, trade-offs, review requirements, and escalation triggers with the applicable scenario child.

## Validation

- Every materialized child represents one professional's working context rather than a household, shared team, or organization-wide platform.
- Confidentiality and provider-chain requirements are not weakened merely because a route is convenient or marketed for business use.
- The page does not duplicate scenario recommendations or create empty children for approved but unauthored professional routes.
