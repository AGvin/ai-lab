
# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Start from a recognizable combined situation rather than from a preselected entity class.
- Group scenarios by `personal/`, `professionals/`, `teams/`, and `organizations/` decision scale.
- Let material tasks, existing hardware, budget, skills, privacy/data boundaries, services, deployment preferences, operations, and failure severity jointly shape the recommended route.
- A scenario may coordinate models, software, services, hardware constraints, deployment choices, or operating policies when those are material to the decision.
- Keep canonical model/software/service/hardware facts with their catalog owners and link them instead of copying complete profiles.
- Keep mutable recommendation inputs source-backed and record verification/re-evaluation boundaries when availability, pricing, runtime support, limits, or provider terms matter.
- Do not create empty scenario leaves merely because they exist in the logical map.

## Validation

- Scenario ancestry reflects audience/decision scale rather than a single constraint such as local/cloud, budget, privacy, or VRAM.
- Scenario pages do not become duplicate catalog profiles, benchmark records, or complete solution-architecture monoliths.
- Existing scenario leaves keep path-derived IDs under `decision-support/scenarios/...`.
