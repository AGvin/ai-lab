# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present this scenario for one professional using AI for writing, summaries, planning, research, files, and other general knowledge work where workplace data handling and approved-tool policy materially distinguish the route from personal use.
- Preserve an organization-approved business or enterprise assistant/workspace as the default low-administration work route when the employer permits hosted processing; recheck current product terms, limits, connectors, retention, and administrator controls when material.
- State that consumer accounts may be unsuitable for internal, confidential, client, or regulated work data even when the same provider offers an approved business product.
- Preserve compact private text routes with Phi-4 Mini Instruct or Qwen3 8B and bounded private multimodal routes with Gemma 4 E2B/E4B Instruct when local processing is justified by the data boundary or offline need.
- Treat local model memory ranges and capability as measured conditions rather than guarantees; local setup, updates, latency, and lower quality can outweigh privacy/provider-independence benefits.
- Keep organization-wide policy, workspace administration, identity, procurement, compliance, and platform governance outside this individual-professional scenario; link those owners or organization scenarios when they determine the route.
- Require the complete client/provider/subprocessor path to satisfy the data boundary; a local model endpoint or desktop client does not prove the complete professional workflow is local.
- Compare subscription, API, and local/self-hosted options by accepted-result value including correction and administration time rather than headline price alone.
- Escalate when confidentiality, policy, collaboration, connector/permission needs, task quality, or accepted-result cost exceeds what the simple individual route can support.
- Link assistants and model candidates to their canonical service/model owners rather than duplicating complete profiles.

## Validation

- The scenario owns the workplace half of the former combined `home or office` scenario; personal/home guidance remains in `personal/everyday-home-user/`.
- Consumer access is not treated as automatically acceptable for work data.
- Local processing is not treated as sufficient compliance evidence by itself.
- Organization-wide platform/governance design is not duplicated here.
