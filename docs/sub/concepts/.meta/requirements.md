# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Present `concepts/` as the canonical entry point for reusable abstract AI knowledge.
- Explain that concepts own stable definitions, scope, important properties and boundaries, relationships to neighboring abstract subjects, and authoritative source context.
- Keep concrete ecosystem entities and mutable entity facts under `catalog/`; keep formal normative artifacts under `catalog/specifications/`; keep pedagogy and progressive learning journeys under `learning/`; keep AI Lab project, policy, and navigation material under `project/`.
- Make clear that `concepts/` is not a fallback for evidence records, recommendations, concrete workflows, or unresolved material whose primary owner is elsewhere.
- Render the standard direct-child navigation from the validated materialized child set when reader-facing rendering is activated.
- Keep the landing content concise and descriptive; do not duplicate the complete descendant taxonomy.

## Validation

- The page distinguishes abstract concept ownership from catalog, specification, learning, and project ownership without creating parallel sources of truth.
- The page contains no temporary-placeholder language that implies the selected logical taxonomy is already fully materialized.
- Direct-child navigation contains only currently materialized direct children and does not imply completeness for unmaterialized selected nodes.
- The page does not absorb time-specific evidence, recommendations, or concrete entity facts.
