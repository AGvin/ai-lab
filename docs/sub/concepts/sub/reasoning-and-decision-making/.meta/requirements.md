# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Use the reader-facing title `Reasoning and Decision-Making`.
- Present this domain as canonical reusable knowledge about representing problems and knowledge, deriving conclusions, searching solution spaces, reasoning under uncertainty, satisfying constraints, planning/scheduling, and choosing actions or decisions.
- Distinguish general reasoning/decision concepts from observed reasoning-like behavior of generative models, which remains under `models/behavior-and-failure-modes/reasoning/`, and from concrete agent/workflow implementations.
- Keep knowledge representation, symbolic/automated reasoning, uncertainty, causal reasoning, search/optimization, constraint satisfaction, planning/scheduling, and decision theory as distinct selected concerns rather than reducing the domain to LLM reasoning.
- Explain that methods can be symbolic, probabilistic, optimization-based, search-based, learned, hybrid, or otherwise formally/empirically defined; no one representation or model family is universal.
- Render the standard direct-child navigation from the validated materialized child set when reader-facing rendering is activated.
- Keep concrete solver products, model benchmark results, agent recipes, domain-specific policies, and decision recommendations with their applicable catalog, evaluation, engineering, learning, or project owners.

## Validation

- The page does not equate reasoning and decision-making with chain-of-thought, LLM agents, or one algorithmic paradigm.
- General planning/decision concepts remain distinct from model-behavior and workflow owners.
- Direct-child navigation contains only currently materialized selected descendants.
- Concrete evidence, recommendations, and implementation products are not duplicated into this abstract domain.
