# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Use the reader-facing title `Evaluation and Measurement`.
- Present this domain as canonical reusable knowledge about defining evaluation questions, conditions, datasets/tasks, metrics, procedures, evidence, uncertainty, and interpretation for AI models and systems.
- Distinguish evaluation concepts and methods from concrete evaluation result records, benchmark snapshots, model facts, rankings, recommendations, and project acceptance decisions; those outputs remain with their applicable evidence, catalog, or decision owners.
- Explain that evaluation is scoped: model evaluation, retrieval evaluation, agent evaluation, human evaluation, safety/robustness evaluation, and other selected descendants can require different units of analysis, measurements, and evidence.
- Treat measurement as operationalizing a property through defined observations or metrics rather than assuming broad labels such as `quality`, `capability`, `safety`, or `reliability` are self-defining single numbers.
- Explain that multiple metrics and test conditions may be necessary when important properties trade off or when one aggregate score would hide material failures, subgroup differences, or operating constraints.
- Distinguish benchmark design from benchmark results and evaluation method from the particular model/system being measured.
- Render the standard direct-child navigation from the validated materialized child set when reader-facing rendering is activated.
- Keep mutable scores, leaderboard positions, provider claims, concrete model comparisons, and task-specific selection recommendations with their applicable evidence, catalog, or decision owners.

## Validation

- The page does not equate evaluation with benchmarking alone or with one scalar score.
- Concepts/methods are distinguished from concrete evaluation evidence and recommendation outcomes.
- The evaluated unit and operating conditions are not treated as interchangeable across model, retrieval, agent, and full-system evaluations.
- Direct-child navigation contains only currently materialized direct children.
