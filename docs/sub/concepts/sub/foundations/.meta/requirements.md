# Documentation Requirements

## Requirements

- Use the reader-facing title `Foundations`.
- Present this domain as the entry point for the broad conceptual foundation of artificial intelligence rather than as a duplicate taxonomy of every AI method or implementation.
- Explain the scope needed to orient readers from the general idea of artificial intelligence toward more specific canonical domains such as machine learning, models, reasoning, retrieval, agents, and embodied systems without re-owning those subjects.
- Render the standard direct-child navigation from the validated materialized child set when reader-facing rendering is activated.
- Keep the overview concise and durable; do not embed current products, model rankings, implementation recipes, or a pedagogical reading sequence.

## Validation

- The page does not use `Foundations` as a catch-all for concepts that already have another selected canonical owner.
- Direct-child navigation contains only currently materialized direct children and does not imply that every selected concept node is already implemented.
- The page does not duplicate concrete catalog facts, learning-order metadata, or mutable ecosystem information.
