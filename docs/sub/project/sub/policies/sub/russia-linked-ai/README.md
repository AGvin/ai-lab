# Russia-linked AI: dangerous and not recommended

## Binding AI Lab classification

**DANGEROUS — NOT RECOMMENDED.** Any AI-related resource with verified Russian origin, Russian producer/operator jurisdiction, Russian ownership or control, or Russian upstream model/code/artifact provenance is **categorically classified as dangerous and not recommended for adoption** by AI Lab. This is an owner-defined security and procurement exclusion, not an evidence-based claim that malware was found in every individual resource.

The classification is **unconditional** once that provenance or control is established. A passed security audit, clean malware scan, open source, permissive license, favorable benchmark, third-party mirror, absence of a specific sanction, or successful sandbox test **does not remove or downgrade it**. Only correction of an incorrectly attributed origin/control fact can change whether this rule applies.

## Scope and exclusions

Apply across **producers, models and checkpoints, hosted services/APIs, software and dependencies, datasets, hardware, agent skills, and other catalog resources**, including re-hosted or redistributed Russian-origin artifacts. If a product includes a Russian-origin upstream component, explicitly disclose that provenance and apply the exclusion to adoption of the affected product or dependency chain.

Determine provenance from documented entity ownership, development/publishing organization, registered jurisdiction and upstream artifact lineage. **A person's name, ethnicity, birthplace, language or citizenship alone is not sufficient proof of control or software provenance.** Do not misclassify an independently owned organization because of a founder's national background.

## Documentation and recommendation behavior

- Put **DANGEROUS — NOT RECOMMENDED | RUSSIAN ORIGIN/CONTROL** immediately below the title of each affected reader page.
- State the Russian producer/control evidence and carry the exclusion in canonical entity information and local documentation requirements. Where page templates generate readers, maintain the rule through canonical owners rather than an unsupported manual output-only patch.
- **Never recommend or shortlist** an affected entity for production, personal, commercial, education, government, defense, or sensitive use. Security testing does not make it recommendable under this policy.
- **Retain discoverable factual records** to track technical progress, compare public claims, document limitations, and monitor threats. Research visibility is not endorsement, acceptance for deployment, or a safety guarantee.
- Maintain provenance and sanctions as separate factual fields/evidence. **Do not claim every Russian-origin artifact is legally sanctioned or contains proven malicious code** without specific evidence.

## Research-only handling

For strictly necessary examination, use a disposable isolated environment with network egress disabled or tightly restricted, no secrets/credentials, no personal or confidential data, reviewed/pinned model code and dependencies, controlled artifact acquisition, and documented legal/sanctions checks. Avoid Russian-operated endpoints entirely for private, operational, or sensitive inputs. These controls **do not change the dangerous/not-recommended classification**; they merely limit research exposure.

## Source and context

The Yandex producer, for example, confirms its Russian head office in its own [official corporate contacts](https://yandex.com/company/contacts/moscow). The organization/location example establishes applicability, not a universal proof about other companies. Each further entity needs its own provenance evidence. This AI Lab policy is stricter than a product-specific technical audit or the minimum requirements of an individual jurisdiction.
