
# Decision Support Landing Page

## Description

Entry page for AI Lab decision-oriented material.

## Purpose

Route readers either to selection by a known decision target or to a combined-context scenario.

## Use When

Use for `decision-support/`.

## Owns

- concise distinction between `selection/` and `scenarios/`;
- decision-support ownership/evidence boundary;
- navigation to materialized decision journeys.

## Does Not Own

- canonical entity facts;
- benchmark or experiment records;
- abstract concepts or pedagogy.
